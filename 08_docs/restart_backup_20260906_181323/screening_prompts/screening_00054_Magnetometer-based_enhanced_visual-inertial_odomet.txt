TITLE: Magnetometer-based enhanced visual-inertial odometry for reliable long-range navigation in GNSS-denied high-altitude environment
AUTHORS: Abdollahi M.R.; Pourtakdoust S.H.
YEAR: 2026.0
ABSTRACT: A new visual-inertial odometry (VIO) algorithm for fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) operating in GNSS-denied, low to high altitude (50–800 m) environments is proposed. The method builds upon the Fast Multi-State Constraint Kalman Filter (FMSCKF) framework, introducing key innovations such as a dynamic feature extraction strategy, specialized handling of data for slow-motion imagery, selective feature utilization during filter updates, and magnetometer integration for improved heading estimation. Emphasis is placed on practical implementation and rigorous flight-testing on manned and unmanned multi-rotor vehicles. The proposed system is validated on a custom dataset obtained from a 15 km long-range flight via a conventional fixed-wing UAV in a desert-like environment. Experimental comparisons of results with standard Multi-State Constraint Kalman Filter (MSCKF), a semi-direct visual odometry approach with smoothing (SVO+GTSAM), and the VINS-Mono algorithm demonstrate the efficacy of the proposed system in terms of long-duration navigation, accuracy, and convergence while the other methods diverged. In addition, quantitative evaluation of the proposed integrated system using relative and absolute trajectory error (RTE and ATE) metrics confirms its robustness and accuracy under challenging conditions. © 2026 Elsevier Masson SAS.
