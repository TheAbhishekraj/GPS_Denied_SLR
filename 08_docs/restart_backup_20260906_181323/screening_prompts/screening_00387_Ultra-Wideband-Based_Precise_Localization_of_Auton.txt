TITLE: Ultra-Wideband-Based Precise Localization of Autonomous Vehicle in GPS-Denied Environments
AUTHORS: Lee S.-M.
YEAR: 2021.0
ABSTRACT: In this paper, we propose a multi-tag ultra-wideband (UWB)-based localization method for autonomous vehicles. UWB range sensors can provide precise distance measurements using low power, so it can be used for precise localization. It can also be used in global positioning system (GPS)-denied environments such as indoor parking lots. The proposed multi-tag UWB localization method minimizes the number of UWB anchors installed over the space and the vehicle is equipped with multiple UWB tags to enhance the observability of localization. The performance of the proposed method is verified through practical experiments with a real vehicle. © 2021, The Author(s), under exclusive license to Springer Nature Singapore Pte Ltd.
