TITLE: Map-aided visual SLAM factor graph optimization algorithm for vehicle positioning in GNSS-denied areas
AUTHORS: Liu Y.; Kuang Y.; Liu C.; Zhang X.
YEAR: 2026.0
ABSTRACT: Continuous accurate positioning in global navigation satellite systems (GNSS)-denied environments is extremely important for vehicle navigation, and many existing methods in this domain are founded on recursive localization principles (including inertial navigation systems (INS), dead reckoning (DR) and simultaneous localization and mapping (SLAM), etc.), and thus suffer from cumulative error dispersion over time. To address the challenge of improving localization accuracy during GNSS failures in urban environments, this paper presents a novel real-time visual SLAM factor graph optimization (FGO) algorithm that utilizes High-definition (HD) map, capable of continuously mitigating localization errors without relying on loop closure. The proposed algorithm adopts visual lane identification to recognize road lane and the estimation of vehicle normal offset and heading angle, and to solve the cumulative error problem that exists in SLAM for a long period of time, a real-time joint nonlinear optimization model for eliminating the SLAM error is constructed by taking advantage of the observability of the road normal error and the heading angle error, the precedent and the current position constraints constructed by the inertial measurement unit (IMU), and the covariance constraints of the visual features, and finally realizes the cumulative error by using map-matching. Experiments are conducted on public datasets in challenging urban environments as well as real-world scenarios to evaluate the performance of our algorithm. The experimental results show that the proposed algorithm can effectively eliminate accumulated positioning errors and can maintain a stable and reliable positioning performance with a normal positioning accuracy of better than 0.65 m for a cumulative period of 16 min in GNSS-denied areas. © 2025 Elsevier Ltd
