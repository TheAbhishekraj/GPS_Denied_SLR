TITLE: Towards an UAV Visual Navigation System Based on Map Processing and Spatial Matching Techniques: A Literature Review
AUTHORS: Ramanauskas E.; Jodłowiec M.; Krótkiewicz M.
YEAR: 2025.0
ABSTRACT: This paper reviews state-of-the-art methods for UAV navigation in GNSS-denied environments, focusing on map-based spatial matching and vision-based techniques. It identifies trends and challenges in current approaches, emphasizing the integration of cartographic data, visual odometry, and data fusion. A novel methodology for identifying research gaps is presented, combining systematic literature review, qualitative and quantitative analysis with multi-criteria evaluation. The findings highlight limitations in existing methods and propose a navigation system that leverages spatial pattern matching and object recognition, integrating vision-based and GIS data. This approach addresses real-time processing challenges and offers a foundation for improving UAV navigation accuracy in GNSS-denied settings. © The Author(s), under exclusive license to Springer Nature Singapore Pte Ltd. 2025.
