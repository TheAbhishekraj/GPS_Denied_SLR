TITLE: Deployable, Data-Driven Unmanned Vehicle Navigation System in GPS-Denied, Feature-Deficient Environments
AUTHORS: Misra S.; Sundar K.; Sharma R.; Brink K.
YEAR: 2022.0
ABSTRACT: This paper presents a novel data-driven navigation system to navigate an Unmanned Vehicle (UV) in GPS-denied, feature-deficient environments such as tunnels, or mines. The method utilizes landmarks that vehicle can deploy and measure range from to enable localization as the vehicle traverses its pre-defined path through the tunnel. A key question that arises in such scenario is determine apriori, i.e., before the start of the mission, the number of landmarks the vehicle has to carry, given some information about the environment. The main objective is to determine this number so as to keep the maximum position uncertainty less than or close to a desired value. In this article, we develop a novel vehicle navigation system in GPS-denied, feature-deficient environment by combining techniques from estimation, machine learning, and mixed-integer convex optimization. This article develops a novel, systematic method to perform localization and navigate the UV through the environment with minimum number of landmarks while maintaining desired localization accuracy. We also present extensive simulation experiments on different scenarios that corroborate the effectiveness of the proposed navigation system. © 2022, The Author(s), under exclusive licence to Springer Nature B.V.
