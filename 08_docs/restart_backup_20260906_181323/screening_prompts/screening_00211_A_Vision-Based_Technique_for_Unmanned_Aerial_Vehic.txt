TITLE: A Vision-Based Technique for Unmanned Aerial Vehicles Localization
AUTHORS: Chen L.-Y.; Lin H.-Y.; Munir S.
YEAR: 2025.0
ABSTRACT: Accurate localization is a fundamental requirement for the safe and reliable operation of unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs). Conventional localization approaches primarily rely on Global Navigation Satellite System (GNSS) or Global Positioning System (GPS) signals for outdoor navigation. However, these methods are susceptible to performance degradation or complete failure in GNSS-denied environments. This paper proposes a novel UAV localization framework based on aerial-satellite image matching to enable navigation without GNSS signals. The proposed end-to-end network employs the CLIP model to extract and correlate geospatial features between UAV-acquired aerial images and pre-existing satellite imagery. Furthermore, image orientation information is incorporated into the feature similarity computation to estimate flight heading direction. To evaluate the effectiveness of the proposed approach, an aerial image dataset was collected from UAV flights conducted at an altitude of 100 meters above sea level. Experimental results over a 2.23 km2 area demonstrate the feasibility of the proposed GNSS-denied localization method, achieving average position and heading errors of 39.2 m and 15.9°, respectively. © 2025 IEEE.
