TITLE: Experimental Validation of Autonomous Search for an RF Emitter in an Unknown Indoor Environment
AUTHORS: Ly K.H.; Kim D.Y.; Ristic B.; Asadi E.; Al-Hourani A.
YEAR: 2026.0
ABSTRACT: This paper presents a practical demonstrator and experimental verification of an autonomous search robotic system for the localization of a radio frequency (RF) emitter in an unknown, GNSS-denied indoor environment. The system combines: (1) RSS-based inference for emitter localization - without prior knowledge of the environment or emitter amplitude, with (2) LiDAR-based simultaneous localization and mapping (SLAM) for mapping and self-localization. An online information-theoretic path planner selects actions to maximize entropy reduction in the emitter parameter space. Real-world challenges such as signal variability and sensor noise are addressed by adopting an interval valued path loss exponent. Experimental results demonstrate practical emitter-localization performance and fully autonomous operation in realistic settings. © 2026 IEEE.
