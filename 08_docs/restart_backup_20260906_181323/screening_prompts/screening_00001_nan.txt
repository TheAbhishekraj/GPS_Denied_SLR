AUTHORS: H. Pan; H. Ye; W. Zheng; L. Zhou
YEAR: nan
ABSTRACT: This paper proposes a BIM-assisted UAV topological localization and navigation framework for GPS-denied indoor environments. The method transforms BIM data into a semantic-topological graph containing rooms, corridors, staircases, and connectivity relationships. UAV image observations are converted into candidate BIM nodes through spatial semantic recognition and OCR-based textual matching rather than directly estimating metric coordinates. A topology-aware recursive localization mechanism is then introduced by integrating BIM adjacency constraints and historical states to infer the most probable indoor location. Furthermore, semantic-cost A* planning is employed to validate whether the estimated topological nodes can support indoor navigation tasks. Experiments were conducted in a real university teaching-building environment with 721 indoor images and 74 navigation frames. Results demonstrate that the proposed framework achieves 95.95% localization coverage and 100% valid-frame node accuracy, showing strong robustness and interpretability for indoor UAV localization and navigation.
