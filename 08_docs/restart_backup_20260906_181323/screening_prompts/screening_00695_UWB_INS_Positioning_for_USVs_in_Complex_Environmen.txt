TITLE: UWB/INS Positioning for USVs in Complex Environments: A Dual-LSTM Enhanced UKF Framework with Error Compensation
AUTHORS: Yan X.; Yang X.; Xiang Z.
YEAR: 2026.0
ABSTRACT: To address the challenges of significant positioning errors and instability in Unmanned Surface Vehicles (USVs) caused by environmental interference affecting Ultra-Wideband (UWB) and low-cost Inertial Navigation Systems (INS) in Global Navigation Satellite System (GNSS)-denied environments, such as ports and under-bridge areas, this paper proposes a Dual-Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) Enhanced Unscented Kalman Filter (DLE-UKF) framework with error compensation. First, a wind wave intensity quantization model is constructed, utilizing the LSTM-based network (netI) to predict accelerometer trust weights and dynamically mitigate integration drift induced by environmental perturbations. Second, a netII prediction network is designed, which innovatively incorporates Range Power Sum (RPS) as a geometric feature, combined with thrust sequences, to accurately estimate UWB positioning biases and velocities. Finally, a weight adjustment mechanism is introduced into the UKF to automatically balance network predictions and filter estimates based on RPS fluctuations. The proposed method demonstrates superior robustness across diverse scenarios. Specifically, it achieves reductions of up to 58.3% in positioning Root Mean Square Error (RMSE) and 78.3% in velocity RMSE relative to the evaluated baselines (UWB, UKF, netI+Extended Kalman Filter (EKF) (nE), netI+UKF (nU), LSTM and netI+EKF+netII (nEn)), maintaining high localization accuracy under both cruising and drifting scenarios. © 2002-2012 IEEE.
