TITLE: A DNN-Based Optical Aided Autonomous Navigation System for UAV Under GNSS-denied Environment
AUTHORS: Zhang Q.; Zhang H.; Lan Z.; Chen W.; Zhang Z.
YEAR: 2023.0
ABSTRACT: In this paper, a novel UAV autonomous navigation system is proposed, which uses DNN to extract scene features for position matching and optical flow estimation. This method can correct position drift of IMU under GNSS-denied environment. A realistic simulation test platform is designed to validate the fusion navigation system, and we deploy the system on an airborne embedded GPU hardware. Optimization strategies for hardware are designed to significantly improve operational performance, which make it become more practical in engineering. Field experimental results further verify its effectiveness of proposed system. Compared with traditional methods, it is more robust and has higher positioning accuracy. © 2023, Beijing HIWING Sci. and Tech. Info Inst.
