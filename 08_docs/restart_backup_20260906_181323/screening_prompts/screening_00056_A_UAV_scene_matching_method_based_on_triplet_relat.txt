TITLE: A UAV scene matching method based on triplet relationship and sliding mode control for GPS-denied environments
AUTHORS: Chen Z.; Zhou J.; Gao D.; Lin J.; Huang G.; Deng Z.
YEAR: 2026.0
ABSTRACT: Scene matching plays an important role for UAV positioning under GPS-denied conditions. However, traditional feature matching methods struggle with scale, viewpoint, and illumination heterogeneity between UAV and satellite images, often relying on fixed thresholds that cannot adaptively suppress outliers. To address these issues, this paper introduces a scene matching method that integrates triplet relationships with a control-theoretic optimization strategy. Specifically, SuperPoint is first used for feature extraction, followed by triplet relationships and a greedy selection strategy based on matching scores and spatial distribution to improve initial model robustness. For the inlier optimization stage, this paper is the first to embed a sliding mode controller in the iterative refinement process, enabling dynamic residual feedback and adaptive outlier suppression. Additionally, to gain an insight of the working mechanism of sliding mode control strategy on feature matching, this paper provides a theoretical mechanism analysis, a Lyapunov-based convergence proof, and a parameter sensitivity analysis to clarify the physical interpretability and tunability of the SMC(sliding mode control) module. Extensive experiments on the University-1652 dataset, DenseUAV dataset, and a self-collected UAV dataset demonstrate that the proposed method significantly improves spatial representativeness, outlier removal capability, and matching stability. Compared to the RANSAC and TRESAC series of methods, the proposed method achieves an average improvement of approximately 4.51%–9.31% in F-score, reaching up to 96.24% in some complex scenarios, while maintaining relatively stable computational efficiency. The results verify the high matching accuracy and robustness of the proposed method under GPS-denied environments. © 2026 Elsevier Ltd
