Abstract—This paper presents a navigation system that enables 
quadrotor to navigate autonomously within GPS-denied 
below-canopy environment without GPS. The navigation system 
consists of the function of Simultaneous Localization and 
Mapping(SLAM) and path planning. The online real-time 
locating 
estimation 
is 
achieved 
by

odometer in the front end, the graph-based optimization is used 
as an optimization mechanism to map in the back end. The path 
planning finds an obstacle-free trajectory based on the 
waypoints generated by the improved RRT* algorithm fitted 
with the Bezier curve.

I. INTRODUCTION 
The application of quadrotor in forestry has been more 
extensive for its simple operation[1-2]. Lack of GPS signal 
and complex illumination interferences are big problems 
which have brought difficulties to navigate and control 
quadrotor autonomously within GPS-denied below-canopy 
environment compare to navigating above the canopy. It is the 
key issues for quadrotor to navigate within below-canopy 
environment so that we can build the map and plan the path 
[3]. 
The classic SLAM algorithms face more challenges used in 
complex 
GPS-denied 
below-canopy 
environment[4-5]. 
Traditional V-SLAM based key-frame is far less robust in 
changed scene; Hector SLAM included in the Laser-SLAM 
along with Gmapping requires laser sensor with high 
frequency and low measurement noise which is usually 
expensive and heavy. The unstructured environment of the 
forest area limits the navigation ability of quadrotor[6]. The 
problems of high density leaf occlusion and illumination 
difference have great influence on UAV trajectory planning 
and obstacle avoidance.

monocular vision odometer and laser radar to achieve 
quadrotor’s position and map information. On the basis of

II. SLAM BASED ON FUSION OF MONOCULAR VISION AND 
LASER 
A SLAM algorithm consisted of the front-end and the 
back-end[9]has been proposed based on the fusion of 
monocular vision odometer and laser for sake of navigating 
the quadrotor conveniently. We uses the ICP[10] to fuse the 
data of laser and vision odometer and build the map by 
searching the table and Gauss transformation at the front end 
to increase the locating accuracy; the Graph optimization is 
used to optimize the map to improve the optimization 
efficiency at the back end. 
This chapter introduces the proposed SLAM algorithm in 
detail. The location algorithm based on SVO is proposed to 
solve the problem of weak GPS signal and the main steps 
are:1.Estimation of initial position; 2. Optimization of feature 
point position; 3. Optimization of world posture. The fusion 
algorithm provides the obstacle map for quadrotor flying in 
the GPS-denied below-canopy environment and the main 
steps are: 1.Scan matching; 2.Loop closure; 3.Global 
optimization.

III. PATH PLANNING WITHIN BELOW-CANOPY ENVIRONMENT 
In order to ensure the quadrotor flying safely under the 
canopy, it must have real time path planning and obstacle 
avoidance ability. Because of the low computing performance 
of the airborne computer, the path planning requires higher 
running speed. To solve the above problems, we propose an 
adaptive dynamic step size and extended strategy to increase 
the compute speed based on the classical RRT*[15] algorithm. 
The path planning finds an obstacle-free trajectory based on 
the waypoints generated by the improved RRT* algorithm 
fitted with the Bezier curve.

V. CONCLUSION 
In this paper, the quadrotor navigation system is consisted 
of the SLAM system and path planning system. This paper 
proposed a SLAM algorithm based on fusion of monocular 
vision odometer and laser radar along with an improved RRT* 
algorithm to generate a series of waypoints used to create a 
smooth and obstacle-free trajectory fitted with Bezier curves. 
The experiment results show that the proposed SLAM 
algorithm is more suitable to be used within GPS-denied 
below-canopy environment and the proposed path planning 
algorithm can spend less time to generate a smooth trajectory 
for the quadrotor to autonomous navigate within GPS-denied 
below-canopy environment.
