ABSTRACT: This paper presents a novel indoor navigation and control system for swarm robots in a grid-based warehouse environment. The system uses an ESP32-CAM mounted on the ceiling to detect Aruco markers for localization, combined with MQTT-based communication between a central control unit (a laptop) and the swarm robots. The approach offers realtime command transmission, low power consumption, and efficient navigation with minimal sensor hardware on individual bots. Experimental results show the viability of this setup for scalable warehouse logistics automation.
