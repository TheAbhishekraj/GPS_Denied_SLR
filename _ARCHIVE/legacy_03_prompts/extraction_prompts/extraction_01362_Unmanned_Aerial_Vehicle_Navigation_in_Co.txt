ABSTRACT: The utilization of unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) has rapidly expanded in recent years, with applications ranging from aerial surveillance and infrastructure inspection to precision agriculture, and delivery services. To support such diverse and autonomous operations, the flight control system is responsible for executing autonomous flight. A reliable and stable navigation system is important in providing critical information such as UAV's states including position and velocity required by the flight control system. This is accomplished through the integration of diverse onboard sensors with redundancy and multimodal data fusion. On the other hand, achieving accurate linear/angular position estimation remains particularly challenging in Global Navigation Satellite System (GNSS) and visually degraded environments, such as repetitive and low-texture scenes. To address these challenges, this paper presents a unified literature review of multi-sensor fusion strategies for UAV navigation. It further provides an overview of filter-based and optimization-based fusion frameworks, analyzing their implementation approaches and performances. A dedicated section also explores the challenges associated with visually degraded environments, highlighting the importance of visual-inertial odometry (VIO) in UAV navigation and exploring alternative methods. © 2026 IEEE.
