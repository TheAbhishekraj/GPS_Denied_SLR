TITLE: Application of Robust Filtering Algorithm Based on Mahalanobis Distance in Anti GNSS Deception Attack of Multi-sensor Integrated Navigation on Unmanned Platforms
AUTHORS: P. Jiang; W. Dong; Y. Guo
YEAR: 2026
VENUE: NOT_IN_ABSTRACT
DOI: 10.1109/ICAISISAS68969.2026.11567838
ABSTRACT: The multi-source sensor integrated navigation system combining the Strapdown Inertial Navigation System (SINS), Global Navigation Satellite System (GNSS) and Odometry (OD) is widely applied to positioning and orientation of unmanned vehicles, robots and other systems, serving as the foundation for their mission execution. In recent years, GNSS jamming technologies have advanced rapidly. Low-cost jamming devices can generate GNSS suppression or spoofing interference signals, and GNSS jamming has thus become one of the commonly adopted technologies for countering unmanned systems. Malicious jamming poses a severe threat to the safety and stability of intelligent unmanned systems. When the SINS/GNSS/OD multi-source sensor integrated navigation system is subjected to GNSS spoofing interference, time-varying non-Gaussian errors will be injected, which in turn leads to system instability and erroneous navigation results. To address this issue, this paper conducts relevant research and designs an adaptive information factor allocation method based on the Mahalanobis distance. This method enables real-time adaptive allocation of information factor allocation coefficients according to the accuracy of navigation sensors, thereby effectively suppressing the negative impacts of GNSS spoofing interference. Compared with traditional algorithms, the proposed algorithm can identify and detect GNSS spoofing interference more quickly and accurately, isolate abnormal GNSS observations in a timelier manner, minimize the impact of GNSS spoofing interference on the navigation system, and improve the accuracy and reliability of the integrated navigation system for intelligent unmanned platforms.
