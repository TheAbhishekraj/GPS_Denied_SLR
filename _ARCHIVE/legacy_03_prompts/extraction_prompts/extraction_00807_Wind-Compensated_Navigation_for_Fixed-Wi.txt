ABSTRACT: This paper proposes a wind-compensated navigation algorithm for small fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) in *1SS-denied environments. Leveraging the short-term invariance of wind speed, the algorithm computes the wind triangulation and fuses measurements from multiple low-cost onboard sensors, including an inertial measurement unit (IMU), Pitot tube, and barometer, to achieve GNSS-independent state estimation, ensuring continuous and reliable flight performance under adverse conditions. Finally, practical UAV flight experiments based simulations were conducted. Experimental results validated the correctness and superiority of the proposed algorithm.
