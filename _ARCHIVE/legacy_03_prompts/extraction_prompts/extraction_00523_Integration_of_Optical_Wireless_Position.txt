ABSTRACT: This paper integrates optical wireless positioning (LED arrays + Cayley-Menger estimation with Bézier smoothing) and deep-learning drone detection. Atmospheric visibility significantly influences positioning accuracy, which improves from 23.2 cm MAE at 0.1 km to 11.8 cm at 20.0 km visibility for λ = 818 nm, where Bézier curves smooth trajectory errors while reducing path length versus Cayley-Menger results. YOLOv8 demonstrates slightly superior accuracy across most metrics, while YOLOv11 offers competitive performance with lower inference time, highlighting a trade-off between precision and computational efficiency. K-fold cross-validation enhances both models, with CV-YOLOv8 reaching 99.27% precision and 99.09% mAP50, demonstrating robust performance for indoor navigation.
