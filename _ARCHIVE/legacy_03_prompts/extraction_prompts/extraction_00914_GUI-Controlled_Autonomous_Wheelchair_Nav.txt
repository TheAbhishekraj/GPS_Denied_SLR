TITLE: GUI-Controlled Autonomous Wheelchair Navigation with Robust Sensor Fusion and Recovery

DOI: 10.1109/ICRM66809.2025.11349065
ABSTRACT: With the increasing demand for assistive technologies, autonomous wheelchairs have become essential tools in enhancing independent mobility for individuals with motor impairments. These systems significantly reduce dependence on caregivers and improve quality of life. This paper presents the Smart Wheelchair, an autonomous indoor mobility system that integrates a user-friendly graphical user interface (GUI), advanced sensor fusion, robust navigation algorithms, and intelligent recovery strategies. The system utilizes LiDAR, IMU, and wheel encoder data, fused through a dual-layered Extended Kalman Filter (EKF), to ensure accurate localization and mapping. Users can navigate to both predefined and user-defined destinations via the GUI, which also supports dynamic remapping for flexible environment adaptation. The navigation module employs A* for global planning and DWA for local path tracking, while the recovery mechanism autonomously resolves obstacle-induced deadlocks using real-time costmap feedback. Indoor experiments demonstrated a navigation accuracy of 92.5% for predefined goals and 95% for map-based goals. The recovery module successfully resolved 80% of obstruction scenarios autonomously, with GUI-assisted intervention achieving 100% resolution. A usability study involving 15 participants reported a GUI satisfaction score of 95.2%, confirming its accessibility and ease of use. Overall, the system delivers a reliable, adaptable, and inclusive solution for autonomous indoor mobility.
