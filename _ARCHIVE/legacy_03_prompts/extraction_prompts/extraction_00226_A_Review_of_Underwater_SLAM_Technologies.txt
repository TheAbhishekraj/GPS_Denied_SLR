ABSTRACT: Underwater navigation and positioning is a technology that provides attitude, speed, position and other information for underwater vehicles, and is a prerequisite for the successful completion of various underwater tasks. Due to the strong absorption and shielding effect of seawater medium on electromagnetic waves, commonly used radio, satellite and other navigation technologies are difficult to be adopted underwater, and the covert navigation of underwater vehicles makes underwater navigation and positioning become a hot research direction. With the maturity and wide application of SLAM technology, in the face of limited detection means and complex underwater unstructured scenes, underwater SLAM uses multiple information fusion technology to realize the positioning of submarine vehicles and navigation area mapping through the real-time underwater environmental information obtained, which has become one of the effective ways for autonomous underwater vehicles to realize autonomous positioning and navigation. At present, there are more articles related to underwater SLAM technology, but they are different in terms of detection means, fusion algorithms and realization methods, and their related results have been applied in different fields with different degrees of engineering. Especially in the remote mission under ice, since it is not possible to obtain satellite information for positioning correction by surfacing in open water, this puts forward higher requirements for how underwater SLAM technology can realize high-precision navigation and positioning under the condition of lack of important features. Therefore, this paper reviews the development history of underwater SLAM technology by collecting and organizing technical articles related to underwater SLAM, researches the difficulties existing in underwater SLAM technology, combs through the existing underwater SLAM processing, algorithm model and simulation, and summarizes, classifies, and compares and analyzes the different detection means and state estimation processing methods. The purpose of this paper is to help researchers better understand the system structure and development status of underwater SLAM, and to provide feasible and effective methods for solving underwater SLAM problems.
