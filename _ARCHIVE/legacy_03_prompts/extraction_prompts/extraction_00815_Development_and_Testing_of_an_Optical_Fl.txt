ABSTRACT: The study investigates the use of optical flow to enhance navigation in environments where Global Navigation Satellite System signals are unavailable, such as indoors or in urban areas. In this study, we considered the use of optical flow for navigation of autonomous moving objects, which is an important direction in modern navigation systems The theoretical foundations and components of the developed prototype are described. Initial tests revealed issues, and solutions were proposed, including delay compensation through cross-correlation for sensor synchronization. The second round of testing confirmed improved accuracy and noise reduction. Orientation by optical flow allows using a vehicle without a global positioning system, as well as identifying impacts on this system. Integration increases cyber resistance to attacks and, as a result, will increase the survivability of the system. This system is especially well suited for use at low altitudes of up to ten meters, particularly in the agricultural sphere.
