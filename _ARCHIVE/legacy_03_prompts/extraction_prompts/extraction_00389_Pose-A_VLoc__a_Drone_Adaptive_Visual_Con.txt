ABSTRACT: This paper proposes a localization method combining pose rectification and resource-adaptive parallel matching to address cross-view matching and efficiency challenges in GNSS-denied UAV visual localization. The method uses UAV IMU data to rotationally rectify images, reducing perspective differences, and employs dynamic multi-threading to improve matching efficiency.
