ABSTRACT: The effectiveness of multi-UAV (Unmanned Aerial Vehicles) systems has been demonstrated. Cooperative localization has become an important function in multi-UAV systems. This paper presents a rigorous review of cooperative localization solutions for multi-UAV systems. It explains the cooperative localization algorithm step by step. The sensor measurements and information fusion used prior to cooperative localization are described, providing the characteristics of various measurement methods and their impact on state estimation. It also discusses how information fusion affects positioning accuracy. Finally, it analyzes the advantages and disadvantages of different cooperative localization algorithms in multi-UAV systems. It also discusses the development of cooperative localization strategies and existing research gaps. © 2025 Elsevier Ltd
