ABSTRACT: The Doctor Assistant Robot research addresses the growing demands in healthcare by integrating advanced technologies such as WiFi fingerprinting, LiDAR, speech recognition, and natural language processing. Designed for complex healthcare environments, this robot leverages WiFi fingerprinting for precise indoor localization and dynamic mapping, while LiDAR enhances obstacle detection and path planning. Key algorithms, including LightGBM, are employed to train models on WiFi fingerprinting data for accurate localization, and Random Forest algorithms facilitate efficient navigation and obstacle classification. The robot also converts spoken prescriptions into electronic records, improving patient safety and compliance through seamless integration with health records. Equipped with robust hardware, the robot streamlines workflows and reduces the load on healthcare professionals. Performance evaluations indicate high real time accuracy rates: 92% in localization, 95% in obstacle avoidance, and 90 % in speech recognition. However, environmental factors, such as structural noise and human-induced interference, affected localization accuracy, highlighting the need for improved datasets and training techniques. Overall, this research successfully combines multiple technologies to enhance patient care and operational efficiency, representing a significant advancement in robot-assisted healthcare delivery.
