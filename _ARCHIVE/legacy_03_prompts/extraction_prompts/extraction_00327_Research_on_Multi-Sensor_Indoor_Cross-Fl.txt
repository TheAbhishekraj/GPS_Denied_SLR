VENUE: NOT_IN_ABSTRACT
DOI: 10.1109/ICISCAE66104.2025.11307785
ABSTRACT: To address the challenges of insufficient localization accuracy and dynamic obstacle avoidance for mobile robots in indoor multi-floor environments, this paper proposes an autonomous navigation system based on LiDAR-visual fused SLAM and multi-sensor fusion. A high-precision environmental map is constructed by integrating depth camera and 2D LiDAR data, while a BLE/MEMS cross-floor localization algorithm combined with IMU inertial navigation achieves floor discrimination and pose correction. Global path planning and dynamic obstacle avoidance are accomplished through an improved $\mathrm{A}^{*}$ algorithm and Dynamic Window Approach (DWA), with YOLOv5 object detection technology enhancing obstacle recognition capabilities. Experimental results demonstrate that the system achieves a localization error of less than $\mathbf{0. 1 5 m}$ in a $\mathbf{3}$-floor office building, a cross-floor path planning response time below 3 seconds, and a mean Average Precision (mAP) of 92.7% for target detection. This research provides a technical reference for autonomous navigation in complex indoor environments.
