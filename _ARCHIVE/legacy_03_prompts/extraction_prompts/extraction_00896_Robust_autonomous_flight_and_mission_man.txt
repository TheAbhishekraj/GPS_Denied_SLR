AUTHORS: Y. Bi; M. Lan; J. Li; K. Zhang; H. Qin; S. Lai; B. M. Chen
YEAR: 2017
VENUE: NOT_IN_ABSTRACT
DOI: 10.1109/ASCC.2017.8287144
ABSTRACT: This paper addresses the problem of autonomous flight in GPS-denied environments. We present our systematic solution with integrated hardware and software design, robust state estimation and mission management. A micro aerial vehicle (MAV) equipped with 2D laser scanner, camera, on-board computer and flight controller is designed and constructed to realize our flight missions. The 2D laser-based localization and Kalman filter-based sensor fusion provide a robust state estimation for the MAV. We also design a task tree-based mission management method to handle a complete mission with multiple tasks. Moreover, vision-based detection algorithms complement various tasks, such as target searching and precision landing. Extensive experimental results demonstrate the robustness and effectiveness of the overall integrated system. It was also validated by our championship winning in Singapore Amazing Flight Machine Competition (SAFMC) 2017.
