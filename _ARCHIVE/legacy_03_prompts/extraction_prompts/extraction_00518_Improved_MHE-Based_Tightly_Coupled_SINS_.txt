TITLE: Improved MHE-Based Tightly Coupled SINS/USBL Method for Underwater Navigation
AUTHORS: D. Wang; S. Sun; H. Huang; J. Wang
YEAR: 2025
VENUE: NOT_IN_ABSTRACT
DOI: 10.1109/JSEN.2025.3596701
ABSTRACT: In the field of underwater integrated navigation, multisensors are required to obtain accurate navigation information. Traditional sensor data fusion algorithms include Kalman filter (KF), extended KF (EKF), and so on. However, these filtering algorithms do not take into account the constraint problems in practical systems, such as the limitation of effective measurement range of sensors, underwater dynamic environmental noise, and physical boundaries of state variables. This may lead to inaccurate navigation information. Moving horizon estimation (MHE) can explicitly handle the state estimation problem for constrained systems. It has better applicability and accuracy for state estimation in nonlinear systems with constraints and uncertainties. At the same time, the prior covariance matrix in the traditional MHE is computed using EKF. However, in order to simplify the calculation, EKF brings a problem of precision decline. In response to the above issues, we propose an improved MHE-based tightly coupled strapdown inertial navigation system (SINS)/ultrashort baseline (USBL) method for underwater navigation. Using the improved adaptive unscented KF (UKF) to compute the prior covariance matrix in the cost function and employing the pigeon swarm algorithm (PSA) to solve the problem. Simulation and experimental results verify that this method is more applicable and accurate than conventional methods for nonlinear systems.
