ABSTRACT: This paper presents a reliable vision-based navigation system for guided aerial vehicles operating in GPS-denied environments. The proposed approach uses deep learning models trained on aerial map patches and high-resolution images captured directly by the unmanned aerial vehicles (UAV), enabling accurate position estimation without relying on GPS signals. By matching onboard visual information with preprocessed map data, the system enables accurate and stable position estimation. Experimental results show stable localization accuracy, with most positioning errors ranging between 5 m and 15 m using only vision sensors. Moreover, the system generalizes well to previously unseen areas without requiring additional GPS information, making it suitable for autonomous UAV navigation in GPS-denied environments.
