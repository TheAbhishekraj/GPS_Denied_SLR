AUTHORS: X. Li; C. Ni; J. She; Y. Liu
YEAR: 2025
VENUE: NOT_IN_ABSTRACT
DOI: 10.1109/UST66533.2025.11340017
ABSTRACT: The high-precision collaborative positioning of unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) swarm in Global Navigation Satellite System (GNSS)-denied environment has become a core challenge for achieving autonomous complex tasks. In this paper, it is assumed that two UAVs can obtain their relative distance via datalink and observe a common pair of ground targets, which is referred to as a shared-ground-target-groups. This paper constructs a collaborative positioning system based on the measured line-of-sights of these two targets and the distance between UAVs. A simulation platform is developed, and results confirm the feasibility of the proposed collaborative positioning framework.
