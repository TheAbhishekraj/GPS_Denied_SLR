TITLE: Formation Maintenance Method for Multi-UAV in Unknown Environments
AUTHORS: P. Liu; J. Chen; X. Lu; X. Tang; J. Fan; W. Zhou
YEAR: 2025
VENUE: NOT_IN_ABSTRACT
DOI: 10.1109/AIoTC66747.2025.11198761
ABSTRACT: The rapid development of automation technology has significantly contributed to the increasingly widespread application of Multiple Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (multi-UAV). However, in GPS-denied, anchorless and network- infrastructurefree unknown environments, multi-UAV faces challenges in accuracy. This paper proposes a formation maintenance method for multi-UAV in unknown environments. By leveraging Ultra Wide Band (UWB) ranging and communication devices, this method aims to enhance formation maintenance accuracy through adaptive fusion of UWB and Visual-Inertial Odometry (VIO) coordinates, prioritized trilateration, and Adaptive Extended Kalman Filtering (AEKF). The experimental results from the EuRoc dataset and a three-UAV system illustrate that the proposed method shows feasibility in maintaining and reconstructing formations while avoiding collisions.
