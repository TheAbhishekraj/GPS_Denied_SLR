DOI: 10.1109/RAAI64504.2024.10949537
ABSTRACT: This paper presents a new approach for indoor autonomous drone navigation using a deep reinforcement learning algorithm, specifically the Deep Deterministic Policy Gradient algorithm with position-based action (relative position) decisionmaking. Real-world tests with a quadrotor drone demonstrate the effectiveness of this method in practical applications. The unmanned aerial vehicle can safely navigate from a starting point to a destination without prior map knowledge, achieving smooth movements through a position control loop. The results confirm the suitability of this approach for real-world deployment. Compared to traditional map-based path planning algorithms like the A-star algorithm, the paths generated by our method show similar trends. Using relative position as an action in our approach allows the model to adapt to different robot platforms without retraining it when the kinematic model changes.
