TITLE: An Improved Extended Kalman Filter for UAV Localization in UWB Navigation System
AUTHORS: J. Chen; H. Ye; H. Pei
YEAR: 2023
VENUE: NOT_IN_ABSTRACT
DOI: 10.1109/CAC59555.2023.10449981
ABSTRACT: In this paper, we propose a new approach to estimate yaw angle of UAV operating in UWB navigation system. Absolute orientation algorithm which is commonly used to match images is used to align optical-flow-inertial coordinate to UWB navigation coordinate. An improved extended kalman filter fusing optical-flow data and initializing with absolute orientation estimation is designed. A simulation and experiment are conducted to verify the feasibility of the approach of yaw estimation along with accuracy of localization and reach a satisfied result.
