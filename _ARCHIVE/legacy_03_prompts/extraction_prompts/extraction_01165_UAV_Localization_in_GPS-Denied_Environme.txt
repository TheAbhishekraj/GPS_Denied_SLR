ABSTRACT: With the increasingly wide application of unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) in various scenarios, accurate UAV localization in indoor and urban environments has been a critical issue to address. However, in these signal-degraded environments, global positioning system (GPS) localization becomes unreliable; thus, alternative localization methods are essential. This article surveys localization methods for UAV systems in such environments. We classify these methods into two groups: 1) onboard-only methods, which rely exclusively on onboard sensors and 2)hybrid methods, which incorporate external anchors. To evaluate their performance in UAV localization systems, we first analyze the hardware requirements, the data preprocessing strategies, and the localization algorithms of the two groups, and then compare their experimental settings and localization accuracy. Finally, we summarize the strengths and limitations of these two groups and outline several research directions on GPS-denied UAV localization. © 2013 IEEE.
