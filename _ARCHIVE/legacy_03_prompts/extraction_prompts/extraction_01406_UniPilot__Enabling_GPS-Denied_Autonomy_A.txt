TITLE: UniPilot: Enabling GPS-Denied Autonomy Across Embodiments
AUTHORS: Kulkarni M.; Dharmadhikari M.; Khedekar N.; Nissov M.; Singh M.; Weiss P.; Alexis K.
YEAR: 2025
VENUE: NOT_IN_ABSTRACT
DOI: 10.1109/ICAR65334.2025.11338632
ABSTRACT: This paper presents UniPilot, a compact hardware-software autonomy payload that can be integrated across diverse robot embodiments to enable autonomous operation in GPS-denied environments. The system integrates a multi-modal sensing suite including LiDAR, radar, vision, and inertial sensing for robust operation in conditions where uni-modal approaches may fail. UniPilot runs a complete autonomy software comprising multi-modal perception, exploration and inspection path planning, and learning-based navigation policies. The payload provides robust localization, mapping, planning, and safety and control capabilities in a single unit that can be deployed across a wide range of platforms. A large number of experiments are conducted across diverse environments and on a variety of robot platforms to validate the mapping, planning, and safe navigation capabilities enabled by the payload. © 2025 IEEE.
