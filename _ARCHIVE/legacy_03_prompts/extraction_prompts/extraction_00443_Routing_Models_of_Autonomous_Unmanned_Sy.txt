ABSTRACT: The article is devoted to the application of radio frequency identification technology and unmanned systems for inventory automation in warehouses. The methods of autonomous navigation of drones in the premises, use of RFID for object identification and inventory are considered. The proposed solution can speed up the inventory process, especially in warehouses with vertical storage. Technical limitations, such as flight time and safety, as well as prospects for further development of the technology are considered. The paper considers software and hardware implementation of the task of autonomous navigation and identification of objects using machine vision and RFID reader, gives examples of installation of additional equipment to solve the tasks of object identification, and presents software solutions used to automate the inventory process of a closed warehouse.
