TITLE: Thermal Image Based Direct Method Visual-Inertial Odometry for Robot Navigation
AUTHORS: Z. Sun; J. Yuan; Y. Wang; G. Qu; H. Yu
YEAR: 2023
VENUE: NOT_IN_ABSTRACT
DOI: 10.1109/RICAI60863.2023.10488925
ABSTRACT: To perform robot SLAM tasks in environments with GPS-denied, low illumination, and sensor degradation, this paper proposes a thermal visual and IMU sensors tightly-coupled visual-inertial odometry based on a direct method optimization framework, which is immune to illumination conditions and visual occlusions. To address the low-texture and poor image quality of thermal images, the visual part of the system uses a direct method that can work in low-texture and repetitive texture environments. In addition, based on the existing direct method framework, keypoints are extracted and their reprojection error is calculated and added to the optimization objective to fully integrate the advantages of direct and indirect methods. To further improve the system's robustness, the visual and IMU sensors are tightly coupled and fused in the optimization framework. By minimizing the photometric error, IMU pre-integration error, and reprojection error, a cost function is constructed to jointly estimate the robot states and build a sparse points map. The effectiveness of the algorithm is verified through experiments on publicly available datasets.
