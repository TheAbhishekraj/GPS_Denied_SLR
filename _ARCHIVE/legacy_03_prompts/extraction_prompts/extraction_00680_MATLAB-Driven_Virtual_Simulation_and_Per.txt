ABSTRACT: Autonomous Underwater Vehicles (AUVs) require robust navigation and docking capabilities in GPS-denied underwater environments. This work presents a MATLAB-centric virtual simulation framework designed to model, test, and analyze the homing and docking behavior of underwater vehicles under realistic environmental disturbances. MATLAB is used extensively to develop the control algorithms, simulate dynamic responses, compute buoyancy and drag parameters, and visualize docking trajectories. Results confirm that MATLAB-based simulations reliably predict vehicle convergence, stability, and docking accuracy, demonstrating the effectiveness of adaptive feedback control strategies.
