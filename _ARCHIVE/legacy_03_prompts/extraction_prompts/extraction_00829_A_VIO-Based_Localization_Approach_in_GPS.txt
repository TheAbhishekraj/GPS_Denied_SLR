TITLE: A VIO-Based Localization Approach in GPS-denied Environments for an Unmanned Surface Vehicle
AUTHORS: X. Liu; Z. Hu; Z. Sun; J. Lu; W. Xie; W. Zhang
YEAR: 2023
VENUE: NOT_IN_ABSTRACT
DOI: 10.1109/ICARM58088.2023.10218814
ABSTRACT: In this paper, we propose a visual inertial odometer (VIO)-based localization approach for USV in GPS-denied environments. Considering the difficulty in capturing reliable features on the water surface, we set the camera's pitch angle properly to capture high-quality features, including points and lines, distributed on the bridge wall. Our work is based on the state-of-the-art simultaneous localization and mapping (SLAM) framework, VINS-Fusion. The point and line features observed by the left and right cameras are both added into the nonlinear optimization system along with the inertial measurement unit (IMU) measurement between frames. Through the joint optimization of landmark information and system state variables, the precise positioning of USV is realized. To validate the effectiveness of the proposed localization method, experimental results are presented and analyzed.
