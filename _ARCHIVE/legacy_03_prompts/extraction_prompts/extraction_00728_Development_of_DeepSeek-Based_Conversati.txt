ABSTRACT: This paper presents the design and development of Omar, a conversational robot system created to assist users in navigating indoor environments, specifically within the FKTE1 Building at Universiti Malaysia Perlis (UniMAP), called 'Omar'. The system leverages the DeepSeek large language model (LLM) to provide natural, context-aware interaction through voice commands. Integrated with speech-to-text, natural language processing, and text-to-speech modules, Omar can understand user queries, generate helpful navigation instructions, and respond verbally in real time. A custom graphical user interface (GUI) was also developed to complement voice interactions, enhancing accessibility. The robot was tested in realistic conditions, showing effective performance in understanding and guiding users. This work demonstrates the potential of LLM-powered robotics in localized, indoor human-robot interaction settings.
