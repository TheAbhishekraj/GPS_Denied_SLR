TITLE: UAV Cross-View Image Retrieval and Visual Geo-Localization in GNSS-Denied Environments
AUTHORS: Y. -C. Tai; H. -Y. Shen; C. -P. Yi
YEAR: 2026
ABSTRACT: UAV navigation often relies on GNSS, failing in denied environments like urban canyons. We propose an end-toend cross-view geo-localization framework mitigating geometric and appearance discrepancies. It achieves 93.59% Recall@1 on University-1652 and 93.56% on DenseUAV, outperforming baselines. Additionally, two efficient real-time inference strategies reduce the average continuous tracking error from 9.68 m to 0.95 m, demonstrating high-precision potential in GNSS-denied areas.
