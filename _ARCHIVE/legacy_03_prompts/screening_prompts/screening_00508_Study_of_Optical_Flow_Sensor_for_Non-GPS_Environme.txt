TITLE: Study of Optical Flow Sensor for Non-GPS Environment for Micro-Category UAV Application
AUTHORS: D. Sougrakpam; A. Themvinah; C. Thounaojam; D. K. Tarao; T. Chitaranjan; S. S. Singh
YEAR: 2025
ABSTRACT: This paper explores the development of a compact mini quadcopter equipped with an optical flow sensor, designed specifically for precise navigation in environments where GPS signals are unavailable or unreliable. By fusing data from the PX4FLOW optical sensor with inertial navigation inputs from the Pixhawk flight controller, the system achieves robust aerial vehicle localization through advanced Kalman filtering techniques. At its core, it leverages a high-sensitivity CMOS image sensor for computer vision, which performs reliably even in dim indoor spaces or outdoor low-light scenarios, ensuring consistent operation where traditional sensors might falter. The approach further incorporates block matching algorithms to track ground texture motion across sequential image frames, allowing for accurate estimation of the vehicle's position and orientation. Our experimental results highlight the quadcopter's superior localization accuracy over conventional GPS-dependent methods, both indoors and outdoors. This work paves the way for enhanced autonomous micro aerial vehicles, particularly in demanding applications like indoor surveillance.
