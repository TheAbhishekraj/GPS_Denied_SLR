TITLE: A Study on the Performance Complementarity of RGB-D Visual SLAM and LiDAR SLAM in Indoor Navigation Tasks
AUTHORS: W. Zhang; S. Wei
YEAR: 2026
ABSTRACT: Aiming at the problem that the performance boundaries and scene adaptability of RGB-D visual SLAM and LiDAR SLAM solutions in indoor robot autonomous navigation scenarios remain unclear, this paper conducts a full-chain quantitative comparison of localization, mapping, and navigation using RTAB-Map and Gmapping on the TurtleBot3 platform within the Gazebo simulation environment. Experiments show significant performance complementarity between the two, RTAB-Map achieves high global accuracy, its vertical projection mapping method can mark table structures below the laser scanning plane as obstacles, but incurs high computational overhead and is prone to false loop closures at overhanging structures, Gmapping offers high efficiency and good consistency, yet suffers from scanning blind spots for low, thin objects and the risk of failure in dynamic scenes. Both types of algorithms exhibit navigation system failures directly caused by mapping errors, revealing a key mechanism in which mapping deviations such as overfilling or information omission are amplified by navigation decisions. This study provides a quantitative basis for SLAM solution selection in indoor robots and offers a reference direction for vision-LiDAR fusion optimization.
