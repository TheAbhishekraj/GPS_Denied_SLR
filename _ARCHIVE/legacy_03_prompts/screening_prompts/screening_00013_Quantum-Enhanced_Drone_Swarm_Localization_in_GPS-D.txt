TITLE: Quantum-Enhanced Drone Swarm Localization in GPS-Denied Environment
AUTHORS: S. K. Thota
YEAR: 2026
ABSTRACT: Solutions for getting drone swarms localized when GPS is out of the picture. That's a big problem, really cuts into how reliable those autonomous jobs can be. This paper brings in a model that uses quantum stuff to boost the accuracy and how well the drones stick together. The idea here mixes quantum entanglement for syncing up between drones. It also throws in quantum-enhanced Kalman filtering to fuse sensor data better. That way, they get positions figured out more precisely and tougher against messes. We ran experiments on simulated groups of 10 drones. The setup cut down localization errors. It boosted computational efficiency too, by 25 per cent and 30 per cent in swarms dealing with different levels of interference. The thing is, these results point to quantum computing stepping up as a solid, scalable fix for swarm localization issues. It has real potential out there in tough spots without GPS, like disaster responses or city ops and even military stuff. The paper spells out how quantum tech could lead to the next wave of autonomous systems. It spots chances for using quantum algorithms in real-time navigation with multiple agents.
