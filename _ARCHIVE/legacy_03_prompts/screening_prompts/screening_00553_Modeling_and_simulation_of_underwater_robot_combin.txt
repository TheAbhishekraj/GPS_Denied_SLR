TITLE: Modeling and simulation of underwater robot combined navigation and position based on adaptive filter gain UKF
AUTHORS: X. Yang; Z. Li
YEAR: 2022
ABSTRACT: Aiming at the shortcoming of a single sensor of an underwater robot providing inadequate navigation positioning data and low precision data, this paper proposes an improved combination of navigation methods to achieve underwater robot navigation and positioning. Different from the traditional combined navigation, this paper uses gyroscopes, encoders, and ranging sonar to obtain attitude, speed, and position information, which greatly reduces the cost of combined navigation and positioning. The traditional Kalman filtering method adopts the constant value filter gain method, which can only be filtered for linear systems, and the fusion processing of sensor data is narrow in the field of application. This paper adopts the adaptive filter gain unscented Kalman filter (AFGUKF), According to the system state, the filter gain is adjusted in real-time to ensure the universal adaptability of the nonlinear filter system and improve the navigation and positioning accuracy. Simulation results show that the positioning accuracy of the combined navigation system composed of gyroscope, encoder and ranging sonar is significantly better than that of the single sensor navigation system, which meets the positioning control requirements of underwater robots.
