TITLE: Semi-Aerodynamic-Model-Aided Filtering Algorithm for Fixed-Wing Unmanned Aerial Vehicles
AUTHORS: X. Ye; F. Song; Z. He; Y. Cai; X. Zhang; D. Huang
YEAR: 2025
ABSTRACT: To enhance the navigation performance and distur-bance resistance of unmanned aerial vehicles (UAV s) in complex environments, This paper proposes a semi-aerodynamic model fusion algorithm that relies exclusively on ground-measurable pa-rameters. By designing and training a Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) network, we enable drift-free prediction of aerodynamic angles (angle of attack and sideslip angle) using easily accessible onboard data such as control surface deflections, without relying on external positioning. Furthermore, the dynamic model and multi-source navigation problem are formulated within a right-invariant Extended Kalman Filter (EKF) framework on Lie groups, enabling multi-rate fusion of heterogeneous onboard sensor measurements. Experimental results demonstrate the algorithm's resilience in Global Navigation Satellite System (GNSS)-denied environments.
