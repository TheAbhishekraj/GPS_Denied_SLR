TITLE: Motion State Classification for Drone Localisation in GPS-Denied Environments using TinyML on Resource-Constrained Hardware
AUTHORS: V. Barbu; A. -M. Drăgulinescu; A. Țapu
YEAR: 2025
ABSTRACT: Reliable UAV localisation in GPS-denied environments remains challenging, particularly under strict size, weight, and power constraints. This work presents a fully embedded approach that combines inertial and environmental sensing with Tiny Machine Learning models for on-board motion-state inference. A compact feed-forward neural network runs on a Cortex-M4 microcontroller using windowed accelerometer data, achieving real-time classification across eleven motion classes. Experiments show high accuracy for static states (F1 > 0.92) and promising performance for dynamic manoeuvres (F1 ≈ 0.70–0.80) with an 18 ms inference latency. The system demonstrates the feasibility of low-power, GPS-independent motion understanding for future embedded sensor-fusion-based localisation.
