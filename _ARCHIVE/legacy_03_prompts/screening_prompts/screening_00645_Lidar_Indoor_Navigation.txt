TITLE: Lidar Indoor Navigation
AUTHORS: V. Y; N. M; K. B. Maradi; P. Aneesh; K. R
YEAR: 2025
ABSTRACT: For many applications, Indoor navigation is crucial. Especially in environments such as large buildings, underground structures, and complex indoor spaces where GPS signals are weak or unavailable. In this work, a LiDAR-based navigation system that employs Simultaneous Localization and Mapping (SLAM) methods for accurate 2D mapping and localization in indoor environments is introduced. Because it always estimates its location relative to the environment, SLAM allows the system to build good maps of the environment. Optimal path planning is achieved by the use of the A* algorithm, providing a powerful method for finding the best paths to take and avoid obstacles. In addition, the project employs PointNet++ for advanced point cloud processing, which significantly enhances the ability of the system to analyze and classify indoor objects and structures and gain a deeper understanding of the environment. Integrating SLAM, A*, and PointNet++ creates a robust indoor localization and navigation system that is perfect for application in augmented reality, smart environments, and assistive technology for individuals with disabilities. Due to the high mapping and path planning accuracy of the system, it operates reliably in a wide range of real-world indoor environments and greatly enhances navigation efficiency and spatial awareness.
