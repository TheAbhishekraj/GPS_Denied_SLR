TITLE: Integrating RTAB-Map SLAM and Lightweight YOLOv5 Nano for Indoor Asset Detection with Drone-Based Mapping
AUTHORS: A. J. A. Sharkh; S. I. Ismail; S. Rubab; B. Soudan
YEAR: 2025
ABSTRACT: Accurate inventory tracking in indoor warehouse environments remains a persistent challenge, particularly in Global Positioning System (GPS)-denied conditions where traditional solutions such as Light Detection and Ranging (LiDAR) and Radio-Frequency Identification (RFID) are often expensive or infrastructure-dependent. This paper presents a dual-system drone platform that integrates Real-Time Appearance-Based Mapping (RTAB-Map) for vision-based Simultaneous Localization and Mapping (SLAM) and You Only Look Once version 5 Nano (YOLOv5n), a lightweight Artificial Intelligence (AI) model, for real-time object detection on embedded edge hardware. The system operates in two phases: an initial mapping stage using stereo input from an OAK-D Pro camera, followed by an object detection phase. While the RTABMap subsystem successfully generated dense three-dimensional $(3 D)$ point clouds, the resulting spatial maps exhibited significant noise and attempts to convert them into usable twodimensional (2D) occupancy grids for navigation were unsuccessful. In contrast, the YOLOv5n pipeline deployed entirely on the OAK-D Pro's onboard Vision Processing Unit (VPU) achieved high frame rates and consistent object detection. The model's lightweight architecture enabled realtime inference without Graphics Processing Unit (GPU) dependency, supporting cost-efficient deployment in edge-based applications. These findings underscore the viability of embedded AI for indoor asset recognition while also revealing key challenges in vision-only SLAM for autonomous warehouse navigation.
