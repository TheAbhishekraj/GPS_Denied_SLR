TITLE: Federated Deep Reinforcement Learning for Collaborative Autonomous Navigation in GPS-Denied Environments
AUTHORS: N. D. Almalis; G. A. Tsihrintzis; A. Stergioulis; M. Michail
YEAR: 2025
ABSTRACT: This paper presents a novel framework for collaborative autonomous drone navigation in GPS-denied environments, integrating federated deep reinforcement learning (FDRL) with real-time computer vision and multi-sensor fusion. Unlike centralized learning approaches, the proposed architecture enables on-device training and inference, allowing UAVs to learn independently while periodically sharing model updates in a privacy-preserving manner. The system leverages YOLOv8 and DeepLabv3+ for semantic perception, PPO-based policy optimization for adaptive path planning, and FedAvg aggregation for decentralized knowledge synchronization. The framework was evaluated using both simulated and real-world scenarios, including urban canyons and agricultural test fields, with hardware consisting of DJI Matrice 100 drones equipped with RGB, depth, thermal cameras, and IMUs, powered by Jetson Xavier NX boards. Results show significant improvements over baseline and commercial models in key metrics such as detection accuracy, energy efficiency, trajectory deviation, and navigation time. The proposed system demonstrates strong generalization capabilities, real-time performance, and adaptability to dynamic, unstructured environments, establishing a solid foundation for future UAV swarm autonomy in GPS-denied contexts.
