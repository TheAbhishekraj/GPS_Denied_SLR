TITLE: A Resilient Cooperative Homing Method with Foundation Models for Multi-Robot Systems in GPS-Denied Unknown Environments
AUTHORS: H. Lee
YEAR: 2025
ABSTRACT: Multi-robot systems (MRS) operating in GPS-denied unknown environments face critical challenges in achieving cooperative autonomous homing, where resilience to uncertainty, disruption, and adversarial conditions is essential. This paper proposes a resilient cooperative homing method with foundation models for multi-robot systems in GPS-denied unknown environments. The proposed method consists of three components: (i) cooperative homing map construction, enabling reliable shared mapping under noisy and incomplete sensing, (ii) cooperative path planning, generating adaptive and robust trajectories in degraded communication conditions, and (iii) cooperative collision-free navigation, ensuring safe and stable return of multiple robots despite disturbances or adversarial interference. The proposed method can detect the fail in the cooperative homing map construction based on deep learning with foundation methods. In preliminary experiments, the feasibility of the proposed method was verified by showing high accuracy in detecting the fail in cooperative homing map construction.
