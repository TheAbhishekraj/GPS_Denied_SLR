TITLE: Research On Air-Ground Collaborative Navigation Method in Unknown Indoor Environment
AUTHORS: Z. Wang; X. Liang; J. Chen; Z. Huang
YEAR: 2026
ABSTRACT: Indoor positioning and navigation technique is the fundamental function of unmanned system. But due to weak satellite signal in indoor environment, it is not easy to achieve accurate indoor positioning and navigation. To address this matter, the paper proposed an air-ground collaborative navigation method for indoor unknown environment. Firstly, the paper introduced the overall mission of indoor air-ground collaborative navigation and established the hardware subsystems of unmanned aerial vehicle(UAV) and unmanned ground vehicle(UGV) on this basis and realized autonomous control. Secondly, Kalman filtering algorithm was used to integrate the data of light stream and angle sensor, making possible high-precision stable hovering of UAV at a fixed point. Meanwhile, UAV collected indoor information with built-in cameras and sent it back to ground station in the form of coordinate. However, errors would continuously accumulate during the UAV and UGV resulting in inaccurate positioning information for both. Thus, a relative positioning method is designed, which can calibrate the location and angle information of UGV by turning to a fixed barrier as reference point and thereby correct 2D map. The result of simulation and experimentation indicates that flight stability of UAV has been improved after information fusion, time-consuming of air-ground collaborative navigation system is 67% less than single navigation, and navigation success rate is improved by 56% higher while maximum position error is reduced by 40%.
