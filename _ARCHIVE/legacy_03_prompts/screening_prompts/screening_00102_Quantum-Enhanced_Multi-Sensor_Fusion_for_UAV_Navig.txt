TITLE: Quantum-Enhanced Multi-Sensor Fusion for UAV Navigation in GPS-Denied Environments
AUTHORS: A. Moussa; I. N. Him; K. Chawqi; Y. Salih-Alj
YEAR: 2026
ABSTRACT: Reliable navigation in GPS-denied or degraded environments remains a core challenge for Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs), as inertial-vision systems accumulate drift over time. This paper proposes a feasibility study of a quantum-enhanced multi-sensor fusion framework that integrates cold-atom accelerometer-inspired aiding and NV-center-based heading stabilization with conventional IMU, camera, and LiDAR measurements inside a unified Extended Kalman Filter (EKF). The proposed architecture combines high-rate classical sensing with low-rate, bias-stable quantum corrections to suppress long-term drift during GNSS outages. Using real IMU data from the EuRoC MAV benchmark, both a standard IMU+GPS/VO-style EKF and a quantum-enhanced EKF under injected GPS-denial intervals are evaluated. The results show substantially reduced velocity and position drift, extended holdover time, and improved error distributions for the quantum-aided filter. Deployment considerations, including power, cost, integration complexity, and scalability, are discussed to assess the real-world feasibility of quantum-aided fusion as a promising pathway toward resilient all-weather UAV autonomy across highly demanding environments.
