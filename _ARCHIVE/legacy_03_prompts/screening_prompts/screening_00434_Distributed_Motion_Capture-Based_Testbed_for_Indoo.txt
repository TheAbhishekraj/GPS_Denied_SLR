TITLE: Distributed Motion Capture-Based Testbed for Indoor UAV Navigation Research
AUTHORS: M. Kurenda; K. Wojtowicz; K. Sibilski; J. Djabin; J. Kochan; A. Marut
YEAR: 2026
ABSTRACT: This paper presents the design and implementation of a motion capture-based indoor testbed for unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) navigation research. The system integrates a Qualisys motion capture subsystem, a central processing computer, and an ESP32-based cascaded position-velocity controller interfaced with a Betaflight flight controller. Navigation state data are transmitted over a low-latency UDP link and used for real-time control. Experimental validation demonstrates stable indoor flight and confirms correct operation of the distributed architecture and failsafe mechanisms. The platform serves as a controlled research environment for the future transition toward fully onboard autonomous navigation.
