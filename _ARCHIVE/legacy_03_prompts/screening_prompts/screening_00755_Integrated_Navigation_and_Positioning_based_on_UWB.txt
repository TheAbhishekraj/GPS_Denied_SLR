TITLE: Integrated Navigation and Positioning based on UWB
AUTHORS: H. Qian; F. Wang
YEAR: 2020
ABSTRACT: With the rapid development of the service robot industry, the realization of autonomous robot indoor navigation has become the focus of research and development institutions. Due to the low cost and high precision, ultra-wideband (UWB) positioning has become an ideal indoor positioning solution. However, under non-line-of-sight (NLOS) conditions, the positioning accuracy of UWB signals will be reduced, and the deviation of the positioning solution due to signal interference will cause the trajectory of the solution to be discontinuous and jump, which makes it impossible to complete continuous measurement. To solve this problem, the UWB/INS integrated navigation method is adopted. Field tests show that the adopted method can track the navigation continuous without jumping, which greatly improves the positioning accuracy.
