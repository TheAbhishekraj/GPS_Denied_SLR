TITLE: Vision-Based Autonomous Drone Navigation Using Deep Reinforcement Learning
AUTHORS: N. Narole; D. Prasad; S. R. Gaikwad; S. S. Badhiye; B. Jolad; J. S. Nadaf
YEAR: 2026
ABSTRACT: Vision-guided autonomous drone navigation has been a high-priority field of research with the rise of the need of intelligent unmanned aerial vehicles that can be used in complex, dynamic, and GPS-denied settings. The classical and rule-based methods of control do not tend to be generalizable to different visual conditions, obstacles, and environmental versions because of uncertainties. With this, this paper introduces a deep reinforcement learning (DRL)-based autonomous drone navigation framework based on vision, which combines perception, decision-making and control in one learning framework. The system that is proposed would develop autonomous navigation as a sequential decision-making problem, in which the drone would learn the best navigation policies by observing the visual data itself using an on-board camera. The representation of states, actions and rewards are developed to provide stable learning without violating operational limits, e.g. collision avoidance, flight stability and constrained onboard computation. Various DRA algorithms, such as Deep Q-Networks (DQN), Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO), Asynchronous Advantage Actor-Critic (A3C) and Soft Actor-Critic (SAC) are compared to determine whether each can be used in real-time aerial navigation tasks. The perception module is based on convolutional neural network (CNN) that is used to derive compact and informative visual features on the raw image streams, and thus effective environment perception without depending on handcrafted features. This includes reward shaping and exploration techniques that will hasten convergence and improve policy resilience.
