TITLE: Design and Implementation of a Behavior-Based Navigation and Obstacle Avoidance System for Quadrotor UAVs in Indoor Environments Using RPLIDAR Sensor
AUTHORS: M. Y. Mustar; B. S. Tryatmojo; R. A. Ghifari; M. Hisyam; M. A. F. Rizqullah; S. Wahyuni
YEAR: 2025
ABSTRACT: Navigation and obstacle avoidance systems are significant challenges for UAVs in indoor environments without GPS signal support. This study proposes a behavior-based control navigation system on a Quadrotor UAV, with the RPLIDAR A1M8 sensor as the primary source of environmental perception. The system is designed with a behavior-based navigation approach, where the UAV can adaptively choose the correct behavior to avoid obstacles based on distance data obtained from the RPLIDAR sensor. In addition, the ROS-based Hector SLAM algorithm is used separately to support the mapping and tracking process of the UAV position during navigation. The RPLIDAR sensor installed on the system, at an angle reading of 0° with an actual range of 500 cm, shows a maximum positive bias absolute error measurement of 36 cm. The system was successfully implemented according to its objectives. The results show that the UAV can avoid obstacles from various angles. Testing navigation in an indoor environment in the form of an artificial corridor, the vehicle was able to complete navigation without collision with the fastest time of 52 seconds; then, with Hector SLAM-ROS, the vehicle succeeded in creating an arena map and trajectory of the journey during navigation.
