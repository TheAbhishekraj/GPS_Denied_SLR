TITLE: Autonomous Indoor Navigation Using PPO and SAC with Cross-sensor fusion in Simulated Environment
AUTHORS: Seher; S. U. Bazai; M. I. Ghafoor; U. A. Bhatti; M. A. Siddiqui
YEAR: 2025
ABSTRACT: Intelligent robots are increasingly deployed for autonomous navigation in dynamic indoor environments. Sensor selection plays a critical role in determining how effectively a robot perceives and interacts with its surroundings. While RGB cameras and 2D LiDARs are commonly used, they face challenges such as sensitivity to lighting and limited depth perception. This study explores the use of a 3D LiDAR and RGB camera cross-sensor fusion technique , Fusion technique, to improve environmental understanding. We train and evaluate Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) and Soft Actor-Critic (SAC), using these two Reinforcement learning algorithms only Fusion data in a simulated, dynamic and map-free indoor environment. Our results show that PPO outperforms SAC in terms of safety metrics especially Probability of identification. This research highlights the effectiveness of 3D LiDAR data in enabling robust, map-free indoor navigation, offering a promising direction for future autonomous robotic systems.
