## 2. Problem Statement
"This paper describes a multi- robot coordinated heterogeneous vehicle which employs cooperative unmanned ground vehicles (UGVs) and unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) to explore and map a GPS- denied unknown terrain." [p.1]

## 3. Motivation
"Exploration processes like cave exploration, tunnel inspections required an accurate and quick mapping of the environment in emergencies for rescue or maintenance operations." [p.1]

## 4. GPS-Denied Context

## 5. Proposed Method
Autonomous exploration and mapping system using heterogeneous team; COOPERATIVE
"This paper proposes an autonomous exploration and mapping system using 5 UGVs and 4 UAVs in heterogeneous teams." [p.1]

## 6. System Architecture
- Platform: heterogeneous team of 5 UGVs and 4 UAVs
"5 UGVs and 4 UAVs in heterogeneous teams" [p.1]
- Sensors: LIDAR on UGVs, depth sensor camera on UAVs
"Each follower UGV in the system is followed by a UAV at a specific height. The follower UGVs are equipped with a LIDAR sensor for creating the 2D map. Each UAV is equipped with a depth sensor camera that creates the point cloud data" [p.2]
- Compute: ROS-Gazebo simulation
"Nexus omnidirectional robots for unmanned ground vehicles and Hector quadrotors for unmanned aerial vehicles are used to test the proposed multi- robot system in the Robot Operating System (ROS) - Gazebo environment." [p.2]
