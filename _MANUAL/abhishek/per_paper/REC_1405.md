## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: Angle robustness navigation
Category: VISION_OBJECT
The paper proposes a novel angle robustness navigation paradigm for point-to-point navigation tasks. It directly predicts the direction angle based on historical frames using a model with Adaptive Feature Enhance Module, Cross-knowledge Attention-guided Module, and Robust Task-oriented Head Module.
"In this paper, we present a novel angle robustness navigation paradigm to deal with flight deviation in point-to-point navigation tasks." [p.1]
