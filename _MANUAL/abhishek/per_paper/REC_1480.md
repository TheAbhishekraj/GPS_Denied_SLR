## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: NOT_REPORTED (iterative pose estimation)
Category: VISION_OBJECT
The paper proposes an iterative technique for pose estimation using monocular vision. It estimates the robot's pose using individual images by constantly tracking four fixed feature points in a rectangular pattern. The method iteratively corrects the image to estimate camera pose.
"The proposed method estimates the robot's pose using individual images by constantly tracking four fixed feature points in a rectangular pattern, whose positions are known a priori." [p.1]
