## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: NOT_REPORTED
Category: HYBRID
The paper presents a method for UAV self-localization using vision and mmWave radar data. It generates a dense point cloud through depth estimation and semantic segmentation, refined using sparse mmWave radar point cloud. A semantic-guided clustering method removes noise and extracts structural elements.
"This paper presents a robust and cost-effective method for UAV self-localization by using vision and millimeter-wave (mmWave) radar data in GNSS-denied environments." [p.1]
