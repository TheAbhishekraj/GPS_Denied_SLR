## 2. Problem Statement
"localization is a significant challenge for UAVs in GNSS-denied spaces, such as under bridges, where satellite signals are obstructed." [p.1]

## 3. Motivation
"To address this issue, we employed a cooperative localization system consisting of a work-UAV, which performs tasks in the GNSS-denied space, and support-UAVs, which relatively localize the work-UAV." [p.1]
