## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: EKF-Cooperative Monocular SLAM
Category: VIO | COOPERATIVE
The paper presents a cooperative monocular-based SLAM approach for multi-UAV systems in GPS-denied environments. It uses visual information from monocular cameras mounted on aerial vehicles flying in formation. Relative distance measurements between UAVs are obtained via homography and fused into an Extended Kalman Filter (EKF) to improve observability. The system model includes the dynamics of each aerial robot and the Euclidean position of landmarks.
"This work presents a cooperative monocular-based SLAM approach for multi-UAV systems that can operate in GPS-denied environments." [p.1]
