## 2. Problem Statement
"During the last decade, the number of applications of UAVs has continuously increased, making the global UAV market one of those with the highest rate of growth. The worldwide increasing usage of UAVs is causing an ever-growing demand for efficient solutions in order to make them usable in every kind of working condition." [p.1]

## 3. Motivation
"nowadays the main restriction to the usage of UAVs is probably the need of reliable position estimates provided by using the Global Navigation Satellite System (GNSS): since UAVs mostly rely on the integration of GNSS/Inertial Navigation System (INS) to properly fulfil their tasks, they face a major challenge while navigating in GNSS denied environments." [p.1]

## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: Micro-Radar and UWB aided INS
Category: HYBRID
"This work combines the use of radar (as radar odometry) and UWB systems (CUPT), as both systems complement each other." [p.2]

## 6. System Architecture
- Platform: 3DR SOLO Quadcopter
  "The flight tests have been performed with a commercial on shelf UAV (3DR SOLO Quadcopter)" [p.4]
- Sensors: micro FMCW radar, UWB rover, IMU, GPS
  "The useful payload during the flight includes the micro-radar system and the UWB system (Rover)" [p.4]
- Compute: Odoo X86 Ultra
  "The radar data are processed with the aid of the onboard PC (Odoo X86 Ultra) through an ethernet connection." [p.4]
- Communication: UWB anchors
  "Six UWB anchors were distributed on the ground" [p.8]

## 7. Experimental Setup
- real_or_sim: REAL
  "Experimental flight considered in this section" [p.4]
- environment: outdoor open area
  "Flight duration was 140 sec, with 19 waypoints." [p.4]
- trials: 5 test scenarios
  "Five test scenarios were presented in the result section" [p.8]
- baselines: INS dead reckoning, UWB standalone
  "The first test scenario is to evaluate the performance of the IMU only when working as dead reckoning" [p.4]
- dataset: Self-collected

## 18. Best Combination & Accuracy
### 18.1 Best sensor combination
"micro FMCW radar tilted by 60 degrees and a UWB rover" [p.8]

### 18.2 Best algorithm
"Extended Kalman Filter" [p.3]

### 18.3 Best accuracy
UWB/RO RMSE-North: 0.60 m (scenario 5) [p.7]

### 18.4 Baseline comparison at best configuration
"the integration between radar and UWB showed 44% and 26% enhancement in the RMSE-North and RMSE-East direction respectively, when compared to the UWB solution only." [p.8]

### 18.5 Conditions of best accuracy
"when four UWB anchors were used" [p.7]

### 18.6 Trade-offs
"usage of radar only does not provide sufficient information about the current heading direction, whereas the use of a standalone UWB system may require a huge infrastructure" [p.8]

### 18.7 Generalization limits
"these results are obtained with a high density of UWB anchors: such kind of UWB infrastructure might be expensive and/or impracticable in a large area." [p.5]
