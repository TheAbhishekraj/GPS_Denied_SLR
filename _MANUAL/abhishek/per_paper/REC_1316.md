## 2. Problem Statement
"Engineering structures like bridges shadow global navigation satellite system (GNSS), which complicates precise localization. Simultaneous localization and mapping (SLAM) algorithms offer a sufficient solution, since they do not require GNSS. However, testing and comparing SLAM algorithms in GNSS-denied areas is difficult due to missing ground truth data." [p.1]

## 3. Motivation
"This work presents an approach to measuring the performance of SLAM in indoor and outdoor GNSS-denied areas using a terrestrial scanner Leica RTC360 and a tachymeter to acquire point cloud and trajectory information." [p.1]

## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: NOT_REPORTED (evaluation method)
Category: OTHER (SLAM evaluation)
The paper presents an approach for measuring the performance of SLAM in GNSS-denied areas using a terrestrial laser scanner and a tachymeter. It includes time synchronization between tachymeter and SLAM trajectory data, and evaluates three LiDAR-based SLAM algorithms (KISS-ICP, SC-LIO-SAM, MA-LIO).
"This work presents an approach to measuring the performance of SLAM in indoor and outdoor GNSS-denied areas using a terrestrial scanner Leica RTC360 and a tachymeter to acquire point cloud and trajectory information." [p.1]

## 6. System Architecture
- Platform: Husky mobile robotic platform (UGV)
- Sensors: Two Velodyne VLP-16 LiDAR (horizontal and vertical), 3DM-GX5-25 IMU
- Compute: AMD Ryzen 9 5900X 12-core processor (for post-processing)
- Communication: ROS
"The sensor system used in this work consists of a horizontal and vertical Velodyne VLP-16 LiDAR and an IMU 3DM-GX5-25 mounted on a rigid frame" [p.3]
"The sensor setup is mounted on a Husky mobile robotic platform from Clearpath Robotics" [p.3]
