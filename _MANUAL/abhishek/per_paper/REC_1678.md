## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: Asynchronous lidar geolocation
Category: LIDAR | COOPERATIVE
The paper presents an asynchronous lidar system where laser transmitter and detectors are on separate platforms, providing observational redundancy for geolocation in GNSS-denied environments.
"An asynchronous lidar design is one in which the laser transmitter and detectors/receivers are disconnected and carried on separate platforms. This unique geometry offers observational redundancy that can be used to estimate the trajectory of the receiver platforms." [p.1]
