## 2. Problem Statement
"Unfortunately, most indoor environments remain without access to external positioning systems, and autonomous MAVs are very limited in their ability to operate in these areas." [p.1]

## 3. Motivation
"This article aims to investigate a SLAM technique that fuses visual information and measurements from the inertial measurement unit (IMU), to robustly obtain the 6DOF pose estimation of a MAV within a local map of the environment." [p.1]

## 4. GPS-Denied Context
INDOOR
"the MAV must rely on its own onboard sensors to autonomously navigate in unknown, hostile and GPS denied environments, such as ruined or semi-demolished buildings." [p.1]

## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: NOT_REPORTED
Category: VSLAM | HYBRID
The paper presents an indoor SLAM system for MAVs that fuses measurements from a monocular camera and onboard sensors (IMU) using an Extended Kalman Filter. It uses LSD-SLAM and ORB-SLAM algorithms and solves the scale ambiguity using IMU data.
"This article aims to investigate a SLAM technique that fuses visual information and measurements from the inertial measurement unit (IMU), to robustly obtain the 6DOF pose estimation of a MAV within a local map of the environment." [p.1]

## 6. System Architecture
- Platform: Parrot Bebop quadrotor
- Sensors: Frontal fisheye camera, vertical camera, ultrasonic altimeter, 3-axis accelerometer, 2 gyroscopes, barometer
- Compute: Ground station (distributed ROS nodes)
- Communication: Wi-Fi
"Our quadrotor MAV, shown in Fig. 1, is the Bebop from Parrot" [p.2]
"The onboard controller and processor perform sensor readings and basic control of the MAV. The ground station executes our SLAM system" [p.2]
