## 1. Bibliographic Metadata
K. Gryte, T. H. Bryne, S. M. Albrektsen, and T. A. Johansen, "Field Test Results of GNSS-denied Inertial Navigation aided by Phased-array Radio Systems for UAVs," in 2019 International Conference on Unmanned Aircraft Systems (ICUAS), Atlanta, GA, USA, June 11-14, 2019.

## 2. Problem Statement
"Unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) often depend on global navigation satellite systems (GNSS) and magnetic compasses for navigation, making them exposed to malicious attacks and sensitive to magnetic anomalies" [p.1]

## 3. Motivation
"With more frequent use of UAVs, the need for alternatives to GNSS becomes more pressing, to allow safe, continuous operation of such systems." [p.1]

## 4. GPS-Denied Context
MIXED
"By rather relying on inertial navigation aided by spherical position measurements from a phased-array radio system (PARS), these vulnerabilities are avoided." [p.1]

## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: PARS-aided INS with MEKF
Category: COOPERATIVE
"the PARS-based navigation system is implemented using an indirect multiplicative extended Kalman filter (MEKF)" [p.1]
