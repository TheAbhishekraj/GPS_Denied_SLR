## 2. Problem Statement
"However, this autonomous use of UAVs inside a seedling pine forest environment is challenging because of the presence of many obstacles (i.e. the canopy), and the need to precisely and selectively spray the repellent chemical on the top of individual young trees." [p.1]

## 3. Motivation
"The aim of this study is to develop a solution that addresses the problem of estimating the pose of the UAV in the young pine forest as a basis for the development of autonomous UAVs for spraying non-toxic chemicals in the seedling pine forest in subsequent studies." [p.1]

## 4. GPS-Denied Context
FOREST
"When navigating in a young forest environment that is surrounded in certain areas by large trees, the GNSS signal may degrade when the UAV flies closer to the large trees." [p.1]

## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: NOT_REPORTED (LiDAR-odometry based pose estimation)
Category: LIDAR | SLAM
The method uses a graph-based approach to fuse data from onboard IMU, GNSS receiver, and 3D LiDAR. Features are detected from each LiDAR scan, a local map is built, and LiDAR odometry is computed in real time. Noise covariance of LiDAR odometry is updated using fitness score.
"Our method uses graph-based approach to fuse data from an onboard IMU sensors, a GNSS receiver and a 3D LiDAR." [p.1]

## 6. System Architecture
- Platform: DJI Matrice 100 UAV
- Sensors: Velodyne VLP16 3D LiDAR, Epson IMU, GARMIN GPS 18x
- Compute: Raspberry Pi 4 Model B (companion computer), laptop for state estimation
- Communication: ROS
"The DJI Matrice 100 UAV shown in Fig. 2. It is equipped with the Velodyne VLP16 light 'PUCK' LiDAR" [p.5]
"A Raspberry Pi 4 Model B board is used as a companion computer to log data from the FCU, and the different external sensors." [p.5]
