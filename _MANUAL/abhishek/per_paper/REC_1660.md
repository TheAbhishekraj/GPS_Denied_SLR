## 2. Problem Statement
"Especially in GNSS-denied environments, relying solely on the onboard Inertial Measurement Unit (IMU) of the Micro Aerial Vehicles (MAVs) for positioning is not practical." [p.1]

## 3. Motivation

## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: Ranging and IMU-based relative positioning
Category: HYBRID
The paper proposes a cooperative relative positioning method for two MAVs using inter-vehicle ranging and onboard IMU information, fused via Extended Kalman Filter (EKF).
"Specifically, the system model framework is first constructed, and then the Extended Kalman Filter (EKF) algorithm, which is introduced for its ability to handle nonlinear systems, is employed to fuse inter-vehicle ranging and onboard IMU information, achieving joint position estimation of the MAVs." [p.1]
