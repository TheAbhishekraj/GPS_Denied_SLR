## 1. Bibliographic Metadata
Y. Jing, R. Sipahi, and J. Martinez-Lorenzo, "Synchronized Multi-Directional FMCW mmWave Radar–Inertial Odometry: Robust Positioning and Autonomous Navigation Experiments for UAVs in Low-Light Indoor Environments," Drones, vol. 10, no. 2, p. 120, 2026. doi: 10.3390/drones10020120.

## 2. Problem Statement
"While the advent of the Global Navigation Satellite System (GNSS) has substantially improved outdoor positioning, indoor positioning still remains a formidable challenge due to signal obstructions and interference, particularly within confined, dark spaces." [p.2]

## 3. Motivation
"we propose a multi-Radar–Inertial Odometry framework (Hybrid-RIO)... which is specifically designed to operate in perceptually degraded and infrastructure-denied scenarios where conventional vision- or LiDAR-based sensing pipelines tend to fail." [p.3]

## 4. GPS-Denied Context
INDOOR
"in perceptually degraded, GPS-denied environments" [p.3]

## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: Hybrid-RIO (hybrid-filtered Radar-Inertial Odometry)
Category: HYBRID
"This paper presents a robust approach for achieving accurate indoor positioning and autonomous navigation of quadcopters through the fusion of multiple radars and an inertial measurement unit (IMU) named hybrid-filtered Radar–Inertial Odometry (Hybrid-RIO)." [p.1]

## 6. System Architecture
- Platform: quadcopter UAV
"quadcopter" [p.1]
- Sensors: four FMCW mmWave radars (TI IWR1443boost), IMU
"The Hybrid-RIO system integrates four-directional Frequency-Modulated Continuous-Wave (FMCW) millimeter-wave (mmWave) radars simultaneously with a high-performance IMU" [p.1]
- Compute: NVIDIA Jetson TX2 companion computer & PX4 board
"an NVIDIA TX2 companion computer is employed... fundamental flight-control tasks are managed by an onboard PX4 board with an embedded Microcontroller Unit (MCU)." [p.15]
- Communication: ROS / OFFBOARD flight mode
"published to an ROS topic... OFFBOARD flight mode" [p.15, p.19]

## 10. Stated Limitations
"Radar sensors... suffer from coarse spatial resolution, radar cross-section (RCS) fluctuations, multi-path reflections, mutual interference, and sparse detections... positioning estimates accumulate drift over time." [p.3, p.21]

## 11. Future Work
