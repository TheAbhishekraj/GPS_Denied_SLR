## 1. Bibliographic Metadata
X. Zhang, B. Xian, B. Zhao, and Y. Zhang, "Autonomous Flight Control of a Nano Quadrotor Helicopter in a GPS-Denied Environment Using On-Board Vision," *IEEE Transactions on Industrial Electronics*, vol. 62, no. 10, pp. 6392-6403, Oct. 2015.

## 2. Problem Statement
"For the aforementioned nano UAVs, most of them lack the autonomous flight ability in GPS-denied indoor and outdoor environments without using of external ground motion capture systems." [p.1].

## 3. Motivation
"To our best knowledge, few of previous works have demonstrated autonomous control ability of a quadrotor helicopter weighs less than 50 grams without the help of external ground motion capture system in GPS-denied indoor and outdoor environments." [p.1].

## 4. GPS-Denied Context
INDOOR (and outdoor)
"autonomous flight control development in GPS-denied environments" [p.1].

## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: RISE-based nonlinear robust control with modified PTAM
Category: HYBRID
"To increase the robustness with respect to external disturbance and modeling uncertainties, a nonlinear robust feedback methodology is used to formulate the flight controller." [p.2].

## 6. System Architecture
- Platform: "self-built nano quadrotor helicopter" [p.2]
- Sensors: "micro wireless monocular camera", "low-cost IMU", "barometer" [p.2]
- Compute: "customized 32-bits microcontroller board" and "laptop computer as ground station" [p.2]
- Communication: "telemetry radio device which weighs about 1.9 grams" [p.2]

## 18. Best Combination & Accuracy
### 18.1 Best sensor combination
"micro wireless monocular camera, low-cost IMU, barometer" [p.2].
### 18.2 Best algorithm
"RISE (robust integral of the signum of the error) feedback control" with "modified visual SLAM" [p.2].
### 18.3 Best accuracy
Indoor hover RMS, 0.039 m (z-direction), [p.8].
### 18.4 Baseline comparison at best configuration
"RISE based nonlinear position controller can achieve a much better control performance than the PID position controller." [p.11].
### 18.5 Conditions of best accuracy
"indoor hovering test" [p.8].
### 18.6 Trade-offs
"To improve the accuracy of position estimation, the IMU data are fused with the raw visual measurement using a Kalman filter" [p.2].
### 18.7 Generalization limits
"the main drawback of the optical flow based approach is that the integration of the translational velocity will lead to an unacceptable position estimation drift." [p.2].
