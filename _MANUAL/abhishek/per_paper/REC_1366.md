## 2. Problem Statement
"Without access to global coordinates, a UAV must rely on its inertial navigation system (INS). Positions in the INS frame are updated by integrating measurements from gyroscopes and accelerometers. Error in these measurements causes the INS frame to accumulate drift." [p.1]

## 3. Motivation
"The problem addressed in this paper is to localise a GPS-denied UAV, which we will call Agent B, with the assistance of a nearby GPS-enabled UAV, which we will call Agent A." [p.2]

## 4. GPS-Denied Context
MIXED
"GPS signals may be lost in urban environments and enemy controlled airspace (jamming)." [p.1]

## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: NOT_REPORTED (cooperative localisation using DOA measurements)
Category: COOPERATIVE
The paper studies cooperative localisation between a GPS-denied UAV and a GPS-enabled UAV using direction of arrival (DOA) measurements. A semidefinite programming approach with Orthogonal Procrustes is developed to solve for rotation and translation between frames.
"This paper studies a cooperative approach to localisation using DOA measurements." [p.1]
