## 1. Bibliographic Metadata
J. L. Paneque, J. R. Martinez-de Dios, and A. Ollero, "Multi-Sensor 6-DoF Localization For Aerial Robots In Complex GNSS-Denied Environments," in *2019 IEEE/RSJ International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems (IROS)*, Macao, China, 2019.

## 2. Problem Statement
"Our work is motivated by aerial robots that need robust and accurate 6-DOF pose estimations for autonomous safe navigation in complex, unstructured, GNSS-denied environments with high density of obstacles and potentially strong lighting changes." [p.1].

## 3. Motivation
"The need for robots autonomously navigating in more and more complex environments has motivated intense R&D efforts in making robot pose estimation more accurate and reliable." [p.1].

## 4. GPS-Denied Context
MIXED
"complex GNSS-denied industrial and urban scenarios" [p.7].

## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: Multi-sensor multi-hypothesis 6-DoF localization filter
Category: HYBRID
"This paper presents a robust multi-sensor multi-hypothesis 6-DoF localization filter for complex environments." [p.1].

## 6. System Architecture
- Platform: "DARIUS aerial robot" (custom-design hexarotor, MTOW 22 kg) [p.5]
- Sensors: "Velodyne HDL-32E, a ZED stereo camera, an IMU, a laser altimeter and an UWB ToF radio-range sensor" [p.5]
- Compute: "Intel NUC and a Nvidia Jetson TX2" [p.5]
- Communication: "Pixhawk autopilot via a MAVROS interface" [p.5]

## 7. Experimental Setup
- real_or_sim: REAL
- environment: "three different scenarios: School of Engineering of Sevilla (ETSI); a testing scenario near Seville (Karting); and at a road bridge (Bridge)" [p.5]
- trials: "more than 150 validation flights" [p.5]
- baselines: "LOAM, ORB-SLAM2" [p.6]
- dataset: "multi-sensor map" [p.1]
