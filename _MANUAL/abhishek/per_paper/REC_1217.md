## 10. Stated Limitations
"the proposed method is more suitable for UAV localization in scenarios where the flight distance is in the range of tens to hundreds of meters. In higher-altitude or lower-altitude scenarios, the image-registration-based localization method may not be applicable for UAV localization. Furthermore, the limitation of our method lies in its inability to achieve localization in low-texture environments, such as deserts and oceans." [p.16]
