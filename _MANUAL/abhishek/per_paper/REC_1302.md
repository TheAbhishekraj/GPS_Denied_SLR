## 1. Bibliographic Metadata
N. D. Almalis, G. Baris, N. Armenakis, and G. A. Tsihrintzis, "Federated Deep Reinforcement Learning for GNSS-Denied UAV Autonomy," *Electronics*, vol. 15, no. 14, art. no. 3064, 2026, doi: 10.3390/electronics15143064.

## 2. Problem Statement
"Achieving scalable swarm autonomy in Global Navigation Satellite System (GNSS)-denied and communication-constrained environments remains an open challenge at the intersection of robotics, distributed optimization, and reinforcement learning." [p.1]

## 3. Motivation
"This paper introduces a unified federated deep reinforcement learning architecture that enables GNSS-independent multi-UAV autonomy through the principled integration of multi-modal perception, decentralized policy optimization, energy-aware control, and edge-compliant inference." [p.1]

## 4. GPS-Denied Context
MIXED
"in modern operational environments, such as urban canyons with dense high-rise buildings, indoor infrastructures (e.g., industrial facilities, warehouses, tunnels), forested or densely vegetated areas, and regions affected by GNSS jamming or spoofing, satellite-based positioning is often degraded, unreliable, or completely unavailable" [p.2]

## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: Federated deep reinforcement learning architecture
Category: COOPERATIVE | OTHER
The framework formulates joint navigation and dynamic target tracking as a POMDP optimized via PPO over structured motion primitives. A communication-efficient federated learning mechanism enables distributed policy convergence without sharing raw data.
"The proposed framework formulates joint navigation and dynamic target tracking as a partially observable Markov decision process optimized via Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) over structured motion primitives." [p.1]

## 6. System Architecture
- Platform: DJI Matrice 100 quadrotor
- Sensors: 1080p RGB camera, FLIR thermal camera, Intel RealSense D435 depth sensor, 9-DOF IMU
- Compute: NVIDIA Jetson Xavier NX module
- Communication: federated learning, ROS
"Single-UAV field deployment trials were conducted using a DJI Matrice 100 quadrotor platform ... The UAV was equipped with a multi-modal sensing suite ... All onboard computation was performed using an NVIDIA Jetson Xavier NX module" [p.14]
