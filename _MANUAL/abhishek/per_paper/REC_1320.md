## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: Integrated RO/VO/magnetometer/barometer navigation system
Category: HYBRID
The system fuses forward velocities from Radar Odometry and enhanced Visual Odometry with IMU, barometer, and magnetometer measurements via an Extended Kalman Filter. The VO is enhanced with regression trees for drift compensation. The RO uses FMCW radar.
"This paper presents an integrated navigation system for Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs) in GNSS denied environments based on a Radar Odometry (RO) and an enhanced Visual Odometry (VO) to handle such challenges since the radar is immune against these issues." [p.1]
