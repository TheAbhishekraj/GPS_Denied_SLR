## 2. Problem Statement
"As there is generally no GNSS or external localization system available in real-world indoor environments, the UAVs need to rely on their exteroceptive sensors to localize themselves and perceive the environment." [p.1].

## 3. Motivation
"In this paper, we propose a cooperative navigation approach synergizing the advantages of both types of sensors." [p.2].

## 4. GPS-Denied Context
INDOOR
"we focus on the problem of cooperative navigation in GNSS-denied environments" [p.2].

## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: Cooperative navigation with 3D LiDAR relative localization
Category: COOPERATIVE
"A novel approach for cooperative navigation and guidance of a micro-scale aerial vehicle by an accompanying Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV) using 3D Light Detection and Ranging (LiDAR) relative localization is proposed" [p.1].

## 6. System Architecture
- Platform: "Primary UAV is built upon the Tarot 650 frame" [p.6]; "Secondary UAV carries the Intel NUC 10i7FNH" [p.6]
- Sensors: Primary: "Ouster OS0-128 3D LiDAR" [p.6]; Secondary: "Bluefox MLC200Wc camera with the DSL217 fisheye lens" [p.6]
- Compute: "Intel NUC 10i7FNK onboard computer" [p.6]
- Communication: "Nimbro network is employed for transporting the ROS topics and services over the wireless network." [p.7]
