## 2. Problem Statement
"Autonomous monitoring is essential for precision agriculture in greenhouses, yet deploying unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) in confined, GPS-denied environments remains limited by payload, power, and cost constraints." [p.1]

## 3. Motivation
"Integrated sensing and control platforms support the development of data-driven greenhouse management. Internet of Things (IoT) sensors and mobile monitoring systems have emerged as crucial technologies for greenhouse data acquisition." [p.2]

## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: Visual SLAM-based autonomous UAV system with edge computing
Category: VIO
"The proposed system employs a dual-link edge-computing architecture: a lightweight onboard controller handles flight control and sensor acquisition, while visual simultaneous localization and mapping (V-SLAM) is offloaded to an edge computer via the FPV video link." [p.1]

## 6. System Architecture
- Platform: custom quadcopter (34 cm × 31 cm × 26 cm, 1210 g)
  "Its compact dimensions (34 cm × 31 cm × 26 cm) and total mass of 1210 g are optimized for navigation in confined greenhouse spaces." [p.3]
- Sensors: front-facing FPV camera, side-mounted 4K action camera, optical flow sensor, rangefinder
  "The UAV platform integrates multiple sensing modalities, comprising two RGB cameras, an optical flow sensor, and a rangefinder." [p.3]
- Compute: Raspberry Pi CM4 (onboard) + Jetson Orin Nano (edge, ground station)
  "a 2 GB RAM version of the Raspberry Pi CM4... Computationally intensive tasks, such as V-SLAM pose estimation and mission planning algorithms, are offloaded to a Jetson Orin Nano" [p.4]
- Communication: 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi LAN (ROS 2 DDS), 5.8 GHz FPV video, 915 MHz telemetry
  "three communication channels separate data traffic by function: a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi LAN for ROS 2 DDS transport, a 5.8 GHz link for FPV video transmission, and a 915 MHz telemetry channel for dedicated flight monitoring." [p.5]

## 7. Experimental Setup
- real_or_sim: BOTH
  "the system was validated in both a controlled indoor environment and a simulated virtual setting" [p.8]
- environment: commercial greenhouse (7m × 10m), controlled indoor, simulation
  "Experiments were performed in a 7m × 10m greenhouse featuring three parallel racks, each containing five muskmelon plants" [p.11]

  "the system executed daily autonomous missions over 27 consecutive days in a commercial greenhouse" [p.1]

## 10. Stated Limitations
"Several limitations bound these results. All experiments were conducted in a single 7m × 10m greenhouse, and further investigation is required to assess the system's scalability across different greenhouse configurations. The endurance evaluation was handheld rather than in-flight. Marker boards on the racks contributed stable visual features to the SLAM map, though a marker-absent flight remained operational with limited quality degradation. Flower tracking MOTA of 49.1% with FN-dominant error produces systematic undercounting (mean −1.63 flowers), and compute throughput degraded from 9 to 6 Hz during sustained operation." [p.20]

## 11. Future Work
"Future research should focus on system improvement and broader validation. Extending deployment to diverse greenhouse environments and plant species would enhance applicability, while long-term monitoring studies across multiple growing seasons could establish reliability. Further refinement of control precision for complex missions and evaluation under diverse environmental conditions remain essential. Improving object tracking through motion-model-based re-identification... could address the current tracking limitations." [p.20]
