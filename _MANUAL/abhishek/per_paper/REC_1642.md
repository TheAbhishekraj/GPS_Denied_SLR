## 2. Problem Statement
"There are certain scenarios in which there is no GPS or there are structures and/or objects in the airspace that must be avoided and the UAV has to rely in its on- board sensors to localise itself." [p.1]

## 3. Motivation
"This type of scenarios present a challenge due to the uncertainties that are inherent to the perception and motion systems used by the UAV. An additional challenge is presented when a target must be found and followed due to the uncertainty in the target's motion." [p.1]

## 4. GPS-Denied Context
INDOOR
"a UAV flying in a three- dimensional GPS- denied space with obstacles" [p.1]

## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: POMDP-based target finding and tracking
Category: VISION_OBJECT | COOPERATIVE
"This mission is formulated as a Partially Observable Markov Decision Process (POMDP) and uses a modular framework that runs on the Robotic Operating System (ROS)." [p.1]
