## 3. Motivation

## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: Passive millimeter wave imager
Category: OTHER
"We present a passive millimeter wave imager for aerial vehicle navigation in degraded visual and GPS- denied environments. The sensor employs aperture synthesis imaging techniques with optical upconversion of millimeter wave signals and coherent reconstruction in the optical domain to enable a suitably small form factor." [p.1]
