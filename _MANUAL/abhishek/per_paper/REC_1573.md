## 2. Problem Statement
"However, accurate localization of these UAVs in maritime environments especially during landing operation on maritime vessels remains a challenge, particularly in GNSS denied areas." [p.1]

## 3. Motivation

## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: NOT_REPORTED (two visual-based localization methods)
Category: VISION_OBJECT | HYBRID
"The first method is used for estimating the UAV's position with respect to the vessel during the approach phase. It involves a visual UAV detection and tracking approach using the YOLO detector and OceanPlus tracker trained on a custom dataset. The UAV's position with respect to the vessel is estimated using stereo triangulation." [p.1]

## 6. System Architecture
- Platform: DJI Matrice M300
- Sensors: Stereo camera, downward camera
- Compute: Intel i7-10750H CPU, RTX 2080 GPU; NVIDIA Jetson TX2 for onboard
- Communication: NOT_REPORTED
"The proposed method achieves accurate positioning with errors below 10 cm during landing phases in a simulated environment." [p.1]

## 7. Experimental Setup
- real_or_sim: SIM
- environment: Simulated maritime environment in Unreal Engine
- trials: Multiple scenarios with different UAV speeds and camera resolutions
- baselines: ORB-SLAM3, OV2SLAM, VINS-Fusion, etc.
- dataset: Custom synthetic dataset
"A realistic simulated environment was created in Unreal Engine to gather synthetic dataset for evaluation of the position estimation method" [p.8]
