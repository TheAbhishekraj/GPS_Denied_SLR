## 2. Problem Statement
"However, robust localization, as a critical enabler for autonomy, is challenging in ever-changing, cluttered, GPS-denied indoor construction environments." [p.1]

## 3. Motivation
"To enable autonomous navigation of low-priced UAVs in indoor construction environments, this research aims to present an online global localization method that: (1) is inexpensive; (2) requires low computation and storage resources; (3) can be used by multiple platforms; and (4) can handle low-texture, ever-changing construction environments." [p.2]

## 4. GPS-Denied Context
INDOOR
"GPS signals are unreliable in indoor settings. Specifically, indoor construction sites are cluttered and dynamically changing environments, which causes many more challenges in UAVs' localization and autonomous navigation." [p.1]

## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: Tag-based visual-inertial localization
Category: HYBRID
The paper proposes a tag-based visual-inertial localization method for off-the-shelf UAVs with only a camera and an IMU. It uses an on-manifold extended Kalman filter to fuse inertial data and tag measurements (AprilTags). Tag locations are known in the BIM coordinate system.
"This research proposes a tag-based visual-inertial localization method for off-the-shelf UAVs with only a camera and an inertial measurement unit (IMU)." [p.1]

## 6. System Architecture
- Platform: Parrot Bebop2 quadrotor
- Sensors: Monocular camera, IMU
- Compute: Onboard computer (for control) and ground station (for SLAM/EKF)
- Communication: ROS, Wi-Fi
"A compact, inexpensive, commercially available UAV, Parrot Bebop2 [58], is used for method implementation." [p.2]
"The proposed tag-based localization method estimates the full 6-DoF global pose of a UAV with a minimum suite of onboard sensors, i.e., a monocular camera and an IMU, in real-time." [p.2]

## 7. Experimental Setup
- real_or_sim: BOTH
- environment: Laboratory and BIM-enabled simulation
- trials: Five experiments (straight line, mixed maneuvers, planar, 3D circular)
- baselines: Dead reckoning (IMU only), AprilTag package (tag only)
- dataset: NOT_REPORTED
"Experiments are designed and conducted in a laboratory setting (an arena equipped with a motion capture system to provide ground truth data) and a developed simulation environment to evaluate the method's performance." [p.2]

## 9. Ablation / Sensitivity
Comparison of on-manifold EKF vs Euler-angle EKF and tag-only vs fusion.
"To further investigate the impact of opting the proposed on-manifold formulation and tag measurement model, we estimate the same trajectory using our method and an Euler-angle-based EKF with direct tag measurements" [p.18]

## 10. Stated Limitations
"The proposed method is lightweight, inexpensive, and designed to address the technical and practical localization challenges in indoor construction environments." [p.17]
"Tags might not always be visible in an ever-changing construction setting, and the direct pose estimates from tags alone are noisy and suffer from ambiguity." [p.17]

## 11. Future Work
"In our future work, we are interested in feeding the tag-based estimates in a feedback control loop to autonomously fly, perform automatic data collection missions, and extend our validation in an actual construction setting." [p.18]
