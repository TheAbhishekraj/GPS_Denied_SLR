## 2. Problem Statement
"The challenge of collaboration between heterogeneous robots lies in their huge differences in perception, mobility and processing capabilities." [p.1]

## 3. Motivation
"Collaborative heterogeneous robots are expected to perform comprehensive perception, mapping and coordination in search and rescue scenarios." [p.1]

## 4. GPS-Denied Context
MIXED

## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: Continuous-discrete UAV-UGV collaborative mapping framework
Category: COOPERATIVE
"a novel collaborative UAV-UGV mapping framework is proposed in GNSS-denied and unknown environments. The key novelty of this work is the proposing of a unified framework to formulate the UAV-UGV collaborative mapping problem with a continuous-discrete model" [p.1]

## 6. System Architecture
- Platform: UAV and UGV
  "UAV and UGV share their local maps and generate a global fused map" [p.1]
- Sensors: UGV: 3D Velodyne LiDAR; UAV: stereo camera
  "UGV platform is equipped with the Intel Core i7-6700HQ CPU @ 2.60GHz CPU with a 3D Velodyne LiDAR. UAV Platform is equipped with the Intel NUC 6i7KYK CPU @ 2.60GHz CPU with a stereo camera." [p.5]
- Compute: UGV: Intel Core i7-6700HQ; UAV: Intel NUC 6i7KYK
  "UGV platform is equipped with the Intel Core i7-6700HQ CPU @ 2.60GHz CPU... UAV Platform is equipped with the Intel NUC 6i7KYK CPU @ 2.60GHz CPU" [p.5]
- Communication: Long range Wi-Fi
  "The communication between robots is established by long range Wi-Fi with limited bandwidth." [p.5]

## 7. Experimental Setup
- real_or_sim: REAL
  "Real world experiments in GNSS-denied and unstructured scenarios are investigated" [p.4]
- environment: NTU Rainforest, Two-Level Building

- trials: 2 scenarios
  "including NTU Rainforest (Sec. V-B), Two-Level Building (Sec. V-C)" [p.4]
