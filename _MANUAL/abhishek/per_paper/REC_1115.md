## 2. Problem Statement
"Vision-based Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV) localization systems have been extensively investigated for Global Navigation Satellite System (GNSS)-denied environments. However, existing retrieval-based approaches face limitations in dataset availability and persistent challenges including suboptimal real-time performance, memory inefficiency for onboard deployment, environmental sensitivity, and limited generalization capability" [p.1]

## 3. Motivation
"Practical large-scale deployments demand continuous flights at variable altitudes with strict onboard computational constraints, necessitating robust, real-time 4-DoF pose estimation." [p.1]

## 10. Stated Limitations
"First, severe class imbalance — particularly when vegetation exceeds 50% — flattens the posterior distribution and slows convergence. Second, extreme domain shifts such as heavy snow or highly unstructured rural terrain violate the urban structural priors, causing matching failures. Third, the current implementation depends on GPU acceleration; CPU-only execution of SegFormer inference and matrix operations is approximately 15× slower" [p.14]

## 11. Future Work
"future research will proceed along three main directions. We will develop domain-adaptive semantic weighting schemes to extend robustness to rural and unstructured environments, and design uncertainty-aware mechanisms that mitigate segmentation noise. Furthermore, we aim to reduce reliance on continuous IMU input by integrating lightweight global re-localization modules" [p.14]
