## 2. Problem Statement
"This GPS waypoint navigation does however become dangerous in environments where the GPS signal is faulty or is only present in some places and when the airspace is filled with obstacles." [p.1]

## 3. Motivation
"UAVs use ground control stations to plan a path to a goal before flying, using Global Positioning System (GPS) sensors as their source of localisation [6-10]. However, reliable GPS localisation is not always available due to occlusions or the absence of the satellite signal." [p.1]

## 5. Proposed Method
UBNAV (UAV Uncertainty-Based Navigation); OTHER
"we developed UAV Uncertainty-Based Navigation (UBNAV) for UAVs to navigate in GPS-denied environments by executing a policy that takes into account different sources of uncertainty." [p.2]

## 6. System Architecture
- Platform: low cost multi-rotor UAV
"The system uses a low cost multi-rotor UAV flying in a 3D indoor space with no GPS signal available." [p.2]
- Sensors: downward-looking camera, front-looking camera, IMU, magnetometer, ultrasonic, barometric pressure
"The UAV on- board navigation system calculates forward \(V_{f}\) and lateral \(V_{l}\) velocities using optical flow obtained from the downward- looking camera. The UAV obtains the yaw angle \(\Psi_{a}\) from the IMU and magnetometer readings and the altitude \(z_{a}\) from on- board ultrasonic and barometric pressure sensors..." [p.5]
- Compute: onboard ROS modules
"All modules execute in parallel in different threads with different update rates." [p.4]
- Communication: NOT_REPORTED
