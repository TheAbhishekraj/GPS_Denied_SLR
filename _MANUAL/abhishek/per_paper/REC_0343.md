## 2. Problem Statement
"Different from conventional systems, this paper adopts a day camera and a thermal camera to conduct flocking using visual sensing in various illumination with autonomous navigation capabilities." [p.1].

## 3. Motivation

Category: COOPERATIVE
"This paper presents a sophisticated vision-aided flocking system for unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs), which is able to operate in GPS-denied unknown environments" [p.1].
