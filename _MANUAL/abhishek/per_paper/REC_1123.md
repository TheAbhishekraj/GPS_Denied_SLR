## 2. Problem Statement
"Traditional Global Navigation Satellite System (GNSS)-based positioning schemes are vulnerable to signal interference and spoofing attacks, highlighting the importance of visual scene matching as a robust solution for anti-jamming localization. However, this technology faces challenges such as sensitivity to perspective variations and trajectory discontinuities." [p.1]

## 3. Motivation
"IN THE intelligent era driven by the Internet of Things (IoT), uncrewed aerial vehicles (UAVs) act as critical nodes within the integrated air-ground-space IoT architecture, with applications in smart city management, emergency response, intelligent logistics, and other domains becoming increasingly prevalent." [p.1]

### 18.5 Conditions of best accuracy
"SEULoc01 sequence (pitch angle -90°)" [p.12]

### 18.6 Trade-offs
"when the UAV camera is vertically downward (pitch angle of −90°), the homography-based method slightly outperforms the proposed method. This is because the estimated elevation in our method may not fully match the true elevation, introducing some error in absolute geopose estimation." [p.13]

### 18.7 Generalization limits
"DEM tends to introduce large errors in urban scenes due to their low resolution [17], which undermines their suitability for high-precision localization. Although DSM provides high-resolution elevation data, their reliance on prior data acquisition using UAVs or other platforms incurs substantial costs" [p.3]
