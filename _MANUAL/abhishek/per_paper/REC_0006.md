## 2. Problem Statement
"Reliable autonomous navigation of Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs) in GPS-denied environments remains a significant challenge due to signal attenuation, spoofing, and jamming." [p.1].

## 3. Motivation
"Indoor environments such as warehouses, underground facilities, and dense urban areas suffer from severe GPS signal attenuation due to structural occlusions, while outdoor scenarios including forests and open terrains are vulnerable to GPS spoofing, jamming, and atmospheric disturbances. These conditions result in unreliable localization, motivating navigation solutions that do not rely solely on satellite-based positioning [1]." [p.1].

## 4. GPS-Denied Context
MIXED
"Indoor environments such as warehouses and industrial facilities suffer from GPS degradation caused by structural occlusions, while outdoor scenarios including forests and flat terrains lack reliable localization cues." [p.1].

## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: Multi-sensor fusion framework with VSLAM and 2D digital twin
Category: HYBRID
"The proposed approach employs a dual-strategy navigation framework: (i) Visual Simultaneous Localization and Mapping (VSLAM) with 3D point cloud generation for structured indoor environments, and (ii) a VSLAM-based 2D digital twin representation for outdoor environments, integrating visual data with inertial and altitude measurements." [p.1].

## 6. System Architecture
- Platform: "A custom-built quadcopter platform, GATHI (GPS-denied Aerial Tracking and Homing Intelligence), shown in Fig. 1, was developed using a Holybro X500 V2 carbon-fiber frame." [p.3]
- Sensors: "The ZED 2i stereo camera [11] serves as the primary sensing modality for visual-inertial odometry and 3D mapping." [p.3]
- Compute: "an in-house developed UAV platform" [p.1]; onboard via flight controller and companion processing
- Communication: "provides a web-based GUI for real-time visualization and command-based navigation through bidirectional communication with the UAV" [p.1]

## 10. Stated Limitations
"Despite its effectiveness indoors, VSLAM-based navigation relying on dense 3D point clouds is computationally expensive and less suitable for large-scale outdoor environments, while also being sensitive to lighting variations and altitude changes [8], [10]." [p.4].

## 11. Future Work
"Future work will focus on developing a unified digital twin framework that integrates complementary sensing modalities, including thermal imaging and electromagnetic-wave-based sensors, to provide robust and lighting-independent navigation cues." [p.4].
