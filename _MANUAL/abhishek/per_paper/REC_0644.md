## 2. Problem Statement
"their dependence on Global Positioning System (GPS) localization is often compromised in forested areas, under bridges, or above highly dynamic water surfaces." [p.1]

## 3. Motivation
"Rivers pose unique challenges due to their winding paths, dynamic water surfaces, and surrounding vegetation, which can lead to signal loss, causing UAVs to drift, lose control, or fail missions." [p.1]

## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: DDM-enhanced VIO with NeuFlow v2 and KF
Category: VIO
"This paper presents a robust real- time visual- inertial navigation framework that integrates neural network- based optical flow estimation (NeuFlow v2), the Dynamic Divided Method (DDM), and inertial measurement unit (IMU) data through a Kalman Filter (KF)." [p.1]
