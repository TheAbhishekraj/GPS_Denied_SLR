## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: NOT_REPORTED (crowd-sensing data fusion with FMCW radar)
Category: HYBRID
Integrates crowd-sensed data fusion to counter INS drift during GNSS outages. Uses a micro FMCW radar for ground scatterer reflections, processed via FFT to generate range-Doppler map. Forward velocity estimation fused with IMU, magnetometer, barometer using EKF and PF.
"Integrating crowd-sensed data fusion to counter inertial navigation system (INS) drift during GNSS signal outages, the proposed method exploits diverse estimators to enhance its efficacy." [p.1]
