## 2. Problem Statement
"However, further progress in UAV-satellite image registration and alignment remains constrained by two fundamental issues. First, existing learning-based local feature matching methods are typically developed using training data with explicit geometric correspondences... In UAV-satellite registration, however, explicit camera correspondences are generally unavailable, which limits the availability of cross-domain supervision for training local matching models. Second, existing UAV-satellite datasets often exhibit varying degrees of geospatial misalignment among UAV images, GNSS ground truth, and satellite images." [p.1]

## 3. Motivation
"To address these issues, this work investigates how dense correspondences produced by dense matchers can be converted into trainable supervisory signals in the absence of explicit camera correspondences and pixel-level annotations." [p.1]

## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: Co-Lo
Category: VISION_OBJECT
The paper presents Co-Lo, a calibrated UAV-satellite dataset. It introduces a weakly supervised cross-domain adaptation strategy that converts dense correspondences into pseudo-labels for local descriptor learning on repeatable keypoints.
"To address these issues, we present Co-Lo, a calibrated UAV-satellite dataset." [p.1]

## 10. Stated Limitations
"Although the weakly supervised cross-domain adaptation strategy improves descriptor training by establishing keypoint correspondences, several limitations remain. Correspondence Noise: When using the sampled RoMa correspondences as pseudo-labels, the average reprojection error is approximately 4px." [p.10]

## 11. Future Work
"In future work, we aim to establish more cross-domain keypoint correspondences for stable matching. Reducing the reprojection error will be crucial for enabling joint training of the keypoint detector and descriptor." [p.10]
