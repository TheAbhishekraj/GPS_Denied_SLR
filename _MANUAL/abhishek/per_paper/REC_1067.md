## 2. Problem Statement
"Localization techniques classically rely on a combination of Inertial Measurement Unit (IMU) sensors and Global Positioning System (GPS). However, GPS can be costly, and is prone to disconnection due to jamming and dependency on the sensor's environment." [p.4]

## 3. Motivation
"In recent years, the utilization of Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs) has witnessed a remarkable surge, finding their way into an extensive range of applications." [p.4]

## 4. GPS-Denied Context
MIXED
"GPS can be susceptible to disconnection and inaccuracies due to factors such as jamming and environmental conditions [6]." [p.13]

## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: Texel camera-based GPS-denied navigation with ESEKF
Category: HYBRID
"This thesis presents a localization system for UAVs, specifically designed for small UAVs to be used in a local area, by using localization estimation algorithms." [p.4]

## 6. System Architecture
- Platform: sUAV (quadcopter)
  "Fig. 2.2: sUAV with the mounted texel camera payload." [p.18]
- Sensors: IMU, LiDAR unit, EO camera
  "The texel camera contains a LiDAR unit, an electro-optical (EO) camera, and an Inertial Navigation System (INS)." [p.17]
- Compute: onboard and ground station
  "The server used in this experiment was equipped with an AMD 5995WX processor" [p.11]
- Communication: wireless
  "Wireless communication between the UAV edge device and a base station enables real-time inference." [p.1]

## 7. Experimental Setup
- real_or_sim: REAL
  "To acquire data for GPS-denied navigation, two flights were documented" [p.34]
- environment: urban (Utah State University campus)
  "The sUAV traversed the Utah State University campus, initially maintaining a straight trajectory at speeds of 15 m/s." [p.34]

  "The first flight covered a recorded and estimated distance of 3.79783 kilometers, while the second path spanned 4.76594 kilometers." [p.34]

  "The dataset used in this study was acquired from Utah State Universities campus" [p.38]

## 18. Best Combination & Accuracy
### 18.1 Best sensor combination
"the texel camera contains a LiDAR unit, an electro-optical (EO) camera, and an Inertial Navigation System (INS)" [p.17]

### 18.2 Best algorithm
"Error-State Extended Kalman Filtering (ESEKF)" [p.1]
"the LM algorithm is employed to minimize errors in the 3D-to-2D projection and camera pose" [p.33]

### 18.3 Best accuracy
Average error 4.18373 meters (combined) [p.34]

### 18.4 Baseline comparison at best configuration
"This error averages out to 4.18373 meters, which is within the range of U.S civilian based GPS accuracy of 3 to 5 meters" [p.34]

### 18.5 Conditions of best accuracy
"Both flights followed similar paths, yet yielded distinct datasets. The sUAV traversed the Utah State University campus, initially maintaining a straight trajectory at speeds of 15 m/s." [p.34]

### 18.6 Trade-offs
"While the LM algorithm is one of the most effective optimization processes for this research, the computations are costly with a complexity of O(n³) [28]." [p.39]

### 18.7 Generalization limits
"Surveyed areas with limited features in the imagery may present challenges for texel registration, as it could struggle to identify image features." [p.38]
