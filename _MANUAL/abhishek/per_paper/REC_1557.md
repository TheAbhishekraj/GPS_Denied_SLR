## 2. Problem Statement
"The growth of position errors in a GNSS- denied condition is such that the navigation methods are not reliable." [p.1]

## 3. Motivation
"A group of flying robots (FRs) is more robust than a single one, in which more complex missions can be performed [1]." [p.1]

## 4. GPS-Denied Context
MIXED
"GNSS- denied condition" [p.1]

## 5. Proposed Method
Drag-force-based model-based cooperative navigation; COOPERATIVE
"a model- based cooperative navigation system for a group of quadrotors, while no absolute position data is accessible, is proposed." [p.1]

## 6. System Architecture
- Platform: group of quadrotors
"a group of quadrotors" [p.1]
- Sensors: IMU, UWB (for relative range and bearing)
"each quadrotor is equipped with an IMU to perform the DR process (propagation) and relative sensors to measure the interagent relative range and bearing angles." [p.2]
- Compute: central EKF (ground station or one agent)
"the update step is performed on a single central EKF" [p.5]
- Communication: UWB for relative position data
"measuring and communicating the relative position data, including relative distance and bearing angle between the agents." [p.5]
