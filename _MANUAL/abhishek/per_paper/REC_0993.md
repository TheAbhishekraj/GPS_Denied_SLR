## 2. Problem Statement
"One of the most challenging tasks for a flying robot is to autonomously navigate between target locations quickly and reliably while avoiding obstacles in its path, and with little to no a-priori knowledge of the operating environment." [p.1]

## 3. Motivation
"real-world environments are dynamic, partially-known and often GPS-denied. Therefore, there is need to push further on developing fully autonomous navigation systems that rely on onboard sensing only" [p.2]

## 4. GPS-Denied Context
INDOOR
"our proposed solution can deliver fast and robust aerial robot autonomous navigation in cluttered, GPS-denied environments." [p.2]

## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: SVO-based visual odometry with UKF and local mapping
Category: VIO
"We describe the system design and software architecture of our proposed solution, and showcase how all the distinct components can be integrated to enable smooth robot operation." [p.1]
