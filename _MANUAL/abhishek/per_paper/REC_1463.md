## 1. Bibliographic Metadata
X. Zhu, F. Vanegas, and F. Gonzalez, "Decentralised Multi-UAV Cooperative Searching Multi-Target in Cluttered and GPS-Denied Environments," in *2022 IEEE Aerospace Conference (AERO)*, 2022, doi: 10.1109/AERO53065.2022.9843665.

## 2. Problem Statement
"Utilising a team of UAVs to efficiently and cooperatively locate a target in congested and GPS-denied environments remains a difficult challenge." [p.2]

## 3. Motivation
"In order to provide a possible solution for these applications, this paper presents a multi-UAV system that can be utilised for a team of UAVs to autonomously navigate, search and detect multiple targets in a cluttered and GPS-denied environment." [p.2]

## 4. GPS-Denied Context
INDOOR | URBAN_CANYON
"Examples of such environments include search and rescue inside buildings or collapsed building scenarios, urban or natural canyons, in which the GPS signal is limited or blocked." [p.2]

## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: Int-Dec-POMDP
Category: COOPERATIVE
The paper presents a multi-UAV system using a decentralised framework based on Decentralised Partially Observable Markov Decision Processes (Dec-POMDP) to formulate decision-making for autonomous navigation, search and detection of multiple targets in cluttered and GPS-denied environments.
"The multi-UAV system uses a decentralised framework based on Decentralised Partially Observable Markov Decision Processes (Dec-POMDP) to formulate the decision-making process considering uncertainties in the environments and sensing." [p.2]

## 6. System Architecture
- Platform: Small-size quadcopter UAVs
- Sensors: Downward facing cameras, multi-orientation range sensors
- Compute: Onboard computer
- Communication: WiFi
"The UAVs in the proposed decentralised MAS use the identical small-size quad-copter platform with onboard downward facing cameras and multi-orientation range sensors." [p.4]

## 7. Experimental Setup
- real_or_sim: SIM
- environment: Gazebo and ROS simulation, two scenarios with obstacles
- trials: 25 runs per scenario, 20 runs for success rate, 15 runs for scalability
- baselines: Different reward value sets (Set A, B, C)
- dataset: NOT_REPORTED
"The system was tested in several cluttered and GPS-denied environments simulated in Gazebo and ROS interface." [p.2]
