## 2. Problem Statement
"High-altitude flights using VIO concepts for pose estimation face specific challenges of their own related to operation in conditions of reduced visual features and environmental variability, joined with precise sensor fusion." [p.2]

## 3. Motivation
"Visual inertial odometry (VIO) has gained considerable attention in the past few years as a means of high-accuracy autonomous navigation, especially in GNSS- denied environments." [p.1]

## 4. GPS-Denied Context
MIXED
"The proposed system is validated on a custom dataset obtained from a 15 km long-range flight via a conventional fixed-wing UAV in a desert-like environment." [p.1]

## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: FMSCKF with magnetometer integration
Category: VIO
"The method builds upon the Fast Multi-State Constraint Kalman Filter (FMSCKF) framework, introducing key innovations such as a dynamic feature extraction strategy, specialized handling of data for slow-motion imagery, selective feature utilization during filter updates, and magnetometer integration for improved heading estimation." [p.1]
