## 2. Problem Statement
"The traditional navigation mode of the Inertial Navigation System (INS) assisted by GNSS is greatly challenged, since the GNSS is easily interfered with by complex external environments or human factors [1,2]." [p.1]

## 3. Motivation
"Aimed at improving the navigation accuracy of the fixed- wing UAVs in GNSS- dened environments, this paper proposes an algorithm of nongravitational acceleration estimation based on airspeed and IMU sensors" [p.1]

## 4. GPS-Denied Context
MIXED
"The algorithm is actually deployed on low- cost fixed- wing UAVs and is compared with ACF, EKF, and NCF by using real flight data." [p.1]

## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: Airspeed-aided state estimation with nonlinear complementary filter
Category: HYBRID
"we further establish the mapping relationship between vehicle nongravitational acceleration and the vehicle attitude misalignment angle and transform it into the attitude angle rate deviation through the nonlinear complementary filtering model for real- time compensation." [p.1]
