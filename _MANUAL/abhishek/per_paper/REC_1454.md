## 2. Problem Statement
"To address the challenge of visual localisation for UAVs operating in complex terrains under Global Navigation Satellite System (GNSS)-denied environments, this paper proposes an improved adaptive gradient-damped Newton approach to mitigate the trade-off between terrain non-convexity and computational real-time performance." [p.1]

## 3. Motivation
"monocular visual geolocalisation technology, which relies on matching airborne imagery with digital elevation models (DEM), has emerged as a research focus in the navigation field because of its robust anti-interference capability and passive detection characteristics" [p.1]

## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: Adaptive Gradient-Damped Newton Method
Category: VISION_OBJECT
The paper proposes an improved adaptive gradient-damped Newton method for monocular visual geolocalisation. It incorporates a terrain-gradient-based dynamic step-size adjustment mechanism and a tightly coupled vision-geometry framework.
