## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: VINS with dense optical flow velocity estimation
Category: VIO
"This paper presents the Visual-Inertial Navigation System (VINS) developed that utilises sparse and dense Optical Flow patterns within an Extended Kalman Filter (EKF) framework for autonomous navigation in GNSS denied environments. The principal novelty is the use of dense Optical Flow measurements to estimate the velocity of the UAV." [p.1]
