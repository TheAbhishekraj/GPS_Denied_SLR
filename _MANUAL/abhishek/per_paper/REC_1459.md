## 2. Problem Statement
"However, the drone inspection of tunnel surface still faces many challenges. Compared with the open-air condition, the dark environment in confined underground tunnel space without GPS positioning signal is not suitable for UAV flight, owning to the decrease of drone manipulation precision and automatic flight interference from intrados." [p.3]

## 3. Motivation
"To this end, this paper proposed a UAV automatic data acquisition method with the help of visual positioning, which further developed the Mobile SDK of DJI UAV to control the UAV flight following a designated trajectory." [p.3]

## 4. GPS-Denied Context
UNDERGROUND
"the dark environment in confined underground tunnel space without GPS positioning signal is not suitable for UAV flight" [p.3]

## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: MSDK (Mobile Software Development Kit)
Category: VISION_OBJECT
The paper develops a MSDK for drone using visual positioning and predefined controlling code, enabling the drone to automatically fly along a designated sinusoidal route whilst taking videos and images of the tunnel surface.
"In order to facilitate data acquisition, this article developed a mobile software development kit (MSDK) for drone using visual positioning and predefined controlling code" [p.2]

## 6. System Architecture
- Platform: DJI Mavic 2 Enterprise Dual (M2ED)
- Sensors: Camera, spotlight
- Compute: Onboard
- Communication: NOT_REPORTED
