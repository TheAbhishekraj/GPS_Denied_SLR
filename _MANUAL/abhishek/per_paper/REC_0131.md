## 2. Problem Statement
"achieving precise flight control, efficient obstacle avoidance, and reliable real-time decision-making remains challenging, particularly in GPS-denied or cluttered environments." [p.1].

## 3. Motivation
"To address these issues, this work integrates a digital twin (DT) framework with the UAV's control and perception system, creating a synchronized virtual replica that continuously mirrors the drone's physical state." [p.1].

## 10. Stated Limitations
"First, the evaluation is based on an indoor, visually controlled environment where ArUco markers provide stable metric references; performance in outdoor or partially textured environments may exhibit higher localization drift. Second, the trajectory reconstruction is derived from video-based detection rather than direct onboard flight logs... Third, the dynamics modeled in the DT do not incorporate full aerodynamic disturbances or actuator saturation effects" [p.4].

## 11. Future Work
"Future work will integrate onboard sensing, more detailed quadrotor dynamics, and hardware-in-the-loop testing to validate the framework across larger scale and higher dynamic scenarios." [p.4].
