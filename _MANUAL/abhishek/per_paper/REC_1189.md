## 2. Problem Statement

## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: ROG-1 and ROG-2 autonomous UAV systems
Category: HYBRID
"We provide two different hardware and software solutions for fully autonomous UAVs, addressing, in particular, indoor GNSS-denied navigation and collision avoidance in cluttered environments." [p.2]

## 6. System Architecture
- Platform: ROG-1 and ROG-2 coaxial octocopters
  "It is again a coaxial octocopter (Fig.8), codename ROG-2" [p.7]
- Sensors: ZED stereo camera, OpenMV H7 Plus, TeraRanger Tower Evo, Lidar-Lite v3, IMU
  "The sensor suite is composed by a forward-looking Stereolabs ZED stereo camera... a Lidar-Lite v3 laser range finder and two OpenMV H7 Plus monocular cameras" [p.7]
- Compute: NVIDIA Jetson TX2 and Xavier NX
  "NVIDIA Jetson TX2 companion computer" [p.3]; "more powerful NVIDIA Jetson Xavier NX" [p.7]
- Communication: ROS, TCP, PTZ camera, ground control station
  "ROS framework was still used, thanks also to the capabilities of publishing and receiving messages through TCP transport" [p.7]

## 18. Best Combination & Accuracy
### 18.1 Best sensor combination
"forward-looking Stereolabs ZED stereo camera" and "downward-looking OpenMV H7 Plus monocular cameras" [p.7]

### 18.2 Best algorithm
"ZED-VO" [p.17]

### 18.3 Best accuracy
Mean error: 0.078 m (ZED-VO) [p.17]

### 18.4 Baseline comparison at best configuration
"ZED-VO 0.078 m vs RTAB-Map 0.175 m vs ORB-SLAM2 0.184 m" [p.17]

### 18.5 Conditions of best accuracy
"circular trajectory at constant altitude, while keeping fixed yaw" [p.14]

### 18.6 Trade-offs
"the ArUco tracker... continuously searches for markers... over three different images... solution employed up to 90% of the available CPU." [p.21]

### 18.7 Generalization limits
"on one side of the field, a uniform black textureless wall was present. In that area, the lack of features made the visual odometry output quality very low." [p.17]
