## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: Opecker (tethered UAV system)
Category: HYBRID
The paper presents a tethered UAV (TUAV) for structural integrity assessment of underground stone mine pillars. The TUAV works with a UGV (Rhino) that hosts batteries, a self-leveled landing platform, and tether management system. It uses LiDAR for 3D mapping.
"This paper presents a state-of-the-art tethered unmanned aerial vehicle (TUAV) for structural integrity assessment of underground stone mine pillars." [p.1]

## 6. System Architecture
- Platform: Custom quadrotor (Holybro X500 frame)
- Sensors: LiDAR (Intel RealSense L515), tracking camera (Intel RealSense T265), Garmin LidarLite v3/v4, IMU (Pixhawk 4), UWB (DWM1001)
- Compute: Intel NUC 11 with Intel Core i5-1135G7, Arduino UNO R3
- Communication: ROS, WiFi
"The hardware includes the tether management system, designed to control the tension of the tether, and the tether perception system, which provides information that can be used for localization and landing in global navigation satellite systems (GNSS)-denied environments." [p.1]
