## 2. Problem Statement
"Presently, Global Navigation Satellite Systems (GNSS) function as the predominant positioning methodology for Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs). However, in complex electromagnetic environments or adversarial scenarios, GNSS signals are highly susceptible to blockage, interference, or spoofing attacks. This hinders UAVs from acquiring precise positional data, jeopardizing flight safety and exerting a substantial negative influence on mission success rates." [p.2]

## 3. Motivation
"Consequently, achieving high-precision positioning for UAVs in GNSS-denied environments has become a critical technology and research focus for ensuring safe flight operations and mission success." [p.2]

## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: Graph-optimization-based tightly coupled multi-source positioning
Category: HYBRID
"To address UAV positioning in GNSS-denied environments, this study proposes a tightly coupled multi-source positioning method that integrates IMU, vision, and UWB." [p.2]

## 6. System Architecture
- Platform: Drone platform
- Sensors: IMU, visual module, UWB ranging module
- Compute: Onboard computing platform
- Communication: UWB radio
"To do so, the system shown in Figure 8 was mounted onto a drone platform, flight trajectories were generated, and a comparative analysis was conducted with the actual trajectories." [p.18]
"The IMU uses the ADIS16505-2BMLZ model; the visual module uses the MER2-502-79U3M industrial camera; and the UWB component uses a customised ranging module and base station system." [p.17]

## 7. Experimental Setup
- real_or_sim: BOTH
- environment: Typical flight image sequences and public datasets (EuRoC, HPatches)
- trials: Two flight trajectories; 5 EuRoC sequences
- baselines: VINS-Fusion, ORB-SLAM3
- dataset: EuRoC, HPatches
"One uses typical flight image sequences captured by actual UAVs... The other experiment uses the publicly available HPatches dataset [37-39] for validation. Experiments were conducted using the EuRoC public dataset [40] to validate the effectiveness of the proposed IMU/vision/UWB fusion graph optimization localization method and to compare it with the VINS-Fusion [41] and ORB-SLAM3 [42] systems."
