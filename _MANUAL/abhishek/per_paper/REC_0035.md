## 2. Problem Statement
"However, a large variety of applications for MAVs would rely on the autonomous capabilities in GPS-denied environments to handle difficult, dirty, dangerous jobs. A typical scenario is a post-disaster search and rescue mission, in which MAVs must fly among buildings with GPS blocking or even inside buildings without any GPS signal." [p.1].

## 3. Motivation
"To this end, a fully functional system incorporating with robust guidance, navigation and control algorithms is in high demand. What's more, an efficient software architecture and mission management structure is of vital importance." [p.1].

## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: Integrated hardware/software with robust state estimation and mission management
Category: LIDAR (2D laser-based SLAM)
"A micro aerial vehicle (MAV) equipped with 2D laser scanner, camera, on-board computer and flight controller is designed and constructed to realize our flight missions. The 2D laser-based localization and Kalman filter-based sensor fusion provide a robust state estimation for the MAV." [p.1].

## 6. System Architecture
- Platform: "The hexarotor in Fig. 1 is designed to be a lightweight and versatile platform." [p.1]
- Sensors: "The flight control module includes various sensors such as gyroscopes, accelerometers, magnetometers, to measure the status of the MAV. Height is monitored by a TeraRanger One, which is a Time of Flight (TOF) distance sensor. A Hokuyo laser scanner is used for SLAM in the horizontal direction. A downward-facing camera is used for vision based detection and guidance." [p.2]
- Compute: "we use a small-size 80g Upboard with Intel Atom x5 z8350 processor as on-board mission computer." [p.2]
