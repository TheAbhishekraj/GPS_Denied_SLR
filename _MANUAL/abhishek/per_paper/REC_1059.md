## 1. Bibliographic Metadata
Zahran, S.; Moussa, A.; El-Sheimy, N. Enhancing Navigation of Autonomous Drones in GNSS Denied Environment Using Hall Effect Sensor as Flying Odometer and Vehicle Dynamic Model. *ISPRS Int. J. Geo-Inf.* **2019**, *8*, 169.

## 2. Problem Statement
"However, the navigation performance of these small drones deteriorates quickly when the GNSS signals are lost, due to accumulated errors of the low-cost INS that is typically used in these drones." [p.1]

## 3. Motivation
"Due to the rapid increase in the applications employing drones in our daily life, to save time, effort, cost, and perform dangerous tasks, the requirement for a versatile drone that can fulfill its task in different condition/situations is essential." [p.1]

## 4. GPS-Denied Context
MIXED
"Relying on GNSS alone to bound the INS drift is not an adequate solution, as GNSS signals are susceptible to jamming, spoofing, multipath, or blockage." [p.1]

## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: Air-Odo / VDM / INS integration
Category: HYBRID
"This paper presents a novel unconventional method, to enhance the navigation of autonomous drones in GNSS denied environment, through a new utilization of hall effect sensor to act as flying odometer 'Air-Odo' and vehicle dynamic model (VDM) for heading estimation." [p.1]

## 6. System Architecture
- Platform: quadcopter
  "The drone utilized in this work is quadcopter drone, which is composed of four brushless dc motors that control the drone motions, according to their speeds." [p.5]
- Sensors: Hall effect sensor, MEMS-based IMU (MPU-9250), U-Blox GNSS receiver
  "The experiment uses the 3DR Solo quadcopter equipped with MEMS-based low-cost IMU (MPU-9250) and U-Blox GNSS receiver. Air-Odo was attached to the top of the quadcopter" [p.9]
- Compute: onboard
  "All the sensors measurements are fused through a loosely coupled EKF scheme." [p.8]
- Communication: NOT_REPORTED

## 7. Experimental Setup
- real_or_sim: BOTH
  "The proposed approach was verified with an indoor experiment (wind tunnel), and outdoor environment experiment through a real flight." [p.16]
- environment: indoor wind tunnel and outdoor open field
  "an indoor experiment was conducted in a wind tunnel as shown in Figure 8." [p.10]

- trials: "Different GNSS signal outages, ranging from 60 to 185 s, were carried out" [p.16]
- baselines: INS standalone (dead-reckoning)
  "the navigation solution of INS during GNSS signal outage of 65 s has been estimated using EKF in prediction mode without any updates." [p.11]

## 18. Best Combination & Accuracy
### 18.1 Best sensor combination
"fusion between two approaches: (a) the first approach presents an unconventional utilization of contactless rotary magnetic encoder (Hall Effect sensor)... and (b) the second approach utilizes the VDM as a constraint to estimate the heading of the drone" [p.2]

### 18.2 Best algorithm
"All the sensors measurements are fused through a loosely coupled EKF scheme." [p.8]

### 18.3 Best accuracy
65 s outage, Air-Odo/VDM/INS: North RMSE 1.35 m, East RMSE 1.41 m [p.13]

### 18.4 Baseline comparison at best configuration
"The results showed that the navigation system 2D RMSE was enhanced by more than 99% when compared to the INS navigation solution (dead-reckoning mode)." [p.16]

### 18.5 Conditions of best accuracy
"Different GNSS signal outages, ranging from 60 to 185 s, were carried out" [p.16]
"with different velocities varying between 3 and 5 m/s for a total time of 350 s" [p.11]

### 18.6 Trade-offs
"the Air-Odo weight is less than 50 grams with a very low power consumption 150 mW/hr, low-cost (~20 dollars and can be cheaper for mass production). While VDM does not require any additional space, cost, or weight as the feedback from the UAV actuators were utilized." [p.16]

### 18.7 Generalization limits
"Although the presence of wind gust caused errors in UAV velocity estimation" [p.15]
"the solution still suffers from heading drift as the heading is not strongly observable during velocity updates." [p.12]
