## 2. Problem Statement
"Global Navigation Satellite System (GNSS) provides absolute positioning for aerial vehicles in open environments; however, underground settings block these signals entirely, forcing the system to rely solely on onboard sensing for localization." [p.1].

## 3. Motivation
"Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs) offer a safe and efficient solution for inspection and mapping in underground environments such as tunnels and mines, where human access is limited by safety, accessibility, or operational constraints [1]." [p.1].

## 4. GPS-Denied Context
UNDERGROUND
"GNSS-Denied underground tunnels" [p.1].

## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: Dust-resilient autonomous UAV system with LiDAR-Inertial odometry and intensity-based dust filtering
Category: HYBRID
"This paper presents a UAV system for autonomous mapping in dust-laden, GNSS-Denied tunnels. LiDAR-Inertial odometry was integrated with IMU pre-integration to achieve 100Hz state estimation for responsive control in confined spaces." [p.1].

### 18.6 Trade-offs
