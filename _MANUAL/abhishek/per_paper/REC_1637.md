## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: Virtual testbed for evaluating VO algorithms
Category: OTHER
"This work presents a virtual testbed for conducting simulated flight tests over real- world terrain and analyzing the real- time performance of visual navigation algorithms at 31 Hz." [p.1]

## 6. System Architecture
- Platform: Fixed-wing SUAS (simulated and real 1/3-scale Carbon Cub)
- Sensors: Monocular camera (virtual and real Prosilica GT1290)
- Compute: Laptop (Intel Core i7-7500U, Nvidia Quadro M2200)
- Communication: ROS and LCM

## 7. Experimental Setup
- real_or_sim: BOTH
- environment: Camp Atterbury, Indiana (simulated and real)
- trials: Various airspeeds (10, 25 m/s), roll rates (25, 45, 65 deg/s), altitudes (50-400m)
- baselines: DSO, SVO, ORB-SLAM2
- dataset: NOT_REPORTED
