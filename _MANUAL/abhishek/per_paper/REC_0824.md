## 3. Motivation

## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: Homography-Based Visual Servoing (HBVS)
Category: VISION_OBJECT
"By defining a virtual homography matrix, a passive-like error equation can be obtained. A backstepping-based control approach that uses full dynamics to estimate its translation velocity online is proposed." [p.9]
