## 2. Problem Statement
"However, VIO has problems of scale errors and long-term drift." [p.1]

## 3. Motivation
"This study proposes a method to correct the position errors of VIO without the help of GPS information for vertical takeoff and landing (VTOL) UAVs." [p.1]

## 4. GPS-Denied Context
INDOOR | MIXED
"such as indoor and bridge inspections" [p.1]

## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: Landmark-aided VIO with scale correction
Category: HYBRID
"The position of the UAV is estimated by VIO. Then, the accurate position is estimated by the extended Kalman filter (EKF) with the known landmark, which is used to obtain the scale correction using the least squares method." [p.1]

## 6. System Architecture
- Platform: VTOL UAV
- Sensors: VI-sensor (MYNT EYE D-120), IMU
- Compute: Intel NUC
- Communication: Wi-Fi
"The adopted VI-sensor is faced to a desired chessboard with different poses." [p.9]

## 7. Experimental Setup
- real_or_sim: REAL
- environment: Ground test and flight test
- trials: Two flight cases
- baselines: ROVIO, VINS-Mono
- dataset: NOT_REPORTED
"The ground test is carried out before the flight test to verify that the proposed landmark-based VIO is viable in real tests" [p.10]

## 9. Ablation / Sensitivity
"To examine how efficient the algorithm can solve the long-term drift problem, the final estimated location of the original ROVIO, ROVIO with the GPS-based scale estimation, ROVIO with the landmark-based scale estimation, and the proposed algorithm are compared with the ground truth." [p.11]

## 10. Stated Limitations
"In the scale correction estimation process, the VI-sensor needs to be moved fully on three axes to successfully estimate the scale correction." [p.14]

## 11. Future Work
"1. Add more experiment designs to complete full movements in three axes. 2. Add external force estimation in the algorithm. 3. Use a GPS timestamp to synchronize the time of the camera and IMU. 4. Add external pose information in the measurement update process." [p.14]
