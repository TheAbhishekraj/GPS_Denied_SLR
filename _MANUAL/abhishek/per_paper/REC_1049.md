## 9. Ablation / Sensitivity
"Three new reward structures were presented. The reward structure R3 which takes into account the distance from the UAV to the target in the x axis and uses gaussian functions for the y and z axis achieved the higher tracking time ratios for the mostly deterministic target motion models." [p.6]

## 10. Stated Limitations
"the formulation achieves higher tracking time ratio when the target's motion model is mostly deterministic. However, this ratio decreases for the cases where the target is escaping from the UAV" [p.6]
