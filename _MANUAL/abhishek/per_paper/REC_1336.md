## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: Multi-sensor relative estimation framework
Category: HYBRID
The framework fuses updates from multiple view-based odometry algorithms using a relative navigation paradigm. It estimates vehicle states relative to a local coordinate frame (node frame) and maintains observability.
"This allows the vehicle to utilize a suite of complementary exteroceptive sensors or sensing modalities." [p.1]
