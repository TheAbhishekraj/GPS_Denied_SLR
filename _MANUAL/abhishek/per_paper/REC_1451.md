The paper proposes a vision-model-based real-time self-positioning method for UAV using a single camera. It uses edge-based visual localization based on the RAPID tracker, with LSD line segment detector for edge detection.
"a vision-model-based real-time self-positioning method is proposed to support autonomous aerial inspection without the need of GPS support." [p.1]
