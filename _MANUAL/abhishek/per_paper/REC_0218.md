## 2. Problem Statement
"Reliable navigation in Global Navigation Satellite System (GNSS)-denied environments remains a critical challenge for fixed-wing Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs)." [p.1].

## 3. Motivation
"In such conditions, Inertial Navigation Systems (INSs) must be aided by heterogeneous sensors to mitigate drift and ensure long-term accuracy." [p.1].
