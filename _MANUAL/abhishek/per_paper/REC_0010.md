## 2. Problem Statement
"The capability of an Unmanned Aerial Vehicle to navigate in GNSS-denied environments is desired in many situations, especially in situation in which the GNSS signal is not trustable." [p.1].

## 3. Motivation
"The dependency of the GNSS signal to navigate is common because most inertial systems of aircrafts cannot guarantee a trustable positioning information during too much time, since the errors accumulate continuously, mainly for small and low cost systems[4]." [p.1].

## 4. GPS-Denied Context
GNSS_SPOOFED
"A recurrent application of these techniques is work as a backup to Global Navigation Satellite Systems (GNSS), since these systems can be unreliable in some situations, sometimes due to atmospheric conditions [2] or even due to intentional interference [3]." [p.1].

## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: Landmark Recognition-based long-range navigation
Category: VISION_OBJECT
"The main task for the computer vision algorithm is to eliminate the errors cumulated in the flight path. To do this, a Landmark Recognition algorithm has been selected, that is able to estimate the current coordinate, matching previously loaded references with the current captured image [11]." [p.2].
