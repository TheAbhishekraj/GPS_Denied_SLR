## 2. Problem Statement
"This paper studies the problem of localising a Global Positioning System (GPS)-denied Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV) in two-dimensional space." [p.1]

## 3. Motivation
"UAVs which are being used for surveillance will frequently operate in a formation, and to maintain the formation shape, it will generally be necessary for one or more of the UAVs to be able to localise others in the formation [1]." [p.1]
