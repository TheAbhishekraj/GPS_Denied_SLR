## Registration Status: UNREGISTERED

This systematic literature review protocol was **not pre-registered** on OSF (Open Science Framework) or PROSPERO prior to data collection.

**Justification:**  
PROSPERO accepts registrations for systematic reviews in health-related domains only; this review covers engineering/robotics topics (UAV navigation) and therefore falls outside PROSPERO's scope. OSF pre-registration was not completed prior to the search execution date of 2026-06-15. This limitation is acknowledged and disclosed transparently in the manuscript's Methods section in accordance with PRISMA 2020 Item 24 (Registration).

**Action taken:**  
The complete protocol (research questions, PICOC framework, inclusion/exclusion criteria, quality checklist, search strings) is documented in full in `00_scope/PROTOCOL.md` and `00_scope/SEARCH_STRINGS.md` to ensure methodological transparency and reproducibility.
