## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: NOT_REPORTED
Category: VISION_OBJECT | COOPERATIVE
The framework combines bio-inspired passive vision swarm coordination with online monocular point-cloud registration for global localization. UAVs maintain formation using monocular visual cues and align local point clouds to a globally referenced map.
"This paper presents a unified vision-based framework in which UAVs use biologically inspired swarm behaviors together with online monocular point-cloud registration to achieve real-time global localization." [p.1]
