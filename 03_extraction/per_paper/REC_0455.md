## 2. Problem Statement
"We propose a location algorithm to promptly search for crashed unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs), using an airborne communication relay UAV (ACRU) in global positioning system (GPS)-denied environments." [p.1].

## 3. Motivation
"If a UAV crashes during military operations, the reconnaissance information and specific data of the UAV may be retrieved by enemy forces, thereby critically jeopardizing future military operations by revealing the intentions of such operations." [p.1].

## 4. GPS-Denied Context
MIXED
"in global positioning system (GPS)-denied environments" [p.1].

## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: Airborne-Relay-based Algorithm
Category: OTHER
"We propose a method of locating a crashed UAV when the crashed UAV transmits signals in a battlefield environment, a ground control station promptly launches an airborne-communication-relay-UAV (ACRU) to relay the signals." [p.2].

## 10. Stated Limitations
