## 2. Problem Statement
"Traditional UAV navigation relies on the Global Navigation Satellite System (GNSS) for localization; however, this system has disadvantages of instability and susceptibility to interference." [p.1].

## 3. Motivation
"Therefore, to obtain accuracy in UAV pose estimation in GNSS-denied environments, a UAV localization method that is assisted by deep learning features of satellite imagery is proposed." [p.1].
