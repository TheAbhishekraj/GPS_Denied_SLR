## 2. Problem Statement
"In this paper, we address the challenge of navigating through unknown indoor environments using autonomous aerial robots within confined spaces. The core of our system involves the integration of key sensor technologies, including depth sensing from the ZED 2i camera, IMU data, and LiDAR measurements, facilitated by the Robot Operating System (ROS) and RTAB-Map." [p.1]

## 3. Motivation
"The process is particularly challenging indoors due to the absence of GPS signals, the presence of various obstacles, and the need for precise, real-time decision-making [8]. UAVs operating in indoor spaces encounter tight corridors, dynamic environments, and limited space to maneuver, all of which intensify the complexity of navigation." [p.1]

## 4. GPS-Denied Context
INDOOR

## 5. Proposed Method
"We propose a novel integration of Multi-Sensor Fusion with visual and LiDAR-based SLAM algorithms to overcome the limitations of single-sensor navigation." [p.2] This entails combining ZED 2i stereovision, IMU, and 360 LiDAR into RTAB-Map for mapping and safe navigation.

## 6. System Architecture
- Platform: Hexadrone customized quadrotor. [p.3]
- Sensors: ZED 2i Stereo Camera, RPLiDAR A1, IMU. [p.3]
- Compute: NVIDIA Jetson Nano. Pixhawk 4 for flight control. [p.3]

## 10. Stated Limitations
"While the ZED 2i stereo camera proves effective in indoor environments, its visual depth estimation encounters difficulties in highly reflective or featureless areas, reducing point cloud accuracy." [p.5]
