## 1. Bibliographic Metadata
T. Du, Y. Lin, "An Autonomous Continuous Positioning Method for Low-Altitude UAVs Based on Image Recognition in GNSS-Denied Environments." (Recent reference dates up to 2023).

## 2. Problem Statement
"The article proposes a five-stage autonomous continuous positioning method that integrates inertial navigation and image recognition to address the problem of accumulated positioning errors in unmanned aerial vehicles (UAV) in satellite-denied environments." [p.1]

## 3. Motivation
"However, GNSS signals are susceptible to weather conditions and environmental interference. In rainy weather, densely forested areas, or high-rise urban canyons, these signals experience multipath effects or severe attenuation [5,6], making it difficult to maintain stable operation of low-altitude UAVs..." [p.1]

## 4. GPS-Denied Context
URBAN_CANYON
"densely forested areas, or high-rise urban canyons" [p.1]

## 5. Proposed Method
"This method first used inertial navigation (INS) for initial positioning to capture satellite image basemaps... The experiment was based on the UAV-VisLOC dataset, and showed that two-stage matching can reduce the average plane positioning error" [p.1] "innovatively adopted a two-stage matching strategy of template matching and SuperPoint & SuperGlue feature point matching to effectively overcome the interference" [p.1]

## 6. System Architecture
- Platform: UAV (implies generic low-altitude drones)
- Sensors: Downward camera, INS [p.1]
- Compute: Offline (PyTorch, dataset analysis)

## 7. Experimental Setup
- real_or_sim: SIM
- environment: UAV-VisLOC dataset with varied environmental and lighting conditions. [p.1]
- baselines: Single-stage matching. [p.1]
- dataset: UAV-VisLOC dataset [p.1]
