## 2. Problem Statement
"In these environments, where the Global Navigation Satellite Systems (GNSS) is unavailable, and the risk of collision with surrounding structures is high, a positioning system with high accuracy and fast update rates is essential." [p.1]

## 3. Motivation
"Recently, the operational domain of uncrewed aerial vehicles (UAVs) has expanded from outdoor environments to indoor spaces such as factories, power plants, and tunnels." [p.1]

## 4. GPS-Denied Context
INDOOR
"we propose a novel 3-D positioning algorithm for UAVs operating in a GNSS-denied environment." [p.1]

## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: REFMP (Recursive Extended Finite-Memory Positioning)
Category: HYBRID
"The proposed algorithm is based on sensor fusion of the inertial measurement unit (IMU) and ultra-wideband-based wireless sensor network (UWB-WSN)." [p.1]
