## 2. Problem Statement
"subterranean environments pose many operational challenges for autonomous robots including: poor lighting conditions, harsh traversability, lack of access to GNSS signals" [p.1]

## 3. Motivation
"a key enabling technology for the use of autonomous robots in subterranean environments is the ability of the robot to localize itself within a map." [p.1]

## 4. GPS-Denied Context
UNDERGROUND
"GNSS-denied subterranean environment" [p.1]

## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: Cooperative UAV-UGV navigation with EKF
Category: COOPERATIVE
"a cooperative UAV and UGV system, in which a UGV is available to produce a 3D map of the unknown environment and provide the sensing needed to localize the UAV within the map." [p.1]

## 6. System Architecture
- Platform: UAV (multi-rotor) and UGV (Husky)
"Clearpath Robotics Husky UGV platform" [p.2]
- Sensors: UGV: fish-eye camera, 3D LIDAR, UWB; UAV: IMU, laser altimeter
"omnidirectional fish-eye camera, a 128 channel 3D scanning LIDAR, and an ultra wideband (UWB) ranging radio" [p.1]
- Compute: UGV: i7-7700; UAV: NVIDIA Jetson
"i7-7700 processor" [p.2]
- Communication: Wi-Fi
"local Wi-Fi network" [p.1]
