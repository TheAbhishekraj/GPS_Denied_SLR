## 2. Problem Statement
"Autonomous recovery of Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs) in maritime environments is challenged by dynamic ship motion, texture-poor surroundings, and vulnerabilities inherent to Global Navigation Satellite Systems (GNSS)." [p.1]

## 3. Motivation
"To ensure safe touchdown, the UAV requires a robust and precise estimate of its pose relative to the landing deck." [p.1]

## 4. GPS-Denied Context

## 9. Ablation / Sensitivity
"the varying precision across the orientation angles is directly linked to the unequal geometric lever arms provided by the 3D landmark distribution." [p.6]

## 10. Stated Limitations
"the pose estimation step averaged an inference latency of 0.21 s (roughly 4.7 Hz)... it currently falls short of the high-speed real-time benchmarks typically required for aggressive terminal landing maneuvers." [p.7]

## 11. Future Work
"future work must address the computational bottlenecks associated with inference latency... ongoing research will focus on optimizing the feature extraction and matching phases to ensure deployment viability on Size, Weight, and Power (SWaP)-constrained edge compute devices, such as the NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano. Additionally, future initiatives will transition this framework from scale models to full-scale naval vessels" [p.7]
