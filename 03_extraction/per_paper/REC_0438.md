## 1. Bibliographic Metadata
De Mel, Stol, Mills, and Eastwood, "Vision-based object path following on a quadcopter for GPS-denied environments," 2017 International Conference on Unmanned Aircraft Systems (ICUAS), Miami, FL, USA, pp. 456-461, June 2017. DOI: 10.1109/ICUAS.2017.7991500 [p.1]. Affiliation: Department of Mechanical Engineering, University of Auckland, New Zealand [p.1].

## 2. Problem Statement
"development of an object path following algorithm for a quadcopter using on-board vision and localization" [p.1]. Goal: "increase capabilities of autonomous unmanned aerial vehicles (UAV), with the use of object path following. By following an object path, reliance on obstacle avoidance strategies can be reduced" [p.1].

## 3. Motivation
"Path-tracking is of interest due to its inherent obstacle avoidance, from the fact that the target was able to take its path so the UAV should be able to as well" [p.1]. Intended for "follow a human at the same height (2D path-tracking), like when following and filming from a 3rd person perspective" [p.1]. GPS "has limitations in availability and accuracy, such as in urban environments or indoors" [p.1].

## 4. GPS-Denied Context
REAL_WORLD - outdoor tests with moderate wind (up to 28 km/hr) and variable lighting [p.4-5]. "The use of optical flow allows for localization in GPS-denied environments" [p.1]. No GPS used for navigation; a second UAV in GPS hold mode provided overhead video for ground truth [p.5].

## 5. Proposed Method
Dead-reckoning localization from PX4FLOW optical flow + IMU + sonar altitude, processed by ArduCopter EKF at 100 Hz [p.2, p.4]. Pixy camera (hue-based detection) provides target bearing and range [p.2-3]. Nested PI position controller + P yaw tracking controller [p.4]. Path-following algorithm stores object positions in a 50-point circular queue (5 s at 10 Hz) and generates waypoints >=3 m apart with 50 cm capture radius [p.4].

## 6. System Architecture
Custom quadcopter on DJI Flame Wheel F450 frame (45 cm diameter) [p.2]. Sensors: MaxBotix XL-EZ4 sonar (altitude), PX4FLOW optical flow (velocity), Pixy CMUcam5 (target detection, 210 mm red ball, range up to 6 m), 2-DOF camera gimbal [p.2-3]. Compute: Pixhawk flight controller with ArduCopter EKF [p.2]. Controllers: AltHold (baro+sonar), P yaw, PI position (roll/pitch commands, +/-20 deg/s rate limit) [p.4].

## 7. Experimental Setup
- real_or_sim: REAL_WORLD
- environment: Outdoor, moderate wind (<=28 km/hr), variable lighting [p.4-5]
- ground_truth: Second UAV in GPS position-hold mode filming from above; video stabilized to Earth-fixed point [p.5]
- metrics: Euclidean distance from desired position/path [p.5]
- localization test: walked UAV between 1-5 m points, 10 trials each, ~1 m/s, 1 min per trial [p.4-5]
- path-following test: single outdoor trial, 45 s recorded (final 30 s steady-state) [p.5]

## 8. Key Quantitative Results
Localization: 'standard deviation of horizontal position from the true value over all tests was 6.5 cm'; largest absolute error 23 cm at 5 m [p.5]. Position hold: 'within 25 cm in xe and 30 cm in ye during steady state'; max 38 cm, RMS 20 cm [p.5]. Path following: 'rms error of 28 cm and maximum deviation of 78 cm' [p.1, p.5]. Yaw tracking: +/-6 deg outdoors (std 2.2 deg); zero steady-state, max 1.5 deg indoors [p.4].

## 9. Ablation / Sensitivity
Indoor localization 'not quantified due to lighting and surface pattern issues' [p.4]. Pixy performance 'diminished significantly' closer than 1 m; intended separation 3 m [p.3]. Maximum error in path following attributed to 'high wind' and/or 'shaded area' possibly causing incorrect range estimation [p.5]. Rate limit +/-20 deg/s added because 'larger rotational velocities cause a decreased accuracy in the optical flow sensor readings' [p.4].

## 10. Stated Limitations
'No work has been done to solve the problem of occlusions as of yet' [p.3]. Dead-reckoning drift: 'For larger time periods, a method to correct the drift would likely be required' [p.4]. Dynamic obstacles 'would not be avoided by this method' [p.1]. Pixy requires specific colored target (210 mm red ball) [p.3]. Single path-following trial only [p.5].

## 11. Future Work
'more rigorous testing of the path following algorithm, investigating the effects of object speed and radius of curvature of the path' [p.5]. Alternative ground truth based on real time kinematic (RTK) GPS for cm-level accuracy [p.5-6]. 'general feature tracking will become much more readily available for use in lightweight systems, removing the need for a specific target type' [p.3].
