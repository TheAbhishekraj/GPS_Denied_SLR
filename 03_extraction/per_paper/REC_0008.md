## 2. Problem Statement
UAVs cannot rely on GPS in structurally occluded or canyon environments. Quote: "However, there exist challenging scenarios in which the UAV is required to fly under structures, such as bridges, within mines and buildings or below dense canopy, or in natural or urban canyons in which the GPS signal is partially or completely obstructed." [p.3]

## 3. Motivation
Localization and planning are usually treated separately; this work couples them under uncertainty. Quote: "The aim of this research is to combine the use of localisation algorithms such as Simultaneous Localisation and Mapping (SLAM) with Partially Observable Markov Decision Processes (POMDP) algorithms into a framework in which the navigation and exploration tasks are modelled as sequential decision problems under uncertainty." [p.2]

## 4. GPS-Denied Context
MIXED (GPS partially or totally occluded under structures/canopy; no jamming/spoofing tested). Quote: "the UAV is required to fly under structures, such as bridges, within mines and buildings or below dense canopy, or in natural or urban canyons in which the GPS signal is partially or completely obstructed." [p.3]

## 5. Proposed Method
POMDP-based navigation/exploration framework: TAPIR (ABT algorithm) solver over a belief state; depth-sensor octomap occupancy map updated each step; actions are motion commands to PID set-points; SLAM or VO supplies pose in GPS-denied flight. method_category: **OTHER** (POMDP decision framework; localization layer is SLAM/VO). Quote: "We use the TAPIR [10] software which uses the ABT [11] POMDP algorithm to model the UAV navigation and exploration sequential decision problem." [p.3]

## 6. System Architecture
- Platform (simulated): Quote: "We use a model of an off-the-shelf UAV (3DR Iris) flying in different simulated worlds." [p.4]
- Sensors (planned/assumed): depth camera (point clouds for octomap). Quote: "the data acquired by the depth sensor in the form of point clouds" [p.4]
- Compute/Software: ROS modular system: POMDP solver (C++), Gazebo + PX4 SITL, octomap server, observation module. Quote: "The framework is implemented using a modular system shown in Fig. 3" [p.4]
- Communication: Quote: "These motion commands are sent to the UAV flight controller using mavlink through mavros messages using the robotic operating system ROS." [p.2]
- Pose source: Quote: "In a GPS-denied scenario, the pose will come from a SLAM or visual odometry (VIO) [13], [14], [4] system which uses onboard sensors such as depth cameras and or INS to estimate the pose of the UAV in the local frame." [p.4]

Quote: "In the first scenario the UAV takes off from the ground in front of a collapsed building" [p.4]; "the UAV needs to navigate safely and produce a map of the space under the gas station ceiling."
