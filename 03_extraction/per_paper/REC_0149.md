## 2. Problem Statement
"A key challenge for UAVs is maintaining reliable navigation, especially in environments where GPS signals may be blocked, jammed, or unavailable [1]." [p.1].

## 3. Motivation
"Therefore, it is important to develop navigation methods that enable UAVs to operate safely without depending on traditional positioning systems." [p.1].

## 4. GPS-Denied Context
MIXED
"enabling accurate positioning even in complex or GPS-denied environments." [p.1].

## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: Vision-based navigation system with deep CNN and map-patch matching
Category: VISION_OBJECT
"The proposed approach uses deep learning models trained on aerial map patches and high-resolution images captured directly by the unmanned aerial vehicles (UAV), enabling accurate position estimation without relying on GPS signals." [p.1].
