## 2. Problem Statement
"This paper presents a reliable vision-based navigation system for guided aerial vehicles operating in GPS-denied environments. The proposed approach uses deep learning models trained on aerial map patches and high-resolution images captured directly by the unmanned aerial vehicles (UAV), enabling accurate position estimation without relying on GPS signals." [p.1]

## 3. Motivation
"A key challenge for UAVs is maintaining reliable navigation, especially in environments where GPS signals may be blocked, jammed, or unavailable [1]. In such cases, reliance on GPS can lead to mission failure or increase the risk of accidents. Therefore, it is important to develop navigation methods that enable UAVs to operate safely without depending on traditional positioning systems." [p.1]

## 4. GPS-Denied Context
JAMMED

## 5. Proposed Method
"The localization task is structured as an image retrieval problem. [...] VGG16 was integrated with NetVLAD specifically for the localization task to produce a compact, generalized image representation." [p.2] "By matching onboard visual information with preprocessed map data, the system enables accurate and stable position estimation." [p.1]

## 6. System Architecture
- Platform: Fixed-wing UAV. [p.3]
- Sensors: Downward-facing camera on UAV (capturing high-resolution visual data). [p.1]
- Compute: Intel Core i7, 32 GB RAM, NVIDIA RTX 4070 Ti (offline/simulation processing). [p.4]

## 7. Experimental Setup
- real_or_sim: SIM
- environment: Simulated diverse flight environments covering urban, desert, and mountain areas. [p.3]
- baselines: ResNet50, MobileNetV2 [p.4]
- dataset: Custom simulated aerial datasets (Reference Map Database and UAV Captured Images). [p.3]

## 9. Ablation / Sensitivity
Different CNN backbones were compared (VGG16, ResNet50, MobileNetV2). "As shown in Table II, the VGG16 model with NetVLAD outperformed other architectures, achieving higher Recall@1, Recall@5, and Recall@10 accuracy levels." [p.4]

## 10. Stated Limitations
"Although our system achieved strong performance, several challenges remain. Under certain extreme conditions, such as dense fog, heavy rain, or drastic seasonal changes (e.g., complete snow cover), visual features can be severely degraded, causing localization failures." [p.5]

## 11. Future Work
"Future work will focus on integrating multi-sensor fusion, such as combining vision with LiDAR or thermal imaging, to overcome these environmental constraints." [p.5]
