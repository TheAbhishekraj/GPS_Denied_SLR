## 2. Problem Statement
"Modern UAVs are expected to be able to fly safely through the area where GNSS signal is weak or even could not be received." [p.1].

## 3. Motivation
"In order to realize it, the intuitive idea is by adding some airborne sensors and processor chips, which is, however, not practically feasible for the UAVs that have limited payload capability and online-computing resources." [p.1].

## 4. GPS-Denied Context
INDOOR
"Four in-door flight experiments are conducted to verify the effectiveness of the technical solution." [p.1].

## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: Vision-aided navigation system by ground-aerial vehicle cooperation
Category: COOPERATIVE
"we developed and verified a vision-aided guidance system using the air-ground robot cooperation." [p.2].

## 6. System Architecture
- Platform: "commercial quadrotor Parrot AR.Drone2.0 and the ground robot is Turtlebot2" [p.2]
- Sensors: "monocular camera, IMU and atmospheric pressure sensor" on UAV [p.2]; "Microsoft Kinect V2" on ground robot [p.2]
- Compute: "Lenovo Y410p with i7-4700" [p.2]
- Communication: "wireless LAN and wired network" [p.2]
