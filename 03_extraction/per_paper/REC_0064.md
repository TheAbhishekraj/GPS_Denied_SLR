## 3. Motivation
"To overcome the limitations posed by GPS-denied environments for UAVs, we seek to develop an alternative lightweight positioning and navigation methodology solely relying on packet loss measurement." [p.2].

## 4. GPS-Denied Context
INDOOR (and outdoor surveillance)
"Locating UAVs in GPS-denied environments, such as indoors or in highly dense areas, remains a critical challenge for such a surveillance operation." [p.14].

## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: Packet loss-based multilateration positioning with heuristic navigation
Category: OTHER (packet-based localization)
"we propose a packet loss-based positioning approach, wherein a drone estimates its current position by utilizing wireless packet loss from reference points packet transmission by employing a multilateration technique." [p.1].
