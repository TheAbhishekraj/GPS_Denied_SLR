## 2. Problem Statement
GPS reliance blocks true autonomy indoors. Quote: "However, the reliance on GPS presents a significant obstacle to developing truly autonomous UAV capabilities, particularly in indoor or GPS-denied environments." [p.1]

## 3. Motivation
Low-cost autonomous search-and-rescue capability for regions with poor GNSS coverage. Quote: "The proposed method has great potential in a country like Nepal, where most of the terrain is outside the reach of the GPS signals and is prone to disasters." [p.2]

## 4. GPS-Denied Context
INDOOR. Quote: "The research aims to autonomously navigate in GPS-denied spaces by localization and mapping in real time." [p.1]

## 5. Proposed Method
RTAB-Map visual SLAM with external odometry, integrated with A* path planning on a low-cost onboard stack. method_category: **SLAM**. Quote: "Therefore, in this paper, we focus on integrating path planning with SLAM using a low-cost onboard computer and flight controller, providing a cost-effective solution for autonomous SAR operations in GPS-denied environments." [p.2]

## 6. System Architecture
- Platform: quadcopter. Quote: "The proposed system integrates an Intel RealSense D435 depth camera, a Raspberry Pi 4B for onboard computation, a laser rangefinder for altitude measurement, and a Pixhawk 4 flight controller." [p.1]
- Sensors: Intel RealSense D435 depth camera; laser rangefinder (altitude). [p.1]
- Compute: Raspberry Pi 4B (onboard); Pixhawk 4 (flight control). [p.1]
- Communication: NOT_REPORTED (ROS nodes onboard; no link budget given).

## 7. Experimental Setup
- real_or_sim: **BOTH**. Quote: "The RTAB-Map SLAM is first tested in a simulation using ROS and Gazebo to ensure its robustness and further deployed in real-world experiments." [p.1]
- Environment: INDOOR — simulation + two real rooms. Quote: "the quadcopter was also tested at Manufacturing Lab [12] as seen in Fig. 7b and Senior Classroom." [p.4]
- Trials: Quote: "These results were obtained by conducting the experiment seven times in each environment." [p.4] — 5 environments × 7 runs. Flight parameters: "operating at a maximum speed of 0.5 m/s while maintaining a set altitude of 0.5 m" [p.1]
- Baselines: literature comparison of SLAM algorithms (Merzlyakov et al., TUM RGB-D). Quote: "RTAB-Map is most accurate method among compared algorithm in this environment since it is 499% more accurate than ORB-SLAM3 and 36% compared to OpenVSLAM." [p.2]
- Datasets: TUM RGB-D sequences (for the algorithm comparison); self-flown indoor trials.

## 18. Best Combination & Accuracy
18.1 Best sensor combination: Intel RealSense D435 depth camera + laser rangefinder + Pixhawk 4 + Raspberry Pi 4B. Quote: "The proposed system integrates an Intel RealSense D435 depth camera, a Raspberry Pi 4B for onboard computation, a laser rangefinder for altitude measurement, and a Pixhawk 4 flight controller." [p.1]
18.2 Best algorithm: RTAB-Map visual SLAM with A* path planning. Quote: "the quadcopter achieved successful autonomous navigation using the RTAB-Map algorithm integrated with the A* algorithm for path planning" [p.5]
18.3 Best accuracy: mean path error **0.06 m** (Senior Classroom; best of the five environments). Quote: "Similarly, it is least (0.06 m) for senior classroom which has a lot of features compared to others." [p.4]
18.4 Baseline comparison at best configuration: NO_BASELINE for the flight trials; the only comparison is the cited third-party SLAM benchmark (RTAB-Map vs ORB-SLAM3 / OpenVSLAM) [p.2].
18.5 Conditions: feature-rich indoor classroom; altitude 0.5 m; speed up to 0.5 m/s; 7 runs. Quote: "First, the quadcopter was made to take off up to 0.5 m in all environments and then multiple goal points were provided inside the environment using RViz GUI" [p.4]
18.6 Trade-offs: feature-poor environments degrade odometry (0.13 m with single obstacle) vs feature-rich (0.06 m); onboard compute (RPi 4B) limits performance — Quote: "These deviations and errors can be further minimized with better computing unit instead of Raspberry Pi 4B." [p.5]
18.7 Generalization limits: path planning/obstacle avoidance restricted to 2D due to hardware. Quote: "However, the quadcopter's capacity to carry out path planning and obstacle avoidance was only restricted to 2D due to hardware limitations." [p.5]
