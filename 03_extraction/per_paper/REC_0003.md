## 2. Problem Statement
"However, the reliance on GPS presents a significant obstacle to developing truly autonomous UAV capabilities, particularly in indoor or GPS-denied environments." [p.1].

## 3. Motivation
"The proposed method has great potential in a country like Nepal, where most of the terrain is outside the reach of the GPS signals and is prone to disasters." [p.2].

## 4. GPS-Denied Context
INDOOR
"The research aims to autonomously navigate in GPS-denied spaces by localization and mapping in real time." [p.1].

## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: RTAB-Map SLAM with A* and Regulated Pure Pursuit
Category: SLAM
"The proposed system integrates an Intel RealSense D435 depth camera, a Raspberry Pi 4B for onboard computation, a laser rangefinder for altitude measurement, and a Pixhawk 4 flight controller. The RTAB-Map SLAM is first tested in a simulation using ROS and Gazebo to ensure its robustness and further deployed in real-world experiments." [p.1].

## 6. System Architecture
- Platform: "The quadcopter was then integrated with an Intel RealSense D435, a VL53L1X rangefinder, a Pixhawk 4 flight computer, and a Raspberry Pi 4B onboard computer." [p.3]
- Sensors: "an Intel RealSense D435 depth camera, a Raspberry Pi 4B for onboard computation, a laser rangefinder for altitude measurement, and a Pixhawk 4 flight controller" [p.1]
- Compute: "the companion computer handles the SLAM and path planning computations and connects to the ground control station via a Wi-Fi access point" [p.3]
- Communication: "The ground control station (GCS) is an Ubuntu 20.04 computer with ROS Noetic installed, communicating with the onboard companion computer via SSH." [p.3]

## 7. Experimental Setup

## 10. Stated Limitations
"However, the quadcopter's capacity to carry out path planning and obstacle avoidance was only restricted to 2D due to hardware limitations." [p.5].
"The mean altitude deviation is in between 4% to 7% for all cases which is due to the noise associated with laser range finder sensor." [p.5].

## 11. Future Work
"The future scope of this work focuses on improving the robustness and suitability of the quadcopter for real-world deployment by increasing both its computational power and operational range. Key improvements would be upgrading the onboard processing hardware for handling visual SLAM more efficiently and incorporating 3D path planning. Performance can also be optimized by shifting computationally intensive tasks to a ground station via wireless data transfer allowing offboard SLAM and path planning." [p.5].

"RTAB-Map was selected for this project, because it shows the best capability to recover from losing its track throughout the sequences." [p.2].
