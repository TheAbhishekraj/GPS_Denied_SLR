## 3. Abstract / TL;DR
The context of this research project and its application relates to Target Acquisition missions on Mars; more specifically, the problem of searching for water on Mars via a fully autonomous UAV. Since Mars is a GPS denied environment, the UAV will localize itself. The paper presents a simulation framework with VINS-mono for navigation and a multispectral sensor for water detection.
