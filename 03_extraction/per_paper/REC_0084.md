## 1. Bibliographic Metadata

## 2. Problem Statement
"The context of this research project and its application relates to Target Acquisition missions on Mars; more specifically, the problem of searching for water on Mars via a fully autonomous UAV." [p.2].

## 3. Motivation
"If humans really were to colonize Mars, it would be vital that a viable source of water is available." [p.2].

## 4. GPS-Denied Context
NOT_REPORTED
"Since Mars is a GPS denied environment, the UAV will have to localize itself." [p.4].

## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: Simulation framework with VINS-mono for navigation and rudimentary multispectral sensor for water detection
Category: OTHER
"In this paper, we present a method for autonomous navigation and detection of surface water bodies in the GPS denied environments of Mars via a fully autonomous UAV." [p.2].

## 9. Ablation / Sensitivity
"With loop closure optimisation, the RMS error values in the x, y and z coordinates were 30.3348, 44.9588 and 2.9887 meters respectively. This performed worse than no loop closure optimisation" [p.8].

## 10. Stated Limitations
"The current model of the multispectral sensor has some severe limitations. The major limitation of this model is that the standard water index (mNDWI) does not give the expected value" [p.8]. Also "the machine on which the simulation was running began to lag due to the large amount of data being processed (30 Gb)" [p.8].

## 11. Future Work
"Finally, we provide several recommendations for future research... 1. Exploring the concept of combining VINS-mono with star sensors... 2. Using the Kalibr calibration tool... 3. Exploring a solution to use reflectance values instead of radiance values..." [p.9].
