## 1. Bibliographic Metadata
T. Thakkar, R. Ramirez, R. Tsui, S. Bhandari, and A. Raheja, "3D Path Planning for Autonomous UAV Navigation in GPS-Denied Environments," NOT_REPORTED, NOT_REPORTED.

## 2. Problem Statement
"The increasing prevalence of GPS spoofing [1], [2] and jamming [3] attacks on UAV platforms has underscored the need for robust navigation solutions capable of operating reliably in GPS-denied environments." [p.1].

## 3. Motivation
"Many of the existing works only consider point mass models of the vehicles for simulation studies. While useful for preliminary studies, these point mass models do not represent six-degrees-of-freedom motion of the vehicle." [p.1].

## 4. GPS-Denied Context
MIXED
"This paper presents a UAV path planning framework designed to enable smooth, collision-free navigation in static, GPS-denied environments." [p.1].

## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: Sampling-based path planning in SE(3) with multi-objective optimization
Category: OTHER
"The path-planning pipeline implements a complete end-to-end framework for safe autonomous UAV navigation in cluttered, GPS-denied environments using volumetric mapping [4] and sampling-based motion planning [9]." [p.1].

## 6. System Architecture
- Platform: "custom UAV features a 3D-printed chassis with carbon-fiber arms and landing legs, equipped with a stereodepth camera, a LiDAR sensor, and an onboard computer." [p.5]
- Sensors: "stereodepth camera, a LiDAR sensor" [p.5]; "IMU" [p.1]
- Compute: "onboard NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin interfaces with the PX4 flight controller" [p.5]
- Communication: "wirelessly to the ground control station" [p.2]

## 9. Ablation / Sensitivity
"The fastest simplification times are exhibited with InformedRRT* and RRTstatic with values of 0.386 seconds and 0.463 seconds respectively while RRT* and RRT performed the worst with simplification times of 1.05 seconds and 1.81 seconds respectively." [p.6].

## 10. Stated Limitations
"During testing, moderate wind conditions were present and contributed to the small deviations from the planned path." [p.7].

## 11. Future Work
"Future work will extend the framework to dynamic environments with real-time replanning for moving obstacles and improved computational efficiency for multi-UAV scalability, including search-and-rescue applications." [p.8].
