## 2. Problem Statement
"This study proposes a high-accuracy two- stage trilateration method that enables real- time positioning in global position system (GPS)-denied areas" [p.1]

## 3. Motivation
"Most drones rely on the onboard inertia measurement unit (IMU) for navigation, but its accuracy is susceptible to cumulative errors caused by measurement drift." [p.1]
