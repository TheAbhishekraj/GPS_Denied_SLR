## 2. Problem Statement
"However, when global navigation satellite system (GNSS) signals are unavailable or degraded, the accumulated drift of dead-reckoning and inertial estimates significantly reduces localization accuracy, especially in complex 3D environments." [p.1].

## 3. Motivation
"To address this issue, this paper proposes a simulation-based sensor fusion based simultaneous localization and mapping (SLAM) framework for robust UAV positioning without relying on GNSS." [p.1].

## 4. GPS-Denied Context
INDOOR
"GPS-denied warehouse scenarios" [p.2].

## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: LiDAR-camera sensor fusion SLAM in Isaac Sim
Category: SLAM
"The framework directly utilizes sequential 3D LiDAR scans to estimate the UAV pose and incrementally build a dense map of the environment, while high-resolution camera streams provide contextual information for the segmentation task." [p.1].
