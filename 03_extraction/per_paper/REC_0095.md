## 3. Abstract / TL;DR
UAV swarms require accurate relative localization to safeguard missions in GNSS-denied environments. The paper proposes a clustering-based cooperative relative localization scheme for UAV swarms via a coalition formation game approach (inter-intra-cluster localization) using DS-TWR ranging.
