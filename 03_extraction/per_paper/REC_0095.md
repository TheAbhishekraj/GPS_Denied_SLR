## 2. Problem Statement
"Unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) swarms require accurate relative localization to safeguard flight missions in the global navigation satellite system-denied environment due to a lack of absolute position information." [p.1].

## 3. Motivation
"In a GNSS-denied environment, signals can be obscured or jammed with for many reasons, including electromagnetic shielding, signal jamming, spoofing attacks, or other catastrophic GNSS failure events." [p.1].

## 4. GPS-Denied Context

## 10. Stated Limitations
"when the border size exceeds 5000 m, the proposed CA-CFG algorithms cannot solve the packet loss caused by long-distance ranging but still have made a very noticeable localization performance compared with comparison algorithms." [p.15].

## 11. Future Work
