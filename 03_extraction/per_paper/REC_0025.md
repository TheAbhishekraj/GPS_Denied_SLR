## 2. Problem Statement
"Commonly used unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) platforms rely on the use of global navigation satellite system (GNSS) receivers for navigation. To enable the autonomous navigation of cooperative UAVs in GNSS-denied environments, the use of an ultra-wideband (UWB) positioning system is proposed." [p.1].

## 3. Motivation
"A need for a low-cost, rapid deployment and decentralized indoor positioning system, enabling the indoor use of UAVs e.g. in emergency response scenarios [3], arose." [p.1].

## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: UWB indoor-positioning system with GNSS emulation
Category: UWB
"the design and evaluation of a practical and cooperative UWB positioning system using newly available integrated radio frequency hardware and antennas. ... A GNSS emulation system is implemented to evaluate the real-time in-flight use of the UWB positioning system on an experimental UAV carrier." [p.1].

## 6. System Architecture
- Platform: "UAV carrier based on an AscTec FireFly and equipped with the UWB node developed during this work" [p.6]
- Sensors: "The hardware node designed for this research, depicted in Fig. 2, is based on the newly available decaWave ScenSor DWM1000 module." [p.1]
- Compute: "The node was connected to an embedded PC, running a standard linux based operating system." [p.6]
- Communication: "An application calculating the local positions from the UWB positioning system, transforming them into global coordinates, and providing them over a serial port in the form of standardized GNSS messages was used to feign a physical GNSS receiver to the UAV platform." [p.6]

## 10. Stated Limitations
"navigation performance is tightly coupled with magnetometer performance, as magnetometer readings are needed to calculate the position correction direction in navigation control algorithms. This leads to a non-optimal performance in environments with strong magnetic fields." [p.6].

## 11. Future Work
"More research is required to optimize the filtering for the UAV specific navigation controller as well as resolving magnetometer issues indoors, maybe with the help of integrated measurement unit (IMU) sensor fusion, to achieve improved flight performance." [p.7].

## 18. Best Combination & Accuracy
### 18.1 Best sensor combination
"The hardware node designed for this research, depicted in Fig. 2, is based on the newly available decaWave ScenSor DWM1000 module." [p.1] + embedded PC for real-time GNSS emulation connected via serial port to AscTec FireFly flight controller [p.6].
### 18.2 Best algorithm
UWB TDoA/ToF positioning with coordinate transformation into global coordinates and NMEA GNSS message emulation with Kalman-filtered trajectory [p.5-6].
### 18.3 Best accuracy
Positioning accuracy under 10 cm in the horizontal plane and under 20 cm in 3D space with 95% probability [p.1, p.7]; position-hold radius r = 0.369 m at Q(95%) [p.6].
### 18.4 Baseline comparison at best configuration
NO_BASELINE
### 18.5 Conditions of best accuracy
Indoor laboratory flight; 220 s position-hold flight with AscTec FireFly [p.6].
### 18.6 Trade-offs
"navigation performance is tightly coupled with magnetometer performance, as magnetometer readings are needed to calculate the position correction direction in navigation control algorithms. This leads to a non-optimal performance in environments with strong magnetic fields." [p.6].
### 18.7 Generalization limits
Tested in an indoor laboratory; altitude control relies on barometric/inertial measurements rather than UWB z-axis alone [p.6].
