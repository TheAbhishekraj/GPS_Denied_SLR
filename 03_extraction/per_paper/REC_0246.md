## 2. Problem Statement
"For autonomous navigations, UAVs depend on Global Positioning System (GPS) data. However, GPS may be partially lost in certain locations due to high-rise buildings or other obstructions and collapsed/damaged areas." [p.1].

## 3. Motivation
"It is of utmost importance to rescue people from natural disaster zones as fast as possible. However, sometimes this is hard to achieve due to the inherited risk involved with sending humans to disaster zones." [p.1].

## 4. GPS-Denied Context
INDOOR (and outdoor)
"GPS signal is not available in indoor environments." [p.1].

## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: SLAM and YOLOv3 for search and rescue
Category: SLAM
"This paper talks about the performance of SLAM using visual imagery as its single input as well as the performance of YOLOv3 deep learning algorithm to identify victims for search and rescue in GPS-denied environments using UAVs." [p.1].
