## 1. Bibliographic Metadata
Yue, W., Wang, Y., Zhang, X., Wang, L., Liu, Z., Li, L., & Zhu, L. (2026). UAV swarm distributed cooperative localization in GNSS-denied environments: an ICMC and ACPM Fusion approach. *Ad Hoc Networks*, 193, 104363. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.adhoc.2026.104363

## 2. Problem Statement
"With the widespread use of Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs) in urban environments, achieving high-precision localization, particularly in Global Navigation Satellite System (GNSS)-denied environments, presents a significant challenge." [p.1]

## 3. Motivation
"Existing localization methods largely rely on centralized data processing, which often leads to issues such as information redundancy, high computational complexity, and limited real-time responsiveness." [p.1]

## 4. GPS-Denied Context
MIXED
"GNSS-denied environments" [p.1]
"complex urban environments" [p.1]

## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: I-ACPM (ICMC + ACPM)
Category: COOPERATIVE
"This paper proposes a UAV swarm collaborative localization strategy, termed I-ACPM, based on Imperialist Competitive Mechanism-based Clustering (ICMC) and the Ant Colony Pheromone Mechanism (ACPM)." [p.1]

## 6. System Architecture
- Platform: UAV swarm (22 UAVs in simulation)
  "22 UAVs randomly distributed at different positions" [p.9]
- Sensors: AOA and TDOA measurements
  "This approach leverages the AOA and TDOA information received by UAVs" [p.2]
- Compute: MATLAB R2022b
  "The experiments were conducted using MATLAB R2022b." [p.9]
- Communication: Inter-UAV communication links
  "UAVs communicate with each other" [p.1]

## 7. Experimental Setup
- real_or_sim: SIM
  "This section assesses the communication and localization performance of the proposed strategy through simulation experiments." [p.9]
- environment: 2D space 1000m × 1000m
  "The task area is a two-dimensional space (1000m × 1000m)" [p.9]
- trials: 30s simulation
  "22 UAVs continuously move for 30s" [p.9]
- baselines: PLE, WPLE, MLE, SMDS-CFG, HEED
  "the performance of I-ACPM, MLE, and WPLE is close to the CRLB" [p.10]
- dataset: NOT_REPORTED
