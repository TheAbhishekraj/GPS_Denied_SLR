## 2. Problem Statement
"Success of a cooperative mission entirely depends on precise navigation information of each UAV. Though present day UAVs are equipped with GPS, the UAVs have to rely only on navigation information obtained from inertial navigation system while operating in GPS denied environments." [p.1]

## 3. Motivation
"Deploying a large number of micro class of UAVs, and enabling cooperation among them, will cover a wider area and provide time critical intelligence on the enemy movements." [p.1]
