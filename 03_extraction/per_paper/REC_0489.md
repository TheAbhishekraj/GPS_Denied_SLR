## 10. Stated Limitations
"First, the proposed method will not work when face some challenge weather. The camera can't adapt to heavy rain, cloud or fog occlusion, also the UAV platform can't flight in heavy rain or storm wind. Second, the image matching technique is not reliable for area with unstable landform features, such as desert. Third, the proposed approach relies on the image matching to provide the global position. Therefore, it can't adapt to UAV- denied situations, such as under a bridge, tunnel, or other shielding circumstances." [p.27]
