## 2. Problem Statement
"Conventional autonomous navigation systems typically depend on heavy sensor suites such as LiDAR or reliable GPS signals, which are often impractical for lightweight and low- cost rescue drones operating in cluttered indoor spaces" [p.1]

## 3. Motivation
"The increasing frequency and severity of natural disasters ranging from earthquakes to urban structural collapses have underscored the urgent need for rapid, autonomous search and rescue (SAR) technologies." [p.1]

## 4. GPS-Denied Context

"Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs) have become indispensable in search and rescue (SAR) missions by enabling rapid aerial reconnaissance in unstructured and GPS denied environments" [p.1]
