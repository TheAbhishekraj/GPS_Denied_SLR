## 2. Problem Statement
"UAV navigation often relies on GNSS, failing in denied environments like urban canyons. We propose an end-to-end cross-view geo-localization framework mitigating geometric and appearance discrepancies." [p.1]

## 3. Motivation
"UAV navigation often fails in GNSS-denied environments such as urban canyons. To address this issue, cross-view geo-localization matches UAV and satellite images for positioning." [p.1]

## 5. Proposed Method
"We propose a framework integrating feature learning and hard sample mining to improve robustness and deployability under viewpoint and appearance variations." [p.1] Uses ConvNeXt-Base backbone, dynamic sampling strategy, and a symmetric InfoNCE loss for Drone-to-Satellite and Satellite-to-Drone image retrieval.
