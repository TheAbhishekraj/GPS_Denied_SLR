## 1. Bibliographic Metadata
Roggi, Meraglia, and Lovera, "Leonardo Drone Contest 2021: Politecnico di Milano team architecture," 2022 International Conference on Unmanned Aircraft Systems (ICUAS), Dubrovnik, Croatia, pp. 191-196, June 2022. DOI: 10.1109/ICUAS54217.2022.9836103 [p.1]. Affiliation: Dipartimento di Scienze e Tecnologie Aerospaziali, Politecnico di Milano, Italy [p.1].

## 2. Problem Statement
"the winning solution of the second edition of the Leonardo Drone Contest (LDC), an autonomous drone competition" [p.1]. Task: navigate in "a Global Navigation Satellite System (GNSS)-denied environment with autonomous decision making, online planning and collision avoidance capabilities" in an indoor urban-like setting [p.1].

## 3. Motivation
"Leonardo S.p.A. designed and launched the Leonardo Drone Contest (LDC) in collaboration with six Italian universities" to "push the capabilities and performance of autonomous UAVs for what concerns navigation, planning and decision making" [p.1]. The contest aims to create "a national ecosystem that involves and brings together the capabilities of large companies, universities, small and medium enterprises" [p.1].

## 4. GPS-Denied Context
REAL_WORLD - indoor competition field (20 m x 10 m, max height 3 m) in Turin, 28-30 Sept. 2021 [p.2]. "laser scanners were explicitly banned by the rules and, thus, only vision-based sensors were allowed for navigation in the indoor GNSS-denied environment" [p.2]. No motion capture system available [p.5 footnote 6].

## 5. Proposed Method
Modular architecture with three subsystems: Navigation (ZED stereo VO + EKF2 fusion + ArUco-based localization KF + laser altimeter), Decision-making (probabilistic exploration state machine + ArUco detection), Planning and control (Dijkstra global + A* local + B-spline trajectory + PX4 controller) [p.3-5]. Localization KF estimates odom-to-map bias using ArUco markers and laser range [p.4]. Chi-square test rejects outlier altimeter readings over obstacles [p.4].

## 6. System Architecture
Coaxial octocopter (430x300x200 mm, 2.85 kg takeoff), designed with Polimi spin-off ANT-X [p.2]. Sensors: Stereolabs ZED stereo camera (forward, 15 deg down), Lidar-lite v3 laser range finder (altimeter), two OpenMV H7 Plus monocular cameras (forward + downward for ArUco) [p.2]. Compute: Pixhawk 4 FCU + NVIDIA Jetson Xavier NX [p.2]. Middleware: ROS on UAV and ground station [p.2]. OctoMap for 3D mapping [p.4].

## 7. Experimental Setup
- real_or_sim: REAL_WORLD (competition)
- environment: 20 m x 10 m x 3 m indoor field with cardboard buildings, ArUco landing pads, 3 moving ground robots (vacuum cleaners) [p.2]
- ground_truth: ArUco marker detections from monocular cameras used as "best approximation of the ground truth" [p.5]
- task: find intruder robot -> photograph reward string -> land on 6 pads in optimal sequence within 30 min [p.2]
- duration: 20 min mission, ~100 m travelled [p.5]

## 8. Key Quantitative Results
'Politecnico di Milano team reached the first place' with scores 0/5/7/23 = 35 total points across four rounds [p.6]. Table I: Roma Tor Vergata 28, Politecnico di Torino 7, Bologna 6, SantAnna 0, Napoli 0 [p.6]. Localization tracks map frame correctly after takeoff; visual odometry shows 'remarkable drift' in altitude justifying laser altimeter [p.5]. Six successful landings including pads on obstacles of 0.7 m and 2 m height [p.5].

## 9. Ablation / Sensitivity
Laser altimeter vs VO-only: 'visual odometry shows a remarkable drift, which justifies the additional use of the laser altimeter' [p.5]. Chi-square test for outlier rejection: 'correctly rejected the laser measurements corresponding to obstacles' [p.5]. Probabilistic exploration vs random: initial probability map based on free contiguous cells guides search [p.4].

## 10. Stated Limitations
'laser scanners were explicitly banned by the rules' limiting sensor choice to vision [p.2]. 'The field was not equipped with a motion capture system to be used as ground truth' [p.5]. Attitude RB/M assumed equal to RB/O (justified by 'very slow drift' in preliminary FlyART tests) [p.4]. Operator-in-the-loop for landing sequence selection [p.4].
