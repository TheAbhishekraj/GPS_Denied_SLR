## 2. Problem Statement
"Accurate position estimation during navigation is essential for missions conducted using Unmanned Aerial Systems (UASs), particularly in fully autonomous operations or missions performed in Beyond Visual Line of Sight (BVLOs) conditions." [p.1]

## 3. Motivation
"However, GNSS- based approaches are not suitable for indoor navigation scenarios or GPS- denied environments, where satellite signals are unavailable or severely degraded." [p.1]

Category: OTHER
