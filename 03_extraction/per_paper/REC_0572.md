## 2. Problem Statement
"Most of fully autonomous UAVs navigation system is dependent on GNSS for localization. But the security threats that emanates from GPS system; spoofing and jamming makes the reliance on GNSS problematic." [p.1]

## 3. Motivation
"GNSS is used for planning a predefined flight route and it is also involved in correcting or offsetting the deviation on the onboard IMU sensors based relative position tracking. Though GNSS dependent navigation is a most common practice, it is vulnerable to spoofing and jamming attacks as well as it is unreliable in some environments such as indoor, urban with high rise buildings and battlefields." [p.1]
