## 3. Abstract / TL;DR
Presents a nested optimal filter approach (G-H filter + Extended Kalman Filter) for UAVs in GNSS denied environments, integrating Radar Odometry (RO), INS, and magnetometer to sustain a reliable navigation solution and bound low-cost IMU drift.

## 4. Environment & Platform
- **Environment:** Outdoor environment [p.3]
- **Platform:** 3DR SOLO quadcopter [p.2]

## 5. Navigation/Localisation Method
- **Methodology:** G-H filter improves radar velocity estimates, then fused with IMU/MAG via EKF. 
- **Type:** HYBRID (Radar/INS)

## 6. System Architecture
- **Platform:** 3DR SOLO quadcopter [p.2]
- **Sensors:** FMCW radar, MPU6000 IMU, U-Blox GNSS [p.2]
