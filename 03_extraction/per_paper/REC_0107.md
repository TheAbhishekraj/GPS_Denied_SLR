## 2. Problem Statement
"Although most of these UAVs are equipped with Global Navigation Satellite System (GNSS) / Inertial Navigation System (INS) reliable integrated system for localization proposes, they are susceptible to lose their GNSS signals under some environmental and artificial challenging circumstances such as, flying in urban areas, jamming, and spoofing." [p.1].

## 3. Motivation
"This paper presents a nested optimal filter approach (G-H filter, Extended Kalman Filter (EKF)) for UAVs in GNSS denied environment, to enhance the estimated positioning accuracy by integrating the Radar Odometry (RO)/INS/MAG to sustain a reliable navigation solution during GNSS outage." [p.1].

## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: Nested optimal filter (G-H filter + EKF) with Radar Odometry/INS/MAG
Category: HYBRID
"First, the UAV relative velocity is calculated based on image processing approach from the radar range doppler map, then the G-H filter is deployed to improve the accuracy of the vehicle estimated forward velocities based on the vehicle dynamic characteristics to reduce the effect of unrealistic velocity estimate from the radar. These improved velocities are then fused with the Inertial Measurement Unit (IMU), magnetometer, and barometer via EKF to mitigate the positioning RMS errors during the GNSS signals outages." [p.1].

## 9. Ablation / Sensitivity
"The experimental results revealed the massive enhancement in the navigational system solution of the proposed method when compared to the IMU/magnetometer dead reckoning solution." [p.4].

## 10. Stated Limitations
"the navigation systems can maintain reliable performance during GNSS signal outages for a limited time period. This limitation raises the need for a different aiding system to bound the low-cost MEMS-based IMU drift during the unavailability of the GNSS system." [p.4].
