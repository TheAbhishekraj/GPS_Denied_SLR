## 1. Bibliographic Metadata

## 2. Problem Statement
"One of the most demanding capability of the autonomous vehicles is being able to navigate and execute tasks autonomously in complex environments, especially in GPS-denied environments." [p.1].

## 3. Motivation
"In this work, we focus on the problem of autonomous exploration and reconstruction (mapping) for operation in GPS-denied and fully unknown 3D environments." [p.1].

## 4. GPS-Denied Context
MIXED
"GPS-denied 3-dimensional (3-D) unknown environments" [p.1].

## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: Collaborative UAV-UGV exploration and mapping system
Category: COOPERATIVE
"we present a novel integrated vehicular system using collaborative unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) and unmanned ground vehicles (UGVs) for autonomous exploration, mapping, and navigation in GPS-denied 3-dimensional (3-D) unknown environments." [p.1].

## 6. System Architecture
- Platform: "quadrotor platform" for UAV [p.2]; "Mecanum wheels" UGV [p.2]
- Sensors: UAV: "rotary LIDAR device" and "stereo camera (ZED)" [p.2]; UGV: "3D LIDAR" and "RGB-D sensor (KINECT)" [p.2]
- Compute: "Intel-NUC" for UAV [p.2]; onboard processor for UGV [p.2]
- Communication: "wireless network connection through Secure Shell (SSH)" [p.2]
