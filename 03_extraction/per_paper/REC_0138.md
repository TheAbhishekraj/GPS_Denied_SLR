## 2. Problem Statement
"In this paper, we present a novel integrated vehicular system using collaborative unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) and unmanned ground vehicles (UGVs) for autonomous exploration, mapping, and navigation in GPS-denied 3-dimensional (3-D) unknown environments." [p.1]

## 3. Motivation
"One of the most demanded scenarios for autonomous vehicles is to explore unknown environments without global positioning systems (GPS), such as in buildings, underground and urban environments. A single vehicle, however, often suffers from payload limit, limited duration of flight and short detection range. By leveraging multiple heterogeneous vehicles, we can exploit and complement the advantages of individual vehicles towards a common exploration goal." [p.1]

## 5. Proposed Method
"The coarse exploration makes use of a UGV to carry out fast autonomous exploration and active 2.5D simultaneous localization and mapping (SLAM) to generate a coarse environment model, which serves as a navigation reference for subsequent complementary 3-D fine mapping conducted by a UAV." [p.1]

## 6. System Architecture
- Platform: Hexarotor UAV (AscTec Firefly) and UGV (Clearpath Jackal). [p.4]
- Sensors: Asus Xtion Pro depth camera on UAV; Velodyne VLP-16 LiDAR on UGV. "The UAV carries an Asus Xtion Pro depth camera to execute visual navigation and mapping" [p.4]
- Compute: Intel NUC mini PC running ROS on Ubuntu. [p.4]
