## 3. Abstract / TL;DR
The paper addresses accurate outdoors positioning and navigation of UAVs through cooperative localization. Inexpensive ultra-wideband (UWB) transceivers installed on a UAV and a support ground robot enable centimeter-level relative positioning in situations where RTK GNSS is unavailable or inappropriate.

## 4. Environment & Platform
- **Environment:** Outdoor environment next to building / trees [p.3]
- **Platform:** Custom UAV and ClearPath Husky UGV [p.3]

## 5. Navigation/Localisation Method
- **Methodology:** Cooperative UWB-based relative localization using least squares estimator.
- **Type:** UWB / COOPERATIVE

## 6. System Architecture
- **Platform:** UAV + UGV [p.3]
- **Sensors:** 4 UWB transceivers on ground robot; 2 UWB transceivers and Intel RealSense T265 on UAV [p.3]
- **Compute:** PX4 autopilot firmware [p.3]

## 7. Experimental Setup
- **real_or_sim:** BOTH
- **environment:** Outdoor GNSS blocked/reflected area [p.3]
- **baselines:** GNSS and VIO

## 8. Key Quantitative Results
| metric | value + unit | baseline | [p.N] |
|---|---|---|---|
| Navigation error (anchors >10m) | <10 cm | NO_BASELINE | [p.3] |
| Positioning error | >1 m (20% of time) for small UGV setting | NO_BASELINE | [p.3] |

## 9. Ablation / Sensitivity
Positioning error consistently decreases as UWB anchors become more separated on the UGV [p.3].

## 10. Stated Limitations
VIO diverges when UAV altitude increases; small UGV baseline for UWB causes instability [p.3].

## 11. Future Work
Integrating UWB with GNSS data and additional robots configuration [p.4].
