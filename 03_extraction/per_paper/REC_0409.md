## 1. Bibliographic Metadata
Montes-Grova, Gonzalez-Marin, Perez-Grau, and Viguria, "Dust-Resilient Autonomous Navigation and Mapping for UAVs in GNSS-Denied Underground Tunnels," 2026 International Conference on Unmanned Aircraft Systems (ICUAS), DOI: 10.1109/ICUAS69441.2026.11598747 [p.1]. Affiliation: CATEC (Advanced Center for Aerospace Technologies), Sevilla, Spain [p.1].

## 2. Problem Statement
"simultaneous GNSS denial and visibility degradation from airborne dust present critical navigation challenges" in underground tunnels [p.1]. "Conventional visual odometry fails under feature scarcity, while LiDAR-based methods suffer from noise artifacts as suspended particles are misinterpreted as obstacles" [p.1].

## 3. Motivation
"Autonomous UAVs enable safe inspection of hazardous underground environments" for post-blast tunnel construction, progress monitoring, and emergency response where "human access is restricted" [p.1]. Traditional surveying (Total Station) took "approximately two days" vs UAV 480 s [p.5].

## 4. GPS-Denied Context
REAL_WORLD - fully autonomous flight in a real 500 m mining tunnel in Spain under simulated post-blast dust conditions [p.1, p.4]. "underground settings block these signals entirely, forcing the system to rely solely on onboard sensing" [p.1].

## 5. Proposed Method
Tightly-coupled LiDAR-Inertial Odometry (COIN-LIO) with IMU pre-integration at 100 Hz [p.1, p.3]. Real-time intensity-based dust filtering to remove point-cloud contamination [p.1, p.2]. Factor-graph global mapping (GTSAM) with loop closure (KISS-Matcher + Point-to-Plane ICP) [p.3]. Hierarchical exploration: GBPlanner (graph-based global) + Fast-Planner (B-spline local) + ROG-Map occupancy grid [p.3-4]. LUCAS framework for guidance/control [p.4].

## 6. System Architecture
Platform: custom quadrotor, 5.2 kg, 12 min endurance, protective rotor guards, sealed motors, LED lighting, fairing [p.2]. Sensors: Ouster OS0-128 3D LiDAR (128-ch, 10 Hz, 90 deg FOV, dual-return, side-facing), FLIR Hadron 640R (640x512 LWIR + 1080p RGB) on Gremsy G-Hadron gimbal (surveillance only, NOT in nav pipeline), Pixhawk Orange Cube with ICM-20948 IMU at 100 Hz [p.2]. Compute: Intel NUC 10 (i7-10710U, 16 GB RAM), Ubuntu 20.04, ROS Noetic [p.2].

## 7. Experimental Setup
- real_or_sim: REAL_WORLD
- environment: 500 m mining tunnel, Spain; simulated post-blast dust [p.1, p.4]
- ground_truth: LEICA Total Station survey (done ~2 years prior) [p.5]
- metrics: cloud-to-cloud (C2C) distance: mean, RMS, std [p.5]
- speed: exploration 1.5 m/s (0.75 m/s2 accel); return 2.5 m/s (1.25 m/s2) [p.4]
- duration: 480 s total (310 s in + 170 s out) [p.4]

## 8. Key Quantitative Results
"mean accuracy of 0.19 m, a root-mean-square (RMS) error of 0.60 m, and a standard deviation of 0.57 m" C2C vs Total Station [p.5]. "completes exploration and mapping in 480 seconds" [p.1]. Highest errors at tunnel entrance (vegetation) and excavation front (temporal gap between surveys) [p.5-6].

## 9. Ablation / Sensitivity
Intensity-based dust filter: "manual examination of extensive test flights" established clustering threshold; spatial ROI filter for near-field artifacts [p.2-3]. RGB/IR camera explicitly excluded from navigation: "plays no role in the navigation or mapping pipeline" [p.4]. COIN-LIO selected for "robustness in geometrically degenerate environments" vs geometric-only methods [p.3].

## 10. Stated Limitations
"prior tunnel-focused evaluations have been limited to clean environments and teleoperated flights" [p.1] - addressed here. Remaining: "existing solutions do not directly translate to autonomous UAV operation in post-blast tunnel environments" due to particle size/density/reflectivity differences and propeller downwash re-suspension [p.1]. Temporal mismatch with GT (2-year gap) inflates error at boundaries [p.5].

## 11. Future Work
"extending the system to more complex tunnel networks with branches and intersections, as well as increasing robustness under even denser particulate conditions. Additional research will also address long-duration missions through multi-robot cooperation and improved energy management, and the integration of semantic mapping" [p.5-6].
