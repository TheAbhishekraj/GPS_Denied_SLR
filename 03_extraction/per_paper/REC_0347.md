## 1. Bibliographic Metadata
Masthead: "IEEE SENSORS JOURNAL, VOL. 23, NO. 15, 1 AUGUST 2023" and DOI "10.1109/JSEN.2023.3289313" [p.1]. Authors: Zhe Yue (Henan Polytechnic University), Chengkai Tang (Northwestern Polytechnical University), Yuting Gao (Xi'an University of Science and Technology), all in China [p.1].

## 2. Problem Statement
"Visual-inertial odometry (VIO) has been widely applied in the autonomous navigation and positioning of unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) and unmanned ground vehicles (UGVs) in global navigation satellite system (GNSS)-denied environments." [p.1]
"However, existing VIO filtering algorithms have the defects of low positioning accuracy and weak robustness, especially in complex and changeable environments." [p.1]

## 3. Motivation
The paper targets the accuracy-robustness trade-off in filter-based VIO: "CMSCKF has better filtering accuracy but poorer robustness, while HCMSCKF has better robustness but lower filtering accuracy." [p.2]
It proposes an adaptive scheme to combine both advantages [p.2].

## 4. GPS-Denied Context
OUTDOOR / GROUND-VEHICLE VALIDATION
The stated application is UAV/UGV navigation in GNSS-denied environments [p.1], but the real-world validation uses a public ground-vehicle KITTI sequence [p.9].

## 5. Proposed Method
Three-stage robust adaptive VIO filter (AHCMSCKF) [p.2]:
1) characterize VIO system uncertainty using limited-memory exponential weighting;
2) introduce the H-infinity criterion into CMSCKF to obtain HCMSCKF;
3) switch/weight CMSCKF and HCMSCKF according to three uncertainty stages inspired by the IGG III model, with thresholds rho1 = 1.5 and rho2 = 30 [p.7].
The measurement model uses epipolar and trifocal tensor geometry rather than reconstructing 3-D feature points [p.3].

## 6. System Architecture
Real VIO testbed: "the OXTS RT300 [real-time kinematic (RTK)] GPS/INS integrated navigation system and Point Gray Flea 2 grayscale camera (FL2-14S3M-C), which have been time synchronized" [p.8-9].
UAV-specific onboard compute architecture is NOT_REPORTED.

## 7. Experimental Setup
- real_or_sim: BOTH
- environment: numerical simulation plus KITTI vehicle sequence "2011_10_03_drive_0027" in residential areas [p.8-9]
- baselines: pure INS, CMSCKF, HCMSCKF, and VINS-Mono with/without loopback detection [p.8-9]
- dataset: KITTI public real-world dataset [p.9]
- metrics: maximum position error (MPE), maximum attitude error (MAE), and RMSE of position and attitude [p.9]
Ground truth: RTK-corrected OXTS RT300 GPS/INS with "position accuracy of 2 cm and attitude accuracy of 0.1 deg" [p.9].

## 8. Key Quantitative Results
Reported on [p.9]:
- Pure INS: MPE 2486.91 m, MAE 14.3507 deg; position RMSE 1053.04 m, attitude RMSE 6.8726 deg.
- CMSCKF: MPE 18.5498 m, MAE 2.4990 deg; position RMSE 11.6148 m, attitude RMSE 1.5543 deg.
- HCMSCKF: MPE 11.4815 m, MAE 1.5739 deg; position RMSE 6.9518 m, attitude RMSE 1.0047 deg.
- AHCMSCKF: MPE 8.0759 m, MAE 0.9838 deg; position RMSE 4.7401 m, attitude RMSE 0.6479 deg.
The paper states AHCMSCKF improves position and attitude accuracy by 35.36% over CMSCKF, and by 31.81% and 35.51% over HCMSCKF, respectively [p.9].

## 9. Ablation / Sensitivity
Compared against pure INS, CMSCKF, HCMSCKF, and VINS-Mono [p.8-9].
AHCMSCKF outperforms VINS-Mono without loopback, but VINS-Mono with loopback detection is better [p.9-10].
The authors attribute this to the epipolar/trifocal measurement model, which avoids 3-D point reconstruction and therefore cannot perform loopback detection [p.10].

## 10. Stated Limitations
The proposed filter-based VIO cannot perform loopback detection because of its epipolar/trifocal constraints [p.10].
HCMSCKF is described as having "certain conservatism and shortcomings" [p.9].

## 11. Future Work
"we would conduct future work on increasing the correlation loop detection to improve the positioning accuracy based on a slight increase in the computational complexity of the system." [p.10]
