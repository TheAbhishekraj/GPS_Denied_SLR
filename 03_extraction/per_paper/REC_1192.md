## 2. Problem Statement
"This paper presents an approach to localize a UAV in indoor environments using only a quasi-taut tether." [p.1]

## 3. Motivation
"In indoor GPS-denied environments, UAV localization usually depends on vision-based methods combined with inertial sensing" [p.1]

## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: Catenary-based tether localizer
Category: OTHER
"using the tether's sensory feedback, which is fed into a catenary-based mechanics model, to localize the UAV" [p.1]
