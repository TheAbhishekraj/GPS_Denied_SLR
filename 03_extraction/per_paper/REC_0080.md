## 3. Abstract / TL;DR
This paper presents a fully vision-based autonomous UAV navigation system for cluttered, GPS-denied environments. The drone is equipped with an omnidirectional stereo-vision setup (four front/side-facing stereo cameras and one downward-facing stereo camera) built with Ambarella CV1 automotive chips. These sensors enable a sparse optical flow-based visual odometry (for altitude and motion estimation) and dense 3D obstacle mapping via a local 3D confidence grid aligned to the drone's center. An improved version of the A* algorithm (incorporating height costs and safety margins) is used for 3D trajectory planning. The system was tested in indoor real-world scenarios.

## 4. Environment & Platform
- **Environment:** Indoor real-world test environments (cluttered lab space/corridors) [p.5]
- **Platform:** Custom large quadcopter. "we developed an extended-size drone capable of supporting the needed computing hardware and sensing equipment". Sensors mounted on a custom 3D-printed structure [p.3, p.5].

## 5. Navigation/Localisation Method
- **Approach:** Stereo visual odometry and omnidirectional 3D occupancy grid mapping.
- **Methodology:** Localization: The downward-facing stereo pair provides altitude (via disparity) and horizontal motion (via optical flow feature tracking of FAST keypoints). The forward-facing cameras also compute optical flow to prevent drift in vertical position estimates. Together, this acts as a form of visual odometry [p.2]. Mapping: An egocentric 360-degree 3D grid consisting of 20x20x20 cm voxels is maintained. The drone's 5 stereo modules constantly populate this grid, moving and rotating it to match the VIO estimated displacement [p.2-4]. An improved A* planner (A*) searches this 3D grid considering path length, distance to obstacles, and altitude changes [p.4-5].
- **Type:** Localisation + Mapping + Path Planning

## 6. System Architecture
- **Platform:** Custom extended-size quadcopter with high payload capacity [p.3,5]
- **Sensors:** 5 stereo setups (1 front, 2 side, 1 back, 1 down). Each setup uses two 1080p RGB sensors (total 10 cameras) running at 30 fps [p.3].
- **Compute:** 5 Ambarella CV1 automotive-grade chips (one for each stereo setup). Each chip features built-in stereo-matching hardware. A main master PC is likely present since chips communicate over Ethernet UDP, but isn't strictly detailed [p.3-4].
- **Flight control:** Not explicitly detailed (focus is on the CV payload)
- **Power:** The visual payload runs on a separate battery, drawing ~25-30 W total [p.4]

## 9. Ablation / Sensitivity
- Explored weighting ratios between distance factor and height variation factor in the modified A* algorithm, showcasing different trajectory behaviors (e.g., passing under vs over an obstacle depending on height penalties) [p.5, Fig.5].

## 10. Stated Limitations
- **Textureless environments:** The mapping dependency on stereo matching makes it vulnerable in featureless or low-contrast regions where disparity fails. [p.6]
- **Heavy payload:** Required an "extended-size drone" to carry the 5 stereo pairs and processing boards. [p.3]
- **Egocentric mapping only:** The map is limited to a 10m moving window around the drone; it does not build or utilize a permanent global map [p.4].

## 11. Future Work
- Sensor fusion for textureless mapping: "A depth map estimated from a sequence of monocular images and the currently computed disparity map could be fused together in order to obtain an accurate and rich depth information" to overcome stereo matching weaknesses in featureless areas [p.6].

## 15. Notes
- Replaced mechanical LiDAR with a 360-degree array of Ambarella CV1 automotive SoC based stereo cameras.
- The planning algorithm is a 3D A* variant that heavily weights safe distances to obstacles, ensuring the drone picks the safest 3D gap.
- Navigation relies entirely on optical flow and FAST feature tracking; no IMU or separate SLAM filtering (like EKF or g2o) is explicitly detailed in the localization section—just frame-to-frame displacement accumulation.

## 18. Best Combination & Accuracy
### 18.1 Best sensor combination
5x Stereo Camera setups (10x 1080p sensors total covering 360 deg azimuth + down) processed by 5x Ambarella CV1 chips [p.3]
### 18.2 Best algorithm
FAST feature tracking and sparse optical flow for frame-to-frame VIO; confidence-based 3D ego-grid mapping (10m radius, 20cm resolution) updated with VIO translation; Modified A* for 3D trajectory generation with safety margins. [p.2-4]
### 18.3 Best accuracy (metric + value + unit + [p.N])
Indoor horizontal translation error < 10 cm over 3 meters; Altitude estimation error < 2 cm over 2 meters [p.5]
### 18.4 Baseline comparison at best configuration
NO_BASELINE algorithm comparison
### 18.5 Conditions of best accuracy
Indoor laboratory environment with visually textured obstacles (chairs, human mannequin) [p.5]
### 18.6 Trade-offs
Trades drone size/payload capacity to carry 5 independent full stereo camera + SoC assemblies; avoids active LiDAR but requires well-textured environments [p.3-6]
### 18.7 Generalization limits
Tested in a highly controlled indoor laboratory. Fails in environments without texture, and maintaining the global map over large ranges is impossible since the map is an egocentric 10-meter scrolling window [p.4, p.6].
