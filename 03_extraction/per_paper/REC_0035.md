> Compares SLAM front-ends for a ground robot by evaluating trajectory accuracy against a VICON motion-capture reference. The stated comparison reports an average translation error of 0.071 m for Hector SLAM against 0.059 m for Cartographer SLAM, so the two explainable front-ends are separated by roughly one centimetre on this trajectory.

## 2. Problem setting

## 3. Historical context
