## 2. Problem Statement
"Nevertheless, modern navigation systems for UAVs are not able to guarantee the precision of pose estimation when GPS is unavailable in complex environment. The IMU drifts of navigation systems can cause poor calculation accuracy of position, velocity and attitude of all UAVs in the group within dozens of seconds." [p.1]
"To solve this problem, this paper puts forward a new cooperative navigation method based on adaptive Cubature Kalman Filter which shares the relative observations between the UAVs and fuses these data with direct measurements from IMU to obtain better navigation performance." [p.1]

## 3. Motivation
"Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs), when used in a formation setting, can be more advantageous. The concerted operation of UAV formations has many potential applications, such as cooperative reconnaissance, formation combat and search and rescue in mountainous regions." [p.1]
"Unfortunately, GPS signals may be unavailable in complex conditions due to GPS jamming, lack of line of sight, hardware failure, etc. Since the process of obtaining pose estimates by simply integrating the measurements from IMU will lead to significant deviations over time, UAVs cannot only rely on IMU for navigation." [p.2]
"On the basis of Rajnikant Sharma's research work, a novel cooperative navigation method based on adaptive Cubature Kalman Filter (CKF) is proposed in this paper, which shares the relative observations between the UAVs and fuses these data with the IMU measurements from other UAVS within the group to obtain better navigation performance." [p.2]

## 5. Proposed Method
"In these cases, the navigation problem is not resolved by dealing with the pose estimation problems of each vehicle independently, but fusing complex information from different vehicles of the group, which means cooperative navigation [1]." [p.1]
Two filters are compared: "two filtering algorithms based on Extended Kalman Filter (EKF) and adaptive Cubature Kalman Filter (CKF) respectively, are adopted in section 3 for cooperative navigation." [p.2]

## 9. Ablation / Sensitivity
Three-way comparison is the study's ablation: IMU-only (method 1), Sharma's cooperative method (method 2) and the proposed adaptive-CKF method (method 3) [p.5].
Filter choice is also contrasted within the method: "two filtering algorithms based on Extended Kalman Filter (EKF) and adaptive Cubature Kalman Filter (CKF) respectively, are adopted in section 3 for cooperative navigation." [p.2]
The authors' design motivation for the adaptive filter is that "the IMU drifts of navigation systems can cause poor calculation accuracy of position, velocity and attitude of all UAVs in the group within dozens of seconds" [p.1].
