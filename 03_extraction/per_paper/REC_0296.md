## 2. Problem Statement
"This practical study presented a dedicated design for indoor hovering that is more precise than using GPS positioning. We adapted optical flow sensors and ultrasound sensors to comply indoor/outdoor hover mode." [p.1]
"This work start from the design and assembly of the quadrotor equipped with optical flow sensor to achieve hovering indoor." [p.5]

## 3. Motivation
"The issues about navigation precision and indoor hovering of micro aerial vehicles (MAV) have been discussed frequently nowadays." [p.1]
"GPS is based on satellite which can provide relatively consistent accuracy navigation; however, the satellite signals would be lost in city canyon or indoor environments." [p.1]
"The drift of inertial navigation system (INS) will lead to large navigation error when a low-cost INS is used in microaerial vehicles (MAV)." [p.5]
"Although providing useful navigation information, optical flow algorithms are usually time consuming." [p.1]

## 5. Proposed Method
"In this paper, an optical flow sensor/INS/magnetometer integrated navigation system is proposed for micro aerial vehicles (MAV) [1]-[2], where optical flow sensor is used to measure the velocity and position of the MAV" [p.1]
"The scheme, which is based on extended Kalman filter, combines INS and optical flow information to estimate the velocity and position of MAV. The gyro, accelerator, and magnetometer information are fused together to estimate the MAV attitude when the MAV is at static state or uniformly moving state; and the gyro only is used to estimate the MAV attitude when the MAV is accelerating or decelerating." [p.5]
Optical flow principle: "Optical flow estimation is based on SAD block matching. Dedicated integer vector instructions of the Cortex M4 are used. They allow for the computation of the SAD value of four pixels in parallel within a single CPU clock. The SAD value of an 8x8 pixel block is calculated within a search area of +/-4 pixels in both directions. The position of the minimum SAD block value out of the 81 candidates is taken as the flow value" [p.2]
Ultrasound role: "An ultrasonic range sensor is used to measure the distance towards the scene and to scale optical flow values to metric velocity values." [p.1-2]
Method-category note: OPTICAL_FLOW (EKF fusion of optical flow, INS and magnetometer).

## 6. System Architecture
- Platform: "a customized MAV as shown in Fig.6" [p.4] - a quadrotor with four wings/motors [p.3]
- Flight controller: "PX4 autopilot is an open-source autopilot system oriented toward inexpensive autonomous aircraft. ... The newer Pixhawk controller implemented comes with built-in gyroscopes, accelerometers and pressure sensors" [p.2]
- Sensors: "Optical flow sensor module PX4Flow [8, 9]: Sold by 3D Robotics Inc, PX4Flow is an open-source instrument to assist the hovering of UAV indoor." [p.2]; "We use the manufacturer Maxbotix's MB1202 I2CXL-MaxSonar to return the data of distance and to do wide beam detection, reliable people detection, or reliable small obstacle detection." [p.2]
- Software stack: flight control board plus ground stations - "Mission Planner is an open source Ground Control System (GCS)" [p.2] and "QGroundControl is an operator control unit / ground control software for micro air vehicles" [p.2]
- Interfaces: "This difference of displacement is transmitted to the flight control by I2C and the flight control will signal the motors of the four wings to control the movement of MAV" [p.3]

## 9. Ablation / Sensitivity
The single swept variable is illumination: indoor versus outdoor/noon versus outdoor/evening [p.4]; "In conclusion, the performance of the optical flow sensor is closely related to the light according to the figures shown." [p.5]
Attitude estimation logic is also mode-dependent rather than tuned: gyro/accelerometer/magnetometer fusion applies "when the MAV is at static state or uniformly moving state; and the gyro only is used to estimate the MAV attitude when the MAV is accelerating or decelerating." [p.5]

## 10. Stated Limitations
"The principle of optical flow sensor is based on the continuous shooting and calculation of the displacement difference between two successive pictures." [p.3]
"In order to further improve this operation, an independent distance detection module is required. Besides, the fine tuning of the focus of the camera and the data accumulation and adaptation by the firmware are also critical to achieve this function" [p.3]
"The measurement noises of ultrasonic rangefinder are large" is given as the general sensor limitation motivating the fusion [p.1].
