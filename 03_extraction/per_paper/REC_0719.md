## 2. Problem Statement
"Focusing on the low-precision attitude of a current small unmanned aerial rotorcraft at the landing stage, the present paper proposes a new attitude control method for the GPS-denied scenario based on the monocular vision." [p.1]

## 3. Motivation
"However, buildings and other obstacles in urban environments can easily disturb the GPS signal and even cause the signal to get lost due to electromagnetic noise from active emitters and multi-path effects." [p.1]
