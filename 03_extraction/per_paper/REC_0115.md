## 3. Abstract / TL;DR
Presents a low computational approach for UAV navigation in GPS-denied environments using INS as primary and UWB as secondary sensor. Reduces computational load by avoiding conventional complex EKF filters via an "ultra-light-coupling fusion architecture".
