## 2. Problem Statement
"The loss of GNSS signals during a mission can occur due to multiple reasons: navigation in hilly terrain, presence of jammers, low signal patches, faulty sensors, etc. This can be devastating for the UAV and the mission and often leads to abortion of a critical mission." [p.1].

## 3. Motivation
"The aim of this paper to present a novel, computationally lighter approach to outdoor GNSS denied navigation applicable at altitudes >50m using only the most common and standard payload: barometer, flight computer and a monocular camera." [p.2].
