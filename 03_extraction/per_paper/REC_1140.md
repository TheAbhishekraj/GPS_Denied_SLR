## 2. Problem Statement
"The use of Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs) for bridge inspection has gained popularity recently; however, accurately localising the UAV in GPS-denied areas is still challenging, which hinders the development of fully autonomous UAV-assisted bridge inspection solutions." [p.1]

## 3. Motivation
"Bridges are critical components of the infrastructure system, which are subject to deterioration during their lifecycle and may experience severe structural failure without proper maintenance" [p.1]

## 4. GPS-Denied Context
MIXED
"enclosed GPS-denied areas underneath bridge girders" [p.2]

## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: FMC-SVIL (Fiducial Marker-Corrected Stereo Visual-Inertial Localisation)
Category: HYBRID
"This paper proposes a fiducial marker-corrected stereo visual-inertial localisation (FMC-SVIL) method, running on a resource-constrained onboard computer, to estimate UAVs global pose underneath bridge girders." [p.1]

## 6. System Architecture
- Platform: custom quadcopter (carbon fibre frame)
  "Body: Carbon Fibre Frame" [p.15]
- Sensors: Intel RealSense D455 stereo camera, IMU
  "Since the proposed method for UAV localisation requires one stereo camera and IMU for pose estimation, Intel RealSense Camera D455 was selected" [p.15]
- Compute: Nvidia Jetson Xavier NX
  "The onboard computer used is Nvidia Jetson Xavier NX" [p.15]
- Communication: NOT_REPORTED

## 7. Experimental Setup
- real_or_sim: BOTH
  "The proposed method was evaluated and validated through laboratory, simulation, and real-world experiments." [p.18]
- environment: concrete girder bridge (8 spans, ~200m total, 25m between piers, 7.5m pier height)
  "It contains eight spans for a total length of about 200 m." [p.15]
- trials: 6 flight tests (3 sunny, 3 cloudy)
  "we conducted the test in both sunny and cloudy weather conditions. For each weather condition, we conducted three separate tests, resulting in a total of 6 tests." [p.15]
- baselines: VINS-Fusion, ORB-SLAM3
  "its performance and computational cost were benchmarked against two prominent vision-based SLAM algorithms" [p.15]
- dataset: Self-collected

## 9. Ablation / Sensitivity
"the proposed algorithm reduced the UAV localisation error by approximately 0.642 m in the x direction and 1.293 m in the z direction during the first correction" [p.15]
"the RMSE of APE is reduced to one-third from the SVIO to the proposed FMC-SVIL algorithm" [p.15]

## 10. Stated Limitations
"the maximum error in the x direction and y direction using the improved ATM algorithm can be restricted below 0.1 m when the markers bundle is placed 5 m away from the camera" [p.11] (note: this is a positive statement; actual limitation:) "the measurement error can be significantly reduced by around 50% with less dispersion by employing a markers bundle" [p.11]

## 11. Future Work
"we aim to refine our fusion techniques by investigating adaptive weight strategies for effectively combining measurements from both ATM and SVIO modules" [p.19]
"we will address potential errors in determining fiducial marker poses, integrating comprehensive error modelling directly into the fusion module" [p.19]
