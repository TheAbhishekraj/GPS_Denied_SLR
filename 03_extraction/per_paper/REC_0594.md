## 2. Problem Statement
"Navigation of unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) in GPS- denied environment becomes increasingly critical." [p.1]

## 3. Motivation
"Since the UAVs often take off and land in different positions, it may not revisit the same scene. Thus, it may be difficult to detect loop closures as in simultaneous localization and mapping (SLAM)." [p.1]

## 4. GPS-Denied Context
MIXED
"We propose a framework for Google Map aided UAV navigation in GPS- denied environment." [p.1]

## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: HOP (HOG + OF + PF)
Category: VISION_OBJECT
"We propose a framework for Google Map aided UAV navigation in GPS- denied environment. Geo- referenced navigation provides drift- free localization and does not require loop closures. The UAV position is initialized via correlation, which is simple and efficient. We then use optical flow to predict its position in subsequent frames. During pose tracking, we obtain inter- frame translation either by motion field or homography decomposition, and we use HOG features for registration on Google Map. We employ particle filter to conduct a coarse to fine search to localize the UAV." [p.1]

## 10. Stated Limitations

## 11. Future Work
"For future research directions, we will install a gimbal to stabilize the camera against wind and vibration, and a thermal camera for navigation at night. Subsequently, we will perform more evaluation on challenging environments, including day and night conditions. We will also use HOP to give state feedback to an actual system." [p.5-6]
