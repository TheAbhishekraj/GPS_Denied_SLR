## 1. Bibliographic Metadata
Title and authors [p.1]. Masthead: "2021 International Conference on Unmanned Aircraft Systems (ICUAS)", Athens, Greece, June 15-18, 2021; DOI "10.1109/ICUAS51884.2021.9476790" [p.1]. Affiliation: University of Bonn, Germany [p.1].

## 2. Problem Statement
"We present a system for autonomous MAV flights in unknown environments which does not rely on global positioning systems." [p.1]

## 3. Motivation
Manual MAV control in disaster scenarios is error-prone and requires experienced pilots; environments are unknown and GNSS may be unavailable; existing autonomous navigation often relies on GNSS or pre-captured maps [p.1].

## 4. GPS-Denied Context
MIXED
Evaluated in indoor and outdoor search-and-rescue scenarios, including transitions between indoor and outdoor areas [p.1, p.6].

## 5. Proposed Method
Integrated framework: MARS LiDAR odometry using multi-resolution surfel maps and continuous-time B-Spline sliding-window registration; online voxel occupancy mapping; EKF state estimation fusing LiDAR-odometry positions with IMU orientation/acceleration at 50 Hz; state-lattice trajectory planning and time-optimal control [p.1-4].

## 6. System Architecture
MAV based on DJI Matrice 210 v2 with Intel NUC8i7BEH (i7-8559U, 32 GB RAM), Ouster OS0 3D-LiDAR, FLIR AGX thermal camera, and two Intel RealSense D455 RGB-D cameras [p.2].

## 7. Experimental Setup
- real_or_sim: REAL_WORLD
- environment: simulated CBRNE scenarios, outdoor flights, indoor industrial building, and outdoor/indoor transition [p.1, p.5-7]
- baselines: no quantitative external baseline; component benchmarks deferred to cited papers [p.5]
- dataset: custom autonomous flight data [p.5-7]
- metrics: GPS-vs-filter velocity comparison, trajectory tracking error, mission duration, reactive replanning [p.5-7]
Ground truth: outdoor GPS; no motion capture was available inside the industrial building [p.5-7].

## 8. Key Quantitative Results
LiDAR odometry runs at 10 Hz; fused EKF outputs states at 50 Hz [p.3]. Occupancy voxels are 25 cm [p.3]. Maximum flight velocity was limited to 1 m/s near structures [p.6]. The indoor building observation mission took 105 s [p.6]. Trajectory tracking error was always below 1 m in the combined outdoor/indoor scenario [p.7].

## 9. Ablation / Sensitivity
For narrow-doorway indoor flight, local multiresolution planning was disabled and uniform state lattices were used [p.6]. A newly perceived person in the doorway triggered online map update and trajectory replanning [p.6].

## 10. Stated Limitations
"No motion capture system was available to evaluate the accuracy of our localization inside the industrial building of this flight experiment." [p.7]
