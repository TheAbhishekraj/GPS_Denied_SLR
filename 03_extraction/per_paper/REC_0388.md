## 2. Problem Statement
"high-precision navigation in Global Navigation Satellite System (GNSS)-denied environments remains a critical challenge" for UAV swarms [p.1]. "comprehensive reviews dedicated to CN remain limited" despite numerous single-platform surveys [p.1].

## 3. Motivation
"swarm CN exploits intervehicle information exchange (e.g., relative measurements and state sharing) to enhance overall swarm navigation performance, including absolute positioning accuracy, relative positioning precision, consistency, and situational awareness" [p.2]. Existing surveys "remain largely confined to traditional sensor fusion paradigms" [p.2].

## 4. GPS-Denied Context
REVIEW - covers GNSS-denied cooperative navigation across indoor, urban, underground, forest, adversarial/EW, and maritime environments. No original experiments; synthesizes 300+ references [p.1-24].

## 5. Proposed Method
Structured CN framework integrating: (1) multisource information acquisition, (2) cooperative localization techniques under GNSS denial, (3) MSIF algorithms [p.1]. Fusion algorithms categorized into four classes: "mathematically derived, graph-based, filtering-based, and learning-based approaches" [p.1]. Reviews relative measurement info, CN architectures (multileader, single-leader, distributed), and swarm cooperative optimization [p.1, p.19].

## 6. System Architecture
Taxonomy of CN architectures: centralized, decentralized, federated [p.21]. Fusion levels: data-level, feature-level, decision-level [p.22-23]. Representative sensors discussed: IMU, stereo/monocular cameras, LiDAR (2D/3D), mmWave radar, UWB, optical flow, sonar, GNSS [p.3-8]. Key algorithms: EKF, UKF, IEKF, particle filters, factor graphs, federated filters, variational Bayesian methods, neural networks, reinforcement learning [p.11-18].

## 7. Experimental Setup
NOT_APPLICABLE - this is a review article. No original experiments conducted. Synthesizes findings from cited literature spanning 2004-2025 [p.1-24].

## 8. Key Quantitative Results
No original quantitative results. Cites representative findings from literature: e.g., "employing four IMUs can improve navigation performance by 30%-50%" [p.20]. Discusses resilience metrics, covariance intersection, and fault-tolerant fusion performance trade-offs qualitatively [p.19-23].

## 9. Ablation / Sensitivity
NOT_APPLICABLE for a review. However, the paper analyzes sensitivity of IMM-based fusion to transition probability matrix tuning [p.20], and discusses geometric configuration effects on CN accuracy [p.19].

## 10. Stated Limitations
"due to constraints in cost, payload, endurance, and size, a single UAV cannot carry multiple high-precision sensors simultaneously" [p.19]. "multipath effects and NLOS reception can significantly degrade the performance of GNSS-based relative navigation systems" [p.19]. Traditional distance-based CL "often ignore CN performance information" [p.19].

## 11. Future Work
"Future work should explore the integration of conventional perception sensors, such as cameras, LiDAR, and millimeter-wave radar, with UAV external auxiliary information, including kinematic and dynamic models, as well as topological information" [p.20]. Adaptive transition probabilities, resilient fusion with unconsidered submodels, and evolution toward decision-level fusion [p.20-23].

## 12. Contribution Type
REVIEW - systematic survey of multi-UAV cooperative navigation based on multisource information fusion, proposing a three-perspective analytical framework.
