## 2. Problem Statement
"In global satellite navigation system (GNSS)-denied environments, unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) navigation based on visual-inertial odometry (VIO) has been widely studied. However, existing VIO methods still suffer from some practical problems, such as image enhancement oversaturation and unreasonable weighting in backend optimization." [p.1].

## 3. Motivation
"Therefore, this article presents monocular VIO with point-line fusion and backend adaptive optimization to improve the positioning accuracy and robustness of UAV navigation systems." [p.1].

## 4. GPS-Denied Context
INDOOR (and other GNSS-denied environments)
"GNSS signals can be rendered vulnerable and unavailable in GNSS-denied environments such as indoor and underground spaces." [p.1].
