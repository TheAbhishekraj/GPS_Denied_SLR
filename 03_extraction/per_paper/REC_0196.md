## 2. Problem Statement
"Simultaneous localization and mapping (SLAM) is one of the key components of the navigation system of unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs). In this paper, a novel system that enables a UAV to navigation in a GPS-denied environment with an RGB-D (Red, Green, Blue, and Depth) camera and Inertial Measurement Unit (IMU) is presented." [p.1]

## 3. Motivation
"In addition, laser rangefinders cannot provide height and distance information outside the sensing 2D plane, which makes them less efficient in complex 3D scenes. As a result, it is difficult to localize a UAV due to its variation in altitude and motion dynamics." [p.1]

## 5. Proposed Method
"Such a system is realized via a host computer on a UAV, in which the IMU information read from the flight controller and the image information read from the RGB-D camera are fused using extended Kalman filter (EKF)." [p.1]

## 6. System Architecture
- Platform: Quadrotor UAV. [p.1]
- Sensors: RGB-D camera, IMU via flight controller. [p.1]
- Compute: Host computer on a UAV running SLAM algorithm. [p.1]
