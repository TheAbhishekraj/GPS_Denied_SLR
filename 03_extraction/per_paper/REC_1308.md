## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: NOT_REPORTED
Category: HYBRID
The paper develops a system for AUAV navigation in GPS-denied environments using multi-sensor data fusion with Extended Kalman Filter for obstacle avoidance and waypoint tracking.
"In this paper, a system has been developed that enables an AUAV flying along a predetermined route to reliably detect both fixed and moving obstacles in challenging environments where GPS signals are weak or absent, and to perform effective avoidance maneuvers to prevent potential collisions" [p.1]
