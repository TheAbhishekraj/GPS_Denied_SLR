## 2. Problem Statement
"However, navigating UAVs in environments where GPS signals are limited or unreliable, such as indoor or urban environments, presents challenges for safe and efficient operations." [p.1].

## 3. Motivation
"Vision-based obstacle avoidance has emerged as a promising solution to address this challenge as it leverages onboard cameras and advanced object detection algorithms to detect obstacles in real time and autonomously avoid them [6] [7]." [p.1].

## 4. GPS-Denied Context
INDOOR
"This paper introduces an end-to-end pipeline for obstacle avoidance in GPS-denied environments specifically designed for UAVs." [p.1].

## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: YOLO-based vision obstacle avoidance with stereo camera
Category: VISION_OBJECT
"The proposed solution leverages a stereo vision camera and a Raspberry Pi onboard unit. It offers a low-cost solution that is compact and efficient. By utilizing the YOLO (You Only Look Once) object detection algorithm [10], this approach efficiently detects and classifies objects in real time, allowing for obstacle avoidance by considering the surrounding space and adjusting navigation based on velocity." [p.1].

## 6. System Architecture
- Platform: "in-house-built drone with a payload capacity of 1kg" [p.2]
- Sensors: "stereo vision cameras (OAK-D Lite)" [p.1]
- Compute: "Raspberry Pi 4B with 4 GB RAM, 32 GB memory card, 40-pin GPIO, a powerful quad-core with processing speed of 1.2 GHz, and a 64-bit CPU" [p.2]
- Communication: "Serial communication is employed to send control commands between the Raspberry Pi and Pixhawk telemetry." [p.2]
