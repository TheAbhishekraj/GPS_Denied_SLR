## 2. Problem Statement
"When the Global Navigation Satellite System (GNSS) signal is unavailable, the performance of GNSS/Inertial Navigation System (INS) integrated navigation system will be seriously degraded. In order to compensate the performance of GNSS/INS integrated navigation system, an error compensation method based on Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) combined with Bidirectional Long Short-Term Memory (BiLSTM) network is proposed." [p.1]
"In GNSS denied environment, the position and velocity calculated by INS cannot be corrected by GNSS navigation information." [p.1]

## 3. Motivation
"Unmanned Air Vehicle (UAV) swarm technology is based on biological swarm behavior, it can accomplish a variety of complex tasks through mutual perception, interaction, information transmission and collaboration between individual UAVs[1-2]. However, this technology is dependent on high-precision absolute positioning." [p.1]
"However, the dynamics measurement error of IMU accumulates with time, so that INS cannot provide accurate position for a long time. GNSS can provide accurate position and velocity information to compensate the INS navigation error, but its refresh rate is only 10-20Hz." [p.1]
"When UAV executes ground survey missions, due to the complexity of the terrain in the unknown area and the extremely weak of GNSS signal, GNSS signal is easy to failure." [p.1]

## 5. Proposed Method
"In the proposed method, the Inertial Measurement Unit (IMU) dynamics measurement of Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV) is used as the input, the GNSS high-precision navigation information is used as the label. CNN-BiLSTM establishes the relationship between IMU dynamics measurement and GNSS high-precision navigation information through the training of a large number of input and label data, since it can achieve more accurate prediction of position increment and velocity increment." [p.1]
Model structure: "The model structure mainly includes input layer, CNN layer (one-dimensional convolution layer, pooling layer), BiLSTM layer, Dropout layer, full connection layer and output layer." [p.3]
CNN part: "CNN has multiple convolution kernels and two convolution layers with convolution kernel sizes of 20 and 10 respectively. However, the convolution operation of multiple convolution kernels in each layer will produce high-dimensional features of IMU dynamics measurement, so the pooling layer is used to reduce the feature dimension." [p.3]
BiLSTM part: "It is composed of forward LSTM and backward LSTM. BiLSTM can use the information of both forward and backward contexts to better capture the bidirectional semantic dependencies." [p.3]
Method-category note: HYBRID (deep-learning regression used to bridge INS-only operation during GNSS outages).

## 6. System Architecture
- Platform: UAV carrying a GNSS/INS integrated navigation system (IMU measurement as network input); the archived text does not name the airframe [p.1]
- Sensors: IMU ("dynamics measurement such as gyroscope data and accelerometer data provided by the Inertial Measurement Unit (IMU)") [p.1]
- Compute: "All deep learning models are implemented with Tensorflow framework and Keras interface." [p.4]
- Hyper-parameters: "The size of Conv1's convolution kernel is 20, and the step size is 1. The activation function of CNN is ReLU. The mode of pooling is Max-Pooling. The area sizes of Pooling1 and Pooling2 are 4 and 2, respectively. The Dropout layer is to reduce the occurrence of overfitting, the forgetting rate is set to 0.01. Three layers of BiLSTM are added in the model. Each layer calls 128, 64 and 32 neurons for calculation. ... Finally, Adamax algorithm is used to optimize the model, Huber is selected as the loss function." [p.4]
- Outputs: "Pdlat and Pdlon are position increment in latitude and longitude, respectively. ... Vdn and Vde represent velocity increment in north and east directions, respectively." [p.4]

## 9. Ablation / Sensitivity
Model architecture ablation: CNN-BiLSTM versus CNN-LSTM versus LSTM on four predicted quantities (Pdlat, Pdlon, Vdn, Vde) [p.4-5].
Per-quantity breakdown of the improvement: "In this experiment, CNN-BiLSTM reduces RMSE by 42.89%, 6.48%, 19.54%, 20.07% (the average is 22.25%) and MAE by 50.85%, 4.63%, 31.88%, 19.65% (the average is 26.75%) than CNN-LSTM (Respectively in 'Pdlat', 'Pdlon', 'vdn' and 'vde')." [p.5]
GNSS precision sensitivity: the fitting advantage shrinks as GNSS precision improves - "In Fig.7 (d), GNSS is in high-precision. CNN-BiLSTM increases R2 by 0.99%, 0.77%, 0.07%, 0.003% than CNN-LSTM (the average is 0.46%)" [p.5].
