## 2. Problem Statement
"Reliable navigation in GPS-denied or degraded environments remains a core challenge for Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs), as inertial-vision systems accumulate drift over time." [p.1].

## 3. Motivation
"The proposed architecture combines high-rate classical sensing with low-rate, bias-stable quantum corrections to suppress long-term drift during GNSS outages." [p.1].

## 9. Ablation / Sensitivity
"The quantum-enhanced EKF shows a noticeably narrower, and more concentrated histogram, indicating reduced variability. The improvement in drift-rate stability is approximately 1.8x relative to the conventional EKF." [p.5].

## 10. Stated Limitations
"Optomechanical and atom-based quantum sensors introduce notable SWaP penalties compared to classical MEMS devices. Although hybrid optomechanical sensors improve measurement robustness and bandwidth, their optical assemblies require tight environmental control and laser stabilization, making integration into compact UAVs difficult." [p.5].
