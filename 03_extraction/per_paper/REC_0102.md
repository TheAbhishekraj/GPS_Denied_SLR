## 3. Abstract / TL;DR
Reliable navigation in GPS-denied or degraded environments remains a core challenge as inertial-vision systems accumulate drift. This paper proposes a quantum-enhanced multi-sensor fusion framework combining cold-atom accelerometer aiding with conventional IMU, camera, and LiDAR measurements via a unified EKF.
