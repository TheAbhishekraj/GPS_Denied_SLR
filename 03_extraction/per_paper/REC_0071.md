## 2. Problem Statement
"However, as the spread and reliance upon autonomous UAV systems increases, the risk of jamming becomes a substantial safety problem." [p.1].

## 3. Motivation
"The critical flight phases for GNSS localization failure are during takeoff and landing as those take place near ground level, where GNSS signal reception is weaker and where attackers with jammers are most likely to strike." [p.1-2].

## 4. GPS-Denied Context
MIXED
"The system can replace high-accuracy GNSS techniques for precision landings on indoor and outdoor static or moving platforms, while providing better jamming resistance." [p.1].

## 5. Proposed Method

Category: OTHER

## 10. Stated Limitations
"The only problem observed was that the angular measurement was inaccurate for certain azimuth-elevation angle combinations, as - due to imperfect array calibration - a side lobe was becoming dominant over the main lobe and was favored by the digital beamformer." [p.3].

## 11. Future Work
"In a next step, a Kalman filter can be implemented to smoothen the positioning result and filter outliers. A higher Rx channel count and a higher bandwidth will further increase the accuracy. An antenna array with hemispherical field of view will allow using a single radar as landing aid independent on the UAV approach direction." [p.4].
