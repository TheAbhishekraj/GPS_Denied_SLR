## 3. Abstract / TL;DR
This paper presents a cooperative 24 GHz FMCW radar system for 3D localization of UAVs during approach and landing in GNSS-denied environments. The system uses a ground-based radar station (GBRS) and a miniaturized radar unit (MRU) on the UAV. By combining round-trip time of flight (RTOF) and direction-of-arrival (DOA) measurements from a single ground station instead of multi-node multilateration, the system achieves 3D position estimation. Tested on a tiltwing UAV with an RTK GPS system as a reference, it demonstrated an average 3D position error of around 20-31 cm.

## 4. Environment & Platform

## 5. Navigation/Localisation Method
- **Approach:** Range and bearing cooperative radar tracking.
- **Methodology:** The system computes localization relative to a ground station. A single ground-based radar station (8-channel SIMO 24 GHz FMCW with sparse planar antenna array) gets range via round-trip time of flight (RTOF) to a 2-channel transponder (MRU) on the UAV, and determines bearing (azimuth, elevation) via a 2D digital beamformer. The drone's position is then transmitted back to the drone [p.2].
- **Type:** Localisation aid / Ground-based infrastructure

## 6. System Architecture

## 10. Stated Limitations
- **Antenna sidelobes:** "the angular measurement was inaccurate for certain azimuth-elevation angle combinations, as - due to imperfect array calibration - a side lobe was becoming dominant over the main lobe and was favored by the digital beamformer. The results of test runs #1-3 are summarized in Table I (excluding the angular sectors where DOA failed)" [p.3-4]

## 11. Future Work
- "Kalman filter can be implemented to smoothen the positioning result and filter outliers" [p.4]
- "higher Rx channel count and a higher bandwidth will further increase the accuracy" [p.4]
- "antenna array with hemispherical field of view" for landing from any angle [p.4]
- "extend with multiple Rx channels to enable orientation estimation of the UAV" [p.4]

## 18. Best Combination & Accuracy
### 18.1 Best sensor combination
1 x GBRS (24 GHz FMCW SIMO radar, 8-Rx) + 1 x UAV-mounted MRU (active transponder) + FSK data link [p.2]
### 18.2 Best algorithm
FFT-based range derivation combined with 2D digital beamforming for DOA in a single iteration without multilateration [p.2-3]
### 18.3 Best accuracy (metric + value + unit + [p.N])
Mean 3D position error ||Δp|| of 19.66 cm against RTK GPS (Run 1) [p.4]
### 18.4 Baseline comparison at best configuration
NO_BASELINE
### 18.5 Conditions of best accuracy
Valid angles (no sidelobe interference), tiltwing platform in outdoor line-of-sight conditions with cooperative active transponder [p.4]
### 18.6 Trade-offs
Trades complete onboard autonomy for reliance on pre-surveyed ground infrastructure at the landing zone; requires cooperative payload on drone [p.1-2]
### 18.7 Generalization limits
Tested in outdoor rural/flight environments; susceptible to array calibration gaps where DOA fails [p.3-4]; evaluates only positional tracking capability without closing a control loop [p.2-4]
