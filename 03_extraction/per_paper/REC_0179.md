## 9. Ablation / Sensitivity

## 10. Stated Limitations
"As the unambiguous angular region of the dome array does not exceed 140° in elevation plane, there are no measurement results for drone height of less than 1 m." [p.4].

## 11. Future Work
"For further system improvement, the number of radar receive channels can be increased. It would allow to expand the angular coverage of the dome array up to 180° or more in elevation plane and enhance the AOA accuracy." [p.4].
