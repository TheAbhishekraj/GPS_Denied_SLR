## 2. Problem Statement
"A novel concept for ad-hoc landing of unmanned aerial vehicles (UAV) in GNSS-denied environment is presented in this paper." [p.1]

## 3. Motivation
"However, there are scenarios where a GPS signal is not reliable due to shadowing by obstacles or jamming. This uncertainty in localization is most critical during the UAV landing phase [4]." [p.1]

## 4. GPS-Denied Context
JAMMED
"shadowing by obstacles or jamming." [p.1]

## 5. Proposed Method
"In our concept at the beginning of the landing phase the UAV drops several active beacons down to the landing site. It enables the UAV to localize itself via distance and angle information to the deployed beacons." [p.1]

## 6. System Architecture
- Platform: Octocopter primary drone (DJI S1000). [p.4]
- Sensors: 24 GHz secondary radar sensors / active beacons. [p.1]
- Compute: Intel NUC, ROS. [p.4]
