## 1. Bibliographic Metadata
Pugliese, R., & Abel, D. (2022). LiDAR Inertial Mapping of Non-Manhattan Topologies for UAV-Based Aircraft Inspection in GNSS-Denied Environments. *IFAC-PapersOnLine*, 55(38), 49-54. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ifacol.2023.01.132

## 2. Problem Statement
"Operation of civil aircraft requires mandatory regular inspection in order to detect damages to the surface structures that could lead to safety risks in the long term if not remedied." [p.1]

## 3. Motivation
"Autonomous UAV offer great potential for automating the task and significantly reducing the downtimes. Perspectively, the inspection task could be shifted to the runway and performed directly after landing" [p.1]

## 4. GPS-Denied Context
INDOOR
"measurements in a GNSS-denied hangar environment with a Boeing 737-500" [p.1]

## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: Modified LIOM with cylinder feature detection
Category: LIDAR
"we propose a novel modification and extension of LIOM to make it suitable for the application to datasets with strong non-Manhattan characteristics and for the given use case in particular. More precisely, we introduce cylinder feature detection in order to make the conventional plane and edge features robust to homogeneous curved surfaces such as aircraft fuselages." [p.2]
