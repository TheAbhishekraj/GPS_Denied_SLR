## 2. Problem Statement
"In this paper, a method for UAV positioning in GPS-denied environment through a multi-sensor fusion algorithm is proposed." [p.1]

## 3. Motivation
"traditional UAV positioning and navigation system cannot respond to the fault in time, especially in the field search and rescue, battlefield reconnaissance and other scenes. The shielding from mountains and forests, as well as the complex and changeable battlefield environmental, pose challenges to the traditional UAV positioning technology which highly relies on global positioning system." [p.1]

## 4. GPS-Denied Context
FOREST
"The shielding from mountains and forests, as well as the complex and changeable battlefield environmental" [p.1]

## 5. Proposed Method
"This method uses IMU and UWB to obtain acceleration and position information respectively. After processing the two sets of data, the position data is obtained, and the more accurate position data is obtained through the Kalman filter algorithm" [p.1]
