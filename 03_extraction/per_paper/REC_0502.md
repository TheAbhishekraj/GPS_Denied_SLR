## 2. Problem Statement
"However, the dynamic perspectives of uncrewed aerial vehicles (UAVs), compounded by Global Navigation Satellite System (GNSS) signal interference and the accumulation of localization errors, pose significant challenges to existing monocular SLAM methods in complex environments." [p.1]

## 3. Motivation
"Owing to the inherently weak nature of GNSS signals, they are highly susceptible to interference and spoofing attacks, posing significant challenges to system security [8]." [p.1]

## 4. GPS-Denied Context
MIXED
"To further enhance localization performance in GNSS- denied scenarios, we develop a dynamic frame interpolation and tracking approach based on 3- D Gaussian rendering." [p.1]

## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: LD3DGS-SLAM
Category: HYBRID
"We propose LD3DGS- SLAM, a novel framework that integrates monocular SLAM, GNSS, and 3- D Gaussian splatting (3DGS) to enhance UAV- based localization and mapping. The system integrates traditional geometric feature- based localization with the multiview synthesis capability of 3- D Gaussian rendering, achieving a breakthrough in accuracy while enhancing robustness." [p.1]

## 6. System Architecture
- Platform: "We constructed an aerial experimental platform using DJI MA100 and Phantom 4 RTK drones" [p.10]
- Sensors: "a monocular camera" [p.10], "RTK GNSS" [p.10], "IMU 200HZ" [p.10]
- Compute: "the onboard unit executes real- time visual/GNSS fusion estimation, prioritizing rendering speed for live localization and tracking, while more resource- intensive map training and updates are offloaded to the ground server." [p.18]
- Communication: "the system can rely entirely on the visual map without requiring any data communication with servers or reception of radio signals—including GNSS data." [p.18]
