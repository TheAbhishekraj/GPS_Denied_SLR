## 2. Problem Statement
"However, UAVs often lack reliable heading angle measurements, especially in indoor scenarios, space, and GNSS-denied environments, posing an additional observability challenge on range-based relative localization." [p.1].

## 3. Motivation
"Decentralized and autonomous control of Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV) swarms is a key enabler for cooperative systems and infrastructure-less formation flights." [p.1].

## 4. GPS-Denied Context

Method name: Relative Pose Estimation Method with Approximate Maximum-Likelihood Trilateration

## 9. Ablation / Sensitivity
"Formulating the relative localization as an optimization problem makes our solution immune to convergence issues observed with EKFs [5], [10], [12]." [p.8].

## 10. Stated Limitations
"Although the drones manage to keep the formation throughout the experiment, the formation frame rotates w.r.t. the mocap frame. This happens because the drones are mainly stationary, and therefore their heading angle becomes unobservable." [p.7].
