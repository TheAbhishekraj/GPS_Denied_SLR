## 2. Problem Statement
GPS-denied navigation fails under occlusion, spoofing and jamming, across both indoor and outdoor settings. Quote: "Indoor environments such as warehouses and industrial facilities suffer from GPS degradation caused by structural occlusions, while outdoor scenarios including forests and flat terrains lack reliable localization cues." [p.1]

## 3. Motivation
Commercial GNSS receivers are demonstrably vulnerable to RF interference, motivating a non-GNSS navigation stack. Quote: "These conditions result in unreliable localization, motivating navigation solutions that do not rely solely on satellite-based positioning [1]." [p.1]

## 4. GPS-Denied Context
MIXED (indoor occlusion + outdoor spoofing/jamming tested). Quote: "The testing protocol evaluated both spoofing and jamming attacks to establish baseline vulnerability metrics for autonomous systems." [p.2]

## 5. Proposed Method
Dual-strategy vision-centric framework: indoor VSLAM (RTAB-Map) with EKF fusion of IMU + visual odometry and YOLO semantic association; outdoor VSLAM-based 2D digital twin; GUI-driven single-click navigation. method_category: **HYBRID** (VSLAM + inertial EKF fusion). Quote: "The proposed approach employs a dual-strategy navigation framework: (i) Visual Simultaneous Localization and Mapping (VSLAM) with 3D point cloud generation for structured indoor environments, and (ii) a VSLAM-based 2D digital twin representation for outdoor environments, integrating visual data with inertial and altitude measurements." [p.1]

## 6. System Architecture
- Platform: GATHI custom quadcopter. Quote: "A custom-built quadcopter platform, GATHI (GPS-denied Aerial Tracking and Homing Intelligence), shown in Fig. 1, was developed using a Holybro X500 V2 carbon-fiber frame." [p.3]
- Sensors: ZED 2i stereo camera (visual-inertial odometry, depth); integrated IMU. Quote: "The ZED 2i stereo camera [11] serves as the primary sensing modality for visual-inertial odometry and 3D mapping." [p.3]

## 18. Best Combination & Accuracy
18.1 Best sensor combination: ZED 2i stereo camera (depth + integrated IMU) for visual-inertial odometry and 3D mapping, fused via EKF with IMU data. Quote: "The ZED 2i stereo camera [11] serves as the primary sensing modality for visual-inertial odometry and 3D mapping." [p.3]
18.2 Best algorithm: RTAB-Map VSLAM with EKF sensor fusion and YOLO-based semantic node association. Quote: "For indoor environments, the framework employs VSLAM using RTAB-Map for real-time 3D point cloud generation and feature tracking. An Extended Kalman Filter (EKF) is used to fuse Inertial Measurement Unit (IMU) data with visual odometry" [p.2]
18.3 Best accuracy: mean lateral deviation **3.95 cm** (SIL, 100 m flight) and **4.10 cm** (HIL, 50 m flight); max 10 cm (SIL) / 15 cm (HIL). Quote: "The system demonstrated consistent path-following accuracy in simulation and real-world experiments, with mean lateral deviations below 5 cm in both cases." [p.4]
18.4 Baseline comparison: NO_BASELINE — no comparative method is evaluated; the GPS attacks serve as the threat baseline. Quote: "The testing protocol evaluated both spoofing and jamming attacks to establish baseline vulnerability metrics for autonomous systems." [p.2]
18.5 Conditions: indoor warehouse-like environment with static obstacles (storage racks, support columns) and dynamic elements (cranes, forklifts, personnel); SIL 100 m route and HIL 50 m route on the GATHI platform. Quote: "included both static obstacles (storage racks and support columns) and dynamic elements (moving cranes, forklifts, and personnel)." [p.3]
18.6 Trade-offs: dense 3D point clouds are computationally expensive and lighting-sensitive; the lighter 2D digital twin loses accuracy under poor illumination and feature-sparse conditions. Quote: "Simplified 2D digital twin approaches reduce computational load but suffer from degraded performance under poor illumination and feature-sparse conditions [9]." [p.4]
18.7 Generalization limits: less suitable for large-scale outdoor environments; degradation under lighting variation and altitude change. Quote: "VSLAM-based navigation relying on dense 3D point clouds is computationally expensive and less suitable for large-scale outdoor environments, while also being sensitive to lighting variations and altitude changes [8], [10]." [p.4]
