## 1. Bibliographic Metadata
Gallo, E., & Barrientos, A. (2022). Reduction of GNSS-Denied inertial navigation errors for fixed wing autonomous unmanned air vehicles. *Aerospace Science and Technology*, 120, 107237.

## 2. Problem Statement
"The extreme dependency of an autonomous UAV (Unmanned Air Vehicle) inertial navigation to the presence of GNSS (Global Navigation Satellite System) signals, without which it incurs in a slow but unavoidable position drift that may ultimately lead to the loss of the platform" [p.1]

## 3. Motivation
"The main objective of this article is to improve the GNSS-Denied navigation capabilities of autonomous fixed wing low SwAP vehicles, so in case GNSS signals become unavailable, they can continue their mission or safely fly to a predetermined recovery location." [p.4]
