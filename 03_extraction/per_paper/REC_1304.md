## 5. Proposed Method
Method name: Formation-constrained cooperative localization (FCL)
Category: COOPERATIVE
The method integrates known formation geometry into localization. It uses a formation-constrained greedy node activation (GNA) strategy, formation-constrained listener localization incorporating Chan's algorithm with AOA refinement, and global formation constraint optimization via gradient descent.
"we propose a formation-constrained cooperative localization method to improve accuracy by integrating known formation geometry into the localization process." [p.1]

## 6. System Architecture
- Platform: UAV swarm (N agents)
- Sensors: UWB for TOA/AOA measurements
- Compute: NOT_REPORTED
- Communication: Backbone-listener scheme
"The network adopts a backbone-listener scheme [27]. Backbone nodes actively transmit and receive wireless signals to establish mutual constraints; listener nodes passively receive signals for localization, avoiding redundant communication." [p.4]
