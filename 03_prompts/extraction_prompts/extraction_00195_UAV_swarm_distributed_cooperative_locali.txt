TITLE: UAV swarm distributed cooperative localization in GNSS-denied environments: an ICMC and ACPM Fusion approach
AUTHORS: Yue W.; Wang Y.; Zhang X.; Wang L.; Liu Z.; Li L.; Zhu L.
YEAR: 2026
VENUE: Ad Hoc Networks
DOI: 10.1016/j.adhoc.2026.104363
ABSTRACT: With the widespread use of Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs) in urban environments, achieving high-precision localization, particularly in Global Navigation Satellite System (GNSS)-denied environments, presents a significant challenge. Existing localization methods largely rely on centralized data processing, which often leads to issues such as information redundancy, high computational complexity, and limited real-time responsiveness. This paper proposes a UAV swarm collaborative localization strategy, termed I-ACPM, based on Imperialist Competitive Mechanism-based Clustering (ICMC) and the Ant Colony Pheromone Mechanism (ACPM). The ICMC is employed for multi-stage clustering of the UAV swarm, enhancing communication efficiency and reducing information redundancy. Meanwhile, the ACPM is used to fuse localization information both within and between clusters, improving system robustness and localization accuracy. Simulation results demonstrate that I-ACPM significantly enhances localization accuracy, particularly in low-noise environments, with performance approaching the Cramér-Rao Lower Bound (CRLB). Compared to the traditional Maximum Likelihood Estimation (MLE) algorithm, I-ACPM offers substantial improvements in both accuracy and interference resistance. © 2026 Elsevier B.V.
