TITLE: Autonomous Mapping and Navigation Unit for Aerial Robots
AUTHORS: Baiao J.; Prado D.; Soares L.; Soares G.; Vangasse A.; Cruz G.; Lima R.; Pimenta L.; Goncalves V.; Freitas G.
YEAR: 2025
VENUE: 2025 IEEE International Conference on Advanced Robotics, ICAR 2025
DOI: 10.1109/ICAR65334.2025.11338655
ABSTRACT: This paper presents an Autonomous Mapping and Navigation Unit (AM&NU) for multirotor platforms operating without GNSS. The unit combines a Livox Mid-360 LiDAR and an Intel RealSense D435i camera, both with internal IMU, connected to the embedded computer a Jetson Orin NX 16GB. A LiDAR-inertial SLAM module (FAST-LIO2) provides a six-degree-of-freedom pose and an incremental point-cloud map. The map is forwarded to a planner that interpolates user-defined waypoints with cubic Hermite spline segments, producing a smooth reference path. An artificial vector-field controller evaluates the current pose with respect to the path and computes body-frame velocity commands; a multirotor control law converts these commands into thrust and angular-rate inputs. The full pipeline was implemented in ROS2 and validated in simulation. Data collected during outdoor flights were processed offline to verify localization and mapping consistency. The results confirm the feasibility of using the proposed unit for autonomous navigation in GNSS-denied environments. © 2025 IEEE.
