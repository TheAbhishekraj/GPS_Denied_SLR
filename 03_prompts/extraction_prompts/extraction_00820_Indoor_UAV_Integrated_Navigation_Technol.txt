TITLE: Indoor UAV Integrated Navigation Technology Based on UWB and IMU
AUTHORS: Z. Zuo; K. Li; P. Yang; S. Zheng
YEAR: 2022
VENUE: NOT_IN_ABSTRACT
DOI: 10.1109/CAC57257.2022.10055191
ABSTRACT: To solve the problem of poor motion tracking of the indoor Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV), this paper studies the position estimation of Ultra-wideband and Inertial Measurement Units, puts forward an indoor positioning model to estimate the position of UAV, and effectively solves the problem of position tracking accuracy and error caused by NLOS (Non-Line-of-Sight, NLOS). The model uses the concept of data fusion to analyze the data of UWB and IMU by the extended Kalman filter, and then outputs the exact position of the UAV after sensor fusion. First, through the simulation of the model, the model for the position estimation of the UAV reveals the ability of continuous tracking and stability. Secondly, the calibration simulation of the UWB base station is compared with the non-calibration simulation. The results show that the calibrated UWB base station shows more accurate and robust position estimation for the unmanned aerial vehicle after multi-sensor data fusion, which verifies the rationality of multi-sensor data fusion in the proposed algorithm. Compared with existing pose estimation algorithms, the proposed algorithm is more effective for indoor unmanned aerial vehicle navigation, and provides a solution for object tracking under indoor scenes.
