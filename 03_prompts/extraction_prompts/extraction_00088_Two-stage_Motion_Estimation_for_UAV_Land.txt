TITLE: Two-stage Motion Estimation for UAV Landing under GPS-denied Condition
AUTHORS: L. Zhang; G. Yu; X. Song; P. Li; Z. Zhai; Y. Lu
YEAR: 2020
VENUE: NOT_IN_ABSTRACT
DOI: 10.1109/ICUS50048.2020.9274941
ABSTRACT: The landing of Unmanned Aircraft Vehicle (UAV) is still one of the greatest challenges during the pilotless flight. Precise and stable kinetic parameters are significant to control the drone land accurately in GPS-denied environment. This paper proposes an optimization-based motion estimation approach that integrates inertial measurements and visual observations. It runs on two flight stages. In final approach, sparse runway features extracted from infrared images, inertia data and runway geo-information are fused to estimate ego-motion; In final landing, direct sparse odometry and inertial data are used for motion estimation by optimization. A Y-12F aircraft, equipped with an infrared camera, an IMU and a flight data recorder, is modified to collect flight data for algorithm verification. Finally, the experiments on real flight data demonstrate that the accurate approach and landing pose information for fixed-wing UAV can be obtained by the proposed method under GPS-denied condition.
