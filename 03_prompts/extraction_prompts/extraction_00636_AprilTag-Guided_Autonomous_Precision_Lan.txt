TITLE: AprilTag-Guided Autonomous Precision Landing System for UAV-Quadcopter Using HuskyLens AI Camera
AUTHORS: Wiyagi R.O.; Tryatmojo B.S.; Basith S.A.; Handoko H.T.G.; Hasani M.R.A.; Mustar M.Y.
YEAR: 2025
VENUE: ICE3IS 2025 - Conference Proceedings: 5th International Conference on Electronic and Electrical Engineering and Intelligent System
DOI: 10.1109/ICE3IS66769.2025.11281319
ABSTRACT: The development of Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV) technology has driven various innovations, especially in navigation systems and precision landings. The use of conventional GPS modules still has limitations, especially in accuracy and performance in GPS-denied areas. This study proposed an autonomous precision landing system on a UAVQuadcopter using the Huskylens AI Camera as a vision edge computing device to detect and track the AprilTag fiducial marker. The system was developed by integrating Huskylens with a Raspberry Pi4B+ companion computer and implementing PID control based on visual data for landing maneuvers on the Quadcopter. The test results show that Huskylens can detect AprilTag stably with a maximum confidence rate of 1.0 at a distance of up to 250 cm in lighting conditions ranging from 15 - 3155 LUX. At a detection distance below 50 cm, the camera can achieve the highest FPS above 25 FPS. With the implementation of the Huskylens AI camera on the Quadcopter, the tracking control system can keep the marker in the camera frame during the landing process. The fastest landing time was recorded at 19 seconds from an altitude of 180 cm, while in testing at an altitude of 200 cm, the smallest position error was obtained at 6 cm on the x-axis and 5 cm on the y -axis. © 2025 IEEE.
