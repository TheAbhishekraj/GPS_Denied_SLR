TITLE: Depth Control Based on PID Using a Pressure Sensor in an Autonomous Underwater Vehicle
AUTHORS: E. C. Dursun; S. Yilmaz; S. G. Karatop
YEAR: 2025
VENUE: NOT_IN_ABSTRACT
DOI: 10.1109/ELECO69582.2025.11329270
ABSTRACT: In this study, a PID (Proportional-Integral-Derivative) control system based on pressure sensor data is designed and implemented to maintain a stable depth for an autonomous underwater vehicle. The vehicle’s instantaneous depth is continuously measured using a pressure sensor and compared to the desired depth to generate an error signal. This error is processed by the PID algorithm to produce control signals for the vehicle’s thrusters. The developed system enables the underwater vehicle to reach and maintain the target depth with fast response, stability, and minimal overshoot from various initial positions. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness and reliability of the PID-based control approach in depth regulation. This method offers a robust solution for underwater vehicles performing diving operations and maintaining fixed-depth missions.
