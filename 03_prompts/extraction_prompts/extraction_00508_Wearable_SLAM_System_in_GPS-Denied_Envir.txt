TITLE: Wearable SLAM System in GPS-Denied Environments
AUTHORS: Palustre F.; Oh P.
YEAR: 2025
VENUE: 2025 IEEE 15th Annual Computing and Communication Workshop and Conference, CCWC 2025
DOI: 10.1109/CCWC62904.2025.10903870
ABSTRACT: This paper presents a system designed for localization and mapping in GPS-denied environments, particularly in dynamic scenarios such as disaster response scenarios. Using a combination of LiDAR and stereo depth cameras, we evaluate different sensor configurations to improve mapping accuracy and real-time localization. Comparative analysis of the ZED camera configurations (ZED 2i, ZED X, and Dual ZED X Mini) demonstrated that the ZED 2i provides overall superior depth perception and accuracy, 'under varying lighting conditions and distances. Additionally, the angled LiDAR mount proved to be the most effective configuration, generating high-quality point clouds. The results suggest that careful integration of the hardware and setup can significantly improve performance in GPS-denied environments, enabling reliable mapping and localization for real-world applications such as radiation detection and search-and-rescue missions. © 2025 IEEE.
