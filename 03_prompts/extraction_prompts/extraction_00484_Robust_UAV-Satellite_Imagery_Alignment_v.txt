TITLE: Robust UAV-Satellite Imagery Alignment via Cross-Domain Descriptor Learning and Homography Optimization
AUTHORS: Xiao Y.; Wu Y.; Yang C.; Qin Z.; Yu Z.; Zheng H.
YEAR: 2026
VENUE: IEEE Transactions on Geoscience and Remote Sensing
DOI: 10.1109/TGRS.2026.3689254
ABSTRACT: Localization methods that leverage satellite maps and their associated geospatial information are critical for uncrewed aerial vehicles (UAVs) executing time-sensitive missions in global navigation satellite system (GNSS)-denied environments. UAV and satellite images exhibit substantial cross-domain differences, including appearance variations caused by temporal changes and geometric discrepancies between perspective imaging and orthorectification. However, existing methods in the UAV-satellite setting often lack effective supervision for local feature matching, which limits the cross-domain adaptation capability of feature matching models. Existing datasets also provide limited support for rigorous fine-grained registration evaluation. To address these issues, we present Co-Lo, a calibrated UAV-satellite dataset. During dataset construction, dense matching is combined with sparse keypoints to obtain reliable keypoint correspondences. An initial calibration procedure is then used to derive accurate ground-truth homography matrices for evaluation. Based on these supervisory signals, we validate the effectiveness of the proposed weakly supervised cross-domain adaptation strategy. In addition, a dual evaluation protocol is designed to decouple algorithm robustness (across texture variations) from sequence stability (along flight trajectories). Compared with representative sparse-matching and semi-dense-matching baselines, the adapted models show improved robustness in cross-domain matching. In texture-sparse scenes, they also yield relatively stable continuous localization performance.  © 1980-2012 IEEE.
