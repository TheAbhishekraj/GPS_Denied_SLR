YEAR: 2025
VENUE: NOT_IN_ABSTRACT
DOI: 10.1109/AIPIP66876.2025.11299211
ABSTRACT: To address the issue of mapping errors in traditional Gmapping, which relies solely on odometry for motion estimation, this study proposes an improved approach by fusing IMU and odometry data using an Extended Kalman Filter (EKF). The fused, more accurate pose estimates, combined with LiDAR data, are used as input to the Gmapping algorithm, significantly improving mapping accuracy. After completing the indoor map construction using Gmapping, the A* algorithm and Dynamic Window Approach (DWA) are applied to path planning and autonomous navigation for multi-point inspection. Simulation results show that the multi-sensor fusion approach improves the mapping accuracy of SLAM with an average mapping error of about 2.5%. The integration of A* and DWA enables the unmanned inspection system to dynamically adjust the path and realize reliable multi-point autonomous navigation.
