TITLE: Toward High-Precision SLAM Ground Truth in GNSS-Denied Large-Scale Environments
AUTHORS: Gong Z.; Yu A.; Xu X.; Wu Y.; Cai G.
YEAR: 2025
VENUE: IEEE Transactions on Instrumentation and Measurement
DOI: 10.1109/TIM.2025.3553230
ABSTRACT: This article introduces an efficient and highly accurate framework for generating precise trajectories to evaluate the performance of simultaneous localization and mapping (SLAM) algorithms. Traditional methods for acquiring high-precision SLAM trajectories typically rely on external high-accuracy trajectory references, such as real-time kinematic global positioning systems (RTK-GPSs) or motion capture cameras. However, obtaining high-precision trajectories through conventional means in GPS-denied indoor environments or large underground settings poses a significant challenge. To address this issue, we propose a method of high-precision point cloud matching to calibrate lower-precision point clouds, supplemented by contextual consistency optimization for efficient and accurate trajectory acquisition. We demonstrate the robustness and generalizability of our method using our own dataset, LIVOX-Indoor, with experimental evaluation results showing millimeter-level accuracy. © 1963-2012 IEEE.
