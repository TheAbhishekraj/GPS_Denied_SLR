TITLE: SkyAid: A Low-Cost Autonomous Hexacopter for Resilient Medical Supply Delivery in GPS-Denied Environments
AUTHORS: M. A. Ali; K. Mustafa; R. Kisanieh; M. Hajj-Hassan; A. Kassem; A. Cherry
YEAR: 2025
VENUE: NOT_IN_ABSTRACT
DOI: 10.1109/ICM66518.2025.11322518
ABSTRACT: In regions facing severe economic and infrastructural challenges, such as Lebanon, conventional Emergency Medical Services (EMS) are frequently hindered by logistical delays that compromise critical care. This paper presents SkyAid, a low-cost, autonomous hexacopter designed for rapid aerial delivery of medical supplies under eal-world constraints, including component scarcity and unreliable GPS. The system integrates a Pixhawk flight controller, ultrasonic obstacle avoidance, and telemetry for real-time monitoring, achieving 25-minute flight times with 2 kg payloads-demonstrated through rigorous testing of propulsion (thrust-toweight ratio: 2.44:1), navigation accuracy ($<1 ~\mathrm{m}$ delivery error), and GPS-denied operation. By leveraging off-the-shelf components (<$ 2,000 total cost) and open-source software, SkyAid provides a replicable, resilient model for EMS augmentation in resource-limited settings. Experimental results validate its operational readiness, while future work outlines pathways for enhanced autonomy (RTK GPS, AI-driven routing) and scalability (swarm coordination, extended range). This work bridges the gap between academic prototypes and commercial systems, offering a practical, field-deployable solution to address time-sensitive healthcare gaps in underserved regions.
