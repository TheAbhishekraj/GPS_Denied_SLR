ABSTRACT: Underwater navigation remains one of the most critical challenges for autonomous and remotely operated vehicles due to the absence of GPS signals. This paper introduces Acouhouse, a novel underwater acoustic architecture designed to support robust and accurate navigation through the deployment of fixed-position acoustic beacons, or “acoustic lighthouses” that emit positioning signals with absolute reference information. Unlike traditional abovewater lighthouses that rely on visual cues, Acouhouse leverages the superior propagation characteristics of sound in water to guide submerged entities with high spatial awareness. The system is scalable, low-maintenance, and suited for a wide range of applications, from oceanographic surveys to underwater infrastructure inspection.
