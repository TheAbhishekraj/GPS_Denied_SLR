TITLE: Wide-Beam Optical Beacon-Based UAV Self-Positioning

DOI: 10.1109/SIU71813.2026.11636447
ABSTRACT: We propose an optical beacon-based 3D self-positioning method for uncrewed aerial vehicles (UAVs) operating in GPS-denied environments. Following a GPS outage, the UAV flies a pre-programmed circular holding pattern at approximately constant altitude. At the same time, a high-power ground optical beacon transmitter illuminates the expected region with a wide beam. The UAV samples the received signal strength (RSS) at azimuth-uniform waypoints determined from the onboard heading estimate. The resulting RSS sequence is used to estimate the orbit center position, providing a reliable reference for self-positioning during GPS outages. We formulate this task as a nonlinear weighted least-squares (NWLS) problem and solve it using Gauss-Newton (GN) iterations. We also derive the corresponding Cramér-Rao lower bound (CRLB) as a performance benchmark. Simulation results show that the proposed estimator closely follows the CRLB. © 2026 IEEE.
