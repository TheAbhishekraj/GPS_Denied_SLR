TITLE: Evaluation of a Vision based UAV Indoor Inspection for a Well-structured Heavy Industry Environment
AUTHORS: T. -C. Hung; R. He; C. -C. Peng

DOI: 10.1109/ICCE-Taiwan62264.2024.10674055
ABSTRACT: In recent years, unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) have demonstrated their irreplaceable advantages in various commercial applications, such as package delivery, aerial inspections, terrain modeling, pesticide spraying, and vegetation coverage in agriculture. However, these applications heavily rely on the global navigation satellite system (GNSS) as a positioning source for mission planning. Clearly, for missions in indoor environments, GNSS signals may be interfered with or completely obstructed. Therefore, this paper focuses on the inspection tasks of UAVs in heavy industrial indoor environments without GNSS signals and evaluates potential solutions. The paper adopts the key concept of simultaneous localization and mapping (SLAM) as the primary solution. Specifically, visual SLAM (vSLAM) is chosen as the preferred method, primarily due to its low cost, low power consumption, and ease of subsequent system maintenance. Consequently, this note will assess and analyze the challenges that vSLAM may face and conduct a preliminary feasibility assessment for the environment. The evaluations will serve as a basis for the future development of autonomous indoor inspection by UAVs.
