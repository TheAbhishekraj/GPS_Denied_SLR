TITLE: Research on Initial Relative Position Determination Method for Unmanned Aerial Vehicles with Range-Only Measurements in GNSS-Denied Environments
AUTHORS: Cui X.; Ou L.; Zhuang Z.; Sun X.; Gong B.
YEAR: 2025
VENUE: IFAC-PapersOnLine
DOI: 10.1016/j.ifacol.2025.11.320
ABSTRACT: In the Global Navigation Satellite System (GNSS) denial environment, the relative positioning problem between members of an unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) swarm is addressed. A method for solving the initial relative position based on the combination of an Inertial Measurement Unit (IMU), barometric altimeter, and Ultra-Wideband (UWB) ranging is proposed. First, the recursive model of inertial navigation, the UWB ranging model, and the barometric altitude measurement model are established. Then, the UAV's motion trajectory is derived through the established dynamic model. The relative position magnitude at the same time instant from the inertial navigation trajectory is compared with the simulated UWB ranging to form a fitness function. Randomly generated initial relative positions are used as the input population for the genetic algorithm, and the fitness function is employed to evaluate the quality of individuals in each generation. The relative position accuracy is evaluated by calculating the error between the inertial navigation recursive simulated distance and the UWB measurement distance, while considering the barometric altitude information. Subsequently, the observability of the proposed algorithm is analyzed, and the unobservable relative motion form is presented. Finally, numerical simulation experiments are conducted, and the results demonstrate that the proposed algorithm can estimate the 3D initial relative position under various relative motion trajectories, with position errors around two meters.
