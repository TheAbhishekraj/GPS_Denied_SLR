TITLE: A Novel Scene-Matching Positioning Approach for Low-Altitude UAVs Considering Elevation Differences in Satellite Images
AUTHORS: Zhang X.; Li T.; Wang Z.; Kuang Y.
YEAR: 2026
VENUE: IEEE Internet of Things Journal
DOI: 10.1109/JIOT.2026.3664077
ABSTRACT: The precise localization of Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs) is a critical prerequisite for mission success in smart urban environments driven by the Internet of Things (IoT). Traditional Global Navigation Satellite System (GNSS)-based positioning schemes are vulnerable to signal interference and spoofing attacks, highlighting the importance of visual scene matching as a robust solution for anti-jamming localization. However, this technology faces challenges such as sensitivity to perspective variations and trajectory discontinuities. To address these limitations, this paper presents a novel visual scene matching-based UAV localization framework. First, a spatiotemporally constrained candidate map retrieval strategy is proposed, which enables efficient and accurate candidate retrieval by coupling single-frame probability estimation with a Hidden Markov Model. Second, a pose recovery algorithm based on cross-frame elevation difference constraints is introduced, by leveraging feature matches between adjacent UAV frames and between UAV frames and satellite images, the elevation information of satellite images is estimated and 2D-3D correspondences between UAV images and satellite images is established. This constructs a 3D constraint model that enables the UAV to obtain 3D coordinates independent of perspective variations, thereby maintaining localization accuracy under oblique views. Finally, a factor graph joint optimization framework is developed to fuse local relative poses with satellite-based absolute geographic poses, thereby ensuring smooth and stable localization performance. Extensive experiments on public and real-world datasets demonstrate that the proposed method significantly improves localization accuracy and validates its effectiveness in supporting collaborative IoT agent operations in GNSS-denied environments. © 2014 IEEE.
