TITLE: A Tightly Coupled Polarization Compass and VIO Integrated Navigation for Applications in GNSS-denied environments
AUTHORS: J. Li; S. Wang; P. Huang; X. Zhang
YEAR: 2022
VENUE: NOT_IN_ABSTRACT
DOI: 10.1109/ISAS55863.2022.9757349
ABSTRACT: This paper presents a tightly coupled integration scheme for the polarization compass and visual-inertial odometry (P- VIO) integration system. The polarization observations are directly integrated with the visual measurements to improve the accuracy of the VIO pose estimation with the implying heading information. The filtering-based method is adopted for the tightly coupled P- VIO fusion in this paper. In addition, we built a compact P- VIO system for practical applications. To verify effectiveness of the proposed tightly coupled P- VIO algorithm, both a simulation test using KITTI datasets and an outdoor experiment were carried out. It was found that the position error of the tightly coupled P- VIO is 1.05% of the travelled distance, which is reduced by 0.64% compared with that of the traditional VIO. And the heading accuracy is improved by 33.12%.
