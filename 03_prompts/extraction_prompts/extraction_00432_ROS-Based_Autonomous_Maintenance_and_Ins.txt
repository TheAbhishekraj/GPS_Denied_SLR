ABSTRACT: Industries increasingly rely on automation to improve operational efficiency, ensure worker safety, and reduce downtime. This paper presents a ROS-Based Autonomous Maintenance and Inspection Robot designed to navigate complex industrial environments and perform real-time safety assessments. Built using ROS2, SLAM Toolbox, and Nav2, the robot autonomously maps and plans paths around dynamic obstacles using 2D LiDAR. It integrates a YOLO-powered vision system for human and pose detection, enhancing safety monitoring in hazardous zones. Equipped with DHT11 and MQ2 sensors, the robot detects temperature, humidity, and gas/smoke levels, enabling early risk identification. All sensor data is timestamped and logged into CSV files for later analysis. A Foxglove dashboard provides real-time remote monitoring, while manual control is enabled via a Bluetooth joystick. The system supports both scheduled patrols and adaptive inspections, demonstrating significant potential in industrial automation, predictive maintenance, and workplace safety enhancement.
