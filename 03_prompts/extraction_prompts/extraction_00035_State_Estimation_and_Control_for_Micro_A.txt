TITLE: State Estimation and Control for Micro Aerial Vehicles in GPS-denied Environments
AUTHORS: H. Shen; X. Zhang; H. Lu; B. Tian; Q. Zong
YEAR: 2020
VENUE: NOT_IN_ABSTRACT
DOI: 10.1109/WCSP49889.2020.9299710
ABSTRACT: In this paper, a state estimation and control system in GPS-denied environments is presented for a micro aerial vehicle (MAV). For full state estimation, we formulate the lidar-inertial odometry as a factor graph which can solved by a nonlinear optimizer. For hovering and trajectory control, we propose a dual-mode cascaded nonlinear controller, which ensures the hovering accuracy and trajectory tracking performance. In extensive state estimation and flight tests, we evaluate the performance of the developed control and estimation algorithms proposed in this paper on a onboard computer.
