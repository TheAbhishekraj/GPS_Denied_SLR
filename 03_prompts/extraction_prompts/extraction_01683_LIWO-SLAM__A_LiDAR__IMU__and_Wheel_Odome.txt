AUTHORS: Reitbauer E.; Schmied C.; Theurl F.; Wieser M.
YEAR: 2023
VENUE: NOT_IN_ABSTRACT
DOI: 10.33012/2023.19216
ABSTRACT: The paper presents a sensor fusion algorithm for Simultaneous Localization and Mapping (SLAM) for a wheeled robot which fuses LiDAR, IMU, and wheel odometry using a factor graph. Building on the existing algorithm LIO-SAM, a detailed derivation is given for including an odometry factor for a four-wheel-independent steering and four-wheel-independent driving (4WIS4WID) robot. The algorithm is tested and evaluated using datasets collected at a tunnel research facility. The results show that in comparison to an Extended Kalman Filter, drift can be significantly reduced. © 2023 Proceedings of the 36th International Technical Meeting of the Satellite Division of the Institute of Navigation, ION GNSS+ 2023.
