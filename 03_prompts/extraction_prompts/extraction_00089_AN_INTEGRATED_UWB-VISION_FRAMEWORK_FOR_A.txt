TITLE: AN INTEGRATED UWB-VISION FRAMEWORK FOR AUTONOMOUS APPROACH AND LANDING OF UAVS IN GPS-DENIED ENVIRONMENTS
AUTHORS: Liu J.; Yan C.
YEAR: 2025
VENUE: Mathematical Foundations of Computing
DOI: 10.3934/mfc.2024022
ABSTRACT: This paper focuses on the challenge of autonomous landing of unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) on unknown moving unmanned ground vehicles (UGVs) in GPS-denied environments. We present a newly integrated UWB-Vision framework for autonomous approach and landing of UAVs in GPS-Denied environments. Our framework combines ultra-wideband (UWB) relative localization and Apriltag localization techniques. UWB technology is utilized to measure the relative distance between the UAV and UGV, while the inertial measurement unit (IMU) estimates the target's displacement for precise and real-time position estimation. In addition, Apriltag positioning is integrated into the framework to enhance landing accuracy. Experimental results validate the effectiveness and correctness of our proposed approach. This research contributes to the advancement of autonomous UAV landing in GPS-denied environments. © (2025), (American Institute of Mathematical Sciences). All rights reserved.
