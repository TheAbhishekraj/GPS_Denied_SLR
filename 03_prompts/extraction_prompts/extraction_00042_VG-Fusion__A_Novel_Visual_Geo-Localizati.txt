TITLE: VG-Fusion: A Novel Visual Geo-Localization Fused with IMU and LiDAR-Inertial Odometry for UAVs in GNSS-Denied Environments
AUTHORS: Xu Z.; Zhong X.; Wang N.; Hu H.; Liu Q.
YEAR: 2025
VENUE: Unmanned Systems
DOI: 10.1142/S2301385026500561
ABSTRACT: In recent years, unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) have experienced rapid advancements. Currently, the long-range autonomous flight capability of UAVs is heavily dependent on GNSS localization. However, achieving robust and rapid localization in GNSS-denied conditions remains a significant challenge in UAV localization and navigation. Existing research methods face several issues, including high time consumption, substantial computing resource requirements and low localization accuracy in field scenes. In this paper, we present a novel visual geo-localization (VG) method for UAV localization in GNSS-denied field environments utilizing remote sensing-UAV image registration to achieve absolute localization. We propose an improved approach for constructing a remote sensing image database and an enhanced method for image registration. Our method relies solely on the remote sensing image database and the images captured by the UAV’s onboard camera. Furthermore, we use LiDAR-inertial odometry (LIO) to compensate the displacement of VG during its operation, and then we obtain the precise and continuous localization by fuse the compensated VG, LIO and IMU using factor graph optimization (FGO) in order to improve the robustness and continuous of the localization system. This fusion method is referred to as VG-fusion. Finally, we conducted VPR benchmarking on public datasets and practical application tests on our own datasets. Across two different field scenes, our method achieves localization accuracy with an average error of less than 10 m, demonstrating its robustness and accuracy. The test results demonstrate that our approach provides continuous, stable and robust localization on a small UAV platform with limited computing power in GNSS-denied conditions. © 2026 World Scientific Publishing Company.
