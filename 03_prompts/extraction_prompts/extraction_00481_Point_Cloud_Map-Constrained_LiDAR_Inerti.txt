TITLE: Point Cloud Map-Constrained LiDAR/Inertial Fusion Localization in GNSS Denied Environments
AUTHORS: Wang X.; Du S.; Yi Q.; Li Y.; Wang C.
YEAR: 2026
VENUE: 2026 11th International Conference on Electronic Technology and Information Science, ICETIS 2026
DOI: 10.1109/ICETIS70504.2026.11633487
ABSTRACT: Aiming at the problems of long-term accumulated drift and high computational cost in large-scale point cloud map matching for LiDAR/inertial fusion localization under GNSS-denied environments, this paper proposes a lightweight localization method constrained by a prior map. First, a LiDAR/inertial front-end odometry module is used to obtain continuous motion estimates, and pose graph optimization is introduced to correct long-term drift, thereby constructing a globally consistent prior point cloud map. Then, we designed an adaptive local submap cropping and dynamic updating algorithm based on predicted pose to adapt submap scale with local map point quantity and shrink the target map size in the map-matching stage. Finally, the normal distributions transform (NDT) algorithm is adopted to match the real-time point cloud with the prior map, thereby achieving map-constrained localization. Experimental results show that the proposed method achieves lower trajectory errors on multiple test sequences, and reduces the average single-frame processing time by 32% and 58% compared with LIO-SAM-Reloc and FAST-LIO-Loc, respectively. © 2026 IEEE.
