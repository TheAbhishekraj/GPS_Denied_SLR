TITLE: An Aerial-ground Cooperative Positioning Method for Intelligent and Connected Vehicles under GNSS-denied Environments
AUTHORS: L. Du; A. Liu; B. Tong; X. Yan; W. Chen; S. Cai
YEAR: 2025
VENUE: NOT_IN_ABSTRACT
DOI: 10.1109/ICTIS68762.2025.11215072
ABSTRACT: Positioning is an important technology for the safe and reliable driving of intelligent and connected vehicles (ICVs). In complex urban transportation, GNSS-denied environments such as urban canyons and overpasses can cause serious interference to vehicle positioning, reducing the accuracy of positioning and even making it impossible to perform positioning. Low altitude aircraft can receive GNSS positioning signals and communicate with ICVs in GNSS-denied scenarios due to their flight altitude, thereby assisting ICVs in collaborative positioning. In this paper, an aerial-ground cooperative positioning method based on aerial-ground communication under GNSS-denied environments is proposed, which reduces positioning errors by integrating the direction of arrival (DOA) and relative distance (RD) information of ICV and low altitude aircraft. In order to verify the effectiveness of the proposed method, simulation tests are conducted on MATLAB and Driving Scenario Designer. The experimental results showed that the proposed method can continuously and effectively positioning in GNSS-denied environments and improve the accuracy of single point positioning.
