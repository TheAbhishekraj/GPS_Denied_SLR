TITLE: Simultaneous Localization and Mapping using Sensor Fusion for UAV Navigation in GPS-Denied Environments
AUTHORS: Ramirez R.; Lin T.; Kryzia D.; Luo Z.; Faraj N.; Espana D.; Thakkar T.; Bhandari S.; Raheja A.; Chowdhury M.
YEAR: 2026
VENUE: AIAA Science and Technology Forum and Exposition, AIAA SciTech Forum 2026
DOI: 10.2514/6.2026-0855
ABSTRACT: This paper presents the development and system integration of a UAV framework designed for indoor navigation and other GPS-denied environments. The framework features a UAV equipped with Simultaneous Localization and Mapping (SLAM) and object detection algorithms, designed for search and rescue missions or similar applications. As the SLAM model localizes the aircraft within the unknown environment, it creates a map of the surroundings. Meanwhile, computer vision techniques are employed to detect human targets (victims) within the scene. Upon identifying human targets, the corresponding information is relayed to the output of the SLAM algorithm, which uses it to mark the locations of the detected individuals within the map. Once the map and waypoints are generated, these data can be wirelessly transmitted to other vehicles, enabling them to perform path planning on the 3-dimensional map to navigate autonomously through the environment and provide aid to the victims. As the UAV traverses the environment, Visual-Inertial Odomerty techniques are employed to stabilize the vehicle during flight. Simulation and flight test results are shown. © 2026, American Institute of Aeronautics and Astronautics Inc, AIAA. All rights reserved.
