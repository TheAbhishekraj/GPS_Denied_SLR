TITLE: 5G Based Vehicle Localization in GNSS Denied Environments and Its Improvement Utilizing SRCKF
AUTHORS: Chen M.; Xie N.; Huang Z.; Tu J.
YEAR: 2022
VENUE: Journal of Aeronautics, Astronautics and Aviation 
DOI: 10.6125/JoAAA.202212_54(4).06
ABSTRACT: Autonomous vehicle and active traffic safety benefit greatly from high precision vehicle localization, however, owing to the inherit vulnerability of Global Navigation Satellite System (GNSS), traditional GNSS based vehicle localization method cannot offer continuous high accuracy positioning results in GNSS denied environments. Fifth Generation (5G) communication technique based positioning method and Locata are two promising approaches can be used for high precision vehicle localization in the future. This work applies 5G into vehicle localization and compare some distinctive characteristics with Locata. Analysis results reveal that for 5G based vehicle localization, staggered deployment topology of 5G site outperforms one-side and two-side deployment topologies with the same deployment density. Considering the fact that 5G would be much more widely used in the near future compared with Locata, in order to improve the vehicle localization precision when utilizing 5G in GNSS denied environments, Square Root Cubature Kalman Filter (SRCKF) algorithm is introduced to deal with positioning random errors in original measurements, significant improvement could be reached from simulation results by comparing with some traditional filtering methods, and those high precision localization results would be important for future autonomous vehicles. © 2022 The Aeronautical and Astronautical Society of the Republic of China.
