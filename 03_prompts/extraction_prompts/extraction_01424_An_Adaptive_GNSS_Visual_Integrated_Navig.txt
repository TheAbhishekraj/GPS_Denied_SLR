TITLE: An Adaptive GNSS/Visual Integrated Navigation System for Low-altitude UAV under Signal Challenging Environments
AUTHORS: Di Y.; Jiang C.; Su Z.; Chen S.; Chen Y.
YEAR: 2025
VENUE: NOT_IN_ABSTRACT
DOI: 10.6125/JoAAA.202510_57(8).05
ABSTRACT: Reliable and continuous position is critical for a Low-altitude Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV), the Global Navigation Satellite System (GNSS) can generate navigation solutions. However, navigation solutions from the GNSS are not always reliable due to the possible signal jamming, interference and blockage. In view of the drawbacks, in this paper, we proposed a GNSS/Visual integrated navigation system for low-altitude UAV under signal challenging environments. The visual positing system (VPS) is composed of a front-end visual odometry and back-end aerial-satellite images matching based absolute position module. In the front-end task, the consecutive top-view aerial images from UAV cameras are matched frame-by-frame to realize the relative motion estimation; in the back-end task, satellite remote sensing images after fine calibration are matched with the top-view UAV aerial images to estimate the absolute position. Deep learning methods XFeat and SuperGlue are utilized in the front-end and back-end individually. Front-end and back-end modules in the VPS work cooperatively to generate position, which is then integrated with the GNSS using Adaptive Factor Graph Optimization (AFGO) to realize more reliable position information. The performance of the proposed method was evaluated through four long-distance flight tests, with an average position error of approximately 10.14 meters. When a GNSS outage was introduced along the flight path, the system can still output positioning result with the VPS. The experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed method. © 2025, The Aeronautical and Astronautical Society of the Republic of China. All rights reserved.
