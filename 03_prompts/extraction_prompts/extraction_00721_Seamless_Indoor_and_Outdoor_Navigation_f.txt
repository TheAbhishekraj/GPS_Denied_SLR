ABSTRACT: This research introduces a superior localization algorithm to enhance the navigation capabilities of Unmanned Ground Vehicles (UGVs) in urban environments, particularly during GPS outages. The proposed method leverages image-based localization and environmental classification to ensure reliable navigation. Reference images are extracted from Google Maps API along a planned route and compared with real-time UGV camera feeds. Feature-based comparing methods like ORB and SuperPoint provided robust, realtime image matching. Furthermore, a CLIP contrastive learning framework enables accurate classification of indoor, outdoor, and transitional environments, allowing the UGV to adapt its navigation strategy. This integrated approach enhances UGV autonomy and reliability in dynamic, GPS-denied scenarios.
