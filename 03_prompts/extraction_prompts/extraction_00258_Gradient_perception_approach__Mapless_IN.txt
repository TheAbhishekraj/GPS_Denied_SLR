TITLE: Gradient perception approach: Mapless INS/geomagnetic combined navigation in GPS-denied environments
AUTHORS: Yang S.; Tang H.; Zhang X.; Bai W.; Xin J.; Huang Y.
YEAR: 2026
VENUE: Measurement: Journal of the International Measurement Confederation
DOI: 10.1016/j.measurement.2026.122761
ABSTRACT: In GPS-denied environments, errors in angular velocity, acceleration, and zero-bias drift degrade inertial navigation system (INS) accuracy over time. Limited high-resolution geomagnetic maps and magnetic disturbances further reduce navigation reliability. This paper introduces the gradient perception approach (GPA), a mapless framework that fuses geomagnetic vector gradient perception with inertial data. Using a multi-vector parallel guidance model and model predictive control (MPC) with state constraints, GPA generates stable heading corrections without requiring prior high-resolution geomagnetic maps or precise initial INS parameters, relying only on the discrete magnetic signature at the target. Dynamic fusion weights address model uncertainties, allowing graceful degradation to state-propagated tracking in areas with unobservable gradients. Evaluations with SuperMAG-driven magnetic storm simulations, short-term drone tests, and long-term flight logs show the GPA outperforms uncompensated predictive baselines. Monte Carlo simulations under uniform disturbances demonstrate terminal accuracy improvements of 78.19% and 95.89% over LTI-MPC and LTV-MPC, respectively, while empirical flight tests confirm its drift-suppression capability, mainly contingent on the observability of local geomagnetic gradients. © 2026 Elsevier Ltd
