TITLE: Improving the Localization of the Magnetic Field-Based SLAM Through a Novel PINN-UKF Framework
AUTHORS: Magad A.A.; Emzir M.F.
YEAR: 2026
VENUE: IEEE Access
DOI: 10.1109/ACCESS.2026.3690969
ABSTRACT: This study presents a novel framework for magnetic field-based simultaneous localization and mapping (SLAM) for indoor navigation, which integrates a physics-informed neural network (PINN) model with an unscented Kalman filter (UKF). The primary objective is to improve the accuracy and robustness of moving vehicle localization while navigating in global positioning system (GPS)-denied environments, where magnetic field variations can provide useful information to guide the moving vehicle in achieving its task. In the proposed PINN-UKF approach, a PINN is first trained offline to provide an initial physically consistent magnetic field map by embedding Maxwell's equations directly into the learning process. This results in a smooth, divergence-free and curl-consistent magnetic representation that inherently respects the underlying physics of the environment. During the online phase, the UKF is employed to estimate the vehicle's nonlinear state dynamics, while a lightweight continual learning mechanism incrementally updates the PINN parameters using newly acquired magnetic measurements. Numerical simulation experiments with ablation analysis and evaluation on real-world datasets are conducted using a realistic surface vessel dynamic model for indoor navigation. The results demonstrate that the proposed PINN-UKF framework shows good performance and outperforms baseline odometry as well as some state-of-the-art magnetic field-based SLAM alternatives in terms of localization accuracy, achieving an RMSE value of 0.011 m for the proposed PINN-UKF approach and 0.095 m for the odometry. These findings validate the efficacy of combining physics-informed modeling and online map adaptation for enhancing magnetic field SLAM in GPS-denied and magnetically structured environments. The source code used to reproduce the results in this paper is available at https://github.com/AdebMagad/PINN-UKF-SLAM.git. © 2013 IEEE.
