TITLE: Adaptive and Resilient Consensus for UAV-UGV Teams via Virtual Agent Mediation and Topology Switching
AUTHORS: Guo F.; Shen R.; Zhang J.; Sun C.; Zhan Q.
YEAR: 2025
VENUE: IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Biomimetics, ROBIO
DOI: 10.1109/ROBIO66223.2025.11376277
ABSTRACT: This paper addresses the problem of unified cooperative control for UAV-UGV systems under dynamic topology switching in GNSS-denied environments. We propose a novel framework that introduces a gimbal-mounted LiDAR virtual agent to mediate coordination between aerial and ground vehicles. By integrating virtual agent mediation, a unified heterogeneous consensus protocol, and a safety-performance control barrier function (CBF) approach, the system achieves robust formation and task reconfiguration while guaranteeing safety and prescribed performance. Theoretical results establish stability and constraint satisfaction under arbitrary topology switches. Numerical simulations, conducted under realistic settings with randomly assigned initial conditions, demonstrate rapid error convergence, persistent safety, and seamless adaptation to discrete mission events. The proposed framework translates theoretical consensus principles into a practical control strategy for heterogeneous robots in challenging environments.  © 2025 IEEE.
