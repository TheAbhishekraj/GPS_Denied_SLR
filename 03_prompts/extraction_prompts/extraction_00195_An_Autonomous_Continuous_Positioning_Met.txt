TITLE: An Autonomous Continuous Positioning Method for Low-Altitude UAVs Based on Image Recognition in GNSS-Denied Environments
AUTHORS: T. Du; Y. Lin
YEAR: 2026
VENUE: NOT_IN_ABSTRACT
DOI: 10.1109/RAITS68656.2026.11580149
ABSTRACT: The article proposes a five-stage autonomous continuous positioning method that integrates inertial navigation and image recognition to address the prob-lem of accumulated positioning errors in unmanned aerial vehicles (UAV) in satellite-denied environments. This method first used inertial navigation (INS) for initial positioning to capture satellite image basemaps, and achieved aerial image preprocessing through heading angle correction, scale registration, and maximum inscribed rectangle cropping. Then, this study combined a U-Net semantic segmentation model to extract building areas, and innovatively adopted a two-stage matching strategy of template matching and SuperPoint & SuperGlue feature point matching to effectively over-come the interference of seasonal and lighting changes on matching. Finally, the height data was integrated into output three-dimensional coordinates. The experiment was based on the UAV-VisLOC dataset, and showed that twostage matching can reduce the average plane positioning error from 26.32 m in single-stage matching to 12.95 m, a decrease of 49.2%. The maximum error decreased from 68.74 m to 18.67 m. The proposed framework significantly improves the robustness and accuracy of positioning in complex environments, providing reliable technical support for urban low-altitude operations, emergency rescue, and other scenarios.
