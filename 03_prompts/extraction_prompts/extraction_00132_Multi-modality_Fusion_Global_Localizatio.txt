TITLE: Multi-modality Fusion Global Localization Method for UGVs in Rough Terrain GNSS-Denied Environments
AUTHORS: Zhao X.; Su B.; Guang X.
YEAR: 2023
VENUE: Lecture Notes in Electrical Engineering
DOI: 10.1007/978-981-19-6613-2_147
ABSTRACT: The localization has been a key technique for autonomous navigation of UGVs to traverse complex rough terrain scenes. The problem has been solved by GNSS-based method mainly. However, facing the application scenario of GNSS rejection, accurate and robust localization is still a tricky problem. This paper proposes a multi-modality fusion global localization method to solve this problem. Utilizing common techniques, like VO, LiDAR odometry and dead reckoning, a multi-modality localization method is proposed to get the accurate relative position information. Where, visual location is used to update the global position as first correction. Then a novel global localization correction method using road network and road boundary detection is proposed as the second correction of the global localization. The proposed approach offers several redundant position solutions and provide an accurate position in rough terrain case. A wide range navigation experiment is carried out and the experimental results show that the proposed approach can offer an accuracy and robustness localization result in rough terrain GNSS-denied environments. © 2023, The Author(s), under exclusive license to Springer Nature Singapore Pte Ltd.
