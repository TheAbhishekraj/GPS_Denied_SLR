TITLE: Real-time geospatial positioning for uavs in gps-denied environments using lidar data
AUTHORS: Kim H.; Kim D.; Kim S.
YEAR: 2020
VENUE: AIAA Scitech 2020 Forum
DOI: 10.2514/6.2020-2194
ABSTRACT: The localization of unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) in GPS-denied environments has been known as a difficult problem for a long time, mainly due to variation in altitude and motion dynamics. This article proposes a robust and efficient simultaneous localization and mapping (SLAM) solution, with applications for speedy UAVs integrated with Light Detection and Ranging (LiDAR) and Inertial Measurement Unit (IMU) sensors. In each time interval, i) the initial map (surface) is reconstructed using a predicted UAV velocity and registered for the final map of the previous time interval, ii) an accurate velocity is estimated by solving an optimization problem, and iii) the final map is reconstructed using the estimated velocity. For its applications for outdoor speedy UAVs, efficient numerical algorithms are newly developed for filtering, surface reconstruction, and elevation registration. It has been numerically verified that the proposed navigation system could locate the UAV accurately taking less than half of the flight time, although the speed of the UAV exceeds 250 m/s. © 2020, American Institute of Aeronautics and Astronautics Inc, AIAA. All rights reserved.
