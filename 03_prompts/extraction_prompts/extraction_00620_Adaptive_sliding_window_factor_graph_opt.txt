TITLE: Adaptive sliding window factor graph optimization with multi-tag UWB/INS fusion for multirotor UAVs
AUTHORS: Kim H.; Lee J.D.; Chang B.; Bang H.
YEAR: 2026
VENUE: Aerospace Science and Technology
DOI: 10.1016/j.ast.2026.112683
ABSTRACT: Accurate and reliable localization is essential for unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) operating in global navigation satellite system (GNSS)-denied environments, such as indoor spaces or urban canyons, where satellite signals are unavailable or severely degraded. This paper presents an adaptive sliding window factor graph optimization (ASW-FGO) framework for robust 6-degree-of-freedom (6-DoF) UAV localization using multi-tag ultra-wide-band (UWB) ranging and inertial navigation system (INS) measurements. Conventional UWB/INS integration suffers from attitude unobservability in single-tag configurations and a strict trade-off between estimation accuracy and computational efficiency in fixed sliding window factor graph optimization. To address these challenges, a multi-tag UWB configuration is employed to provide sufficient geometric constraints for 6-DoF state estimation. In addition, a Gaussian kernel-based residual weighting scheme is introduced to suppress non-line-of-sight (NLOS) measurement outliers. The resulting kernel weights are further exploited as a reliability metric to adaptively regulate the sliding window size, reducing computational load under stable sensing conditions while preserving robustness when measurement quality degrades. The effectiveness of the proposed ASW-FGO framework is validated through both simulations and real-world UAV flight experiments. In NLOS environments, ASW-FGO achieves position and attitude root mean square errors of 0.15 m and 7.44 ∘, respectively, outperforming extended Kalman filter and fixed sliding window factor graph optimization (FFGO) approaches. Moreover, the proposed method reduces optimization time by up to 72.88 % compared to the FFGO. These results demonstrate that ASW-FGO effectively balances estimation robustness and computational efficiency, enabling reliable UAV localization in complex indoor and GNSS-denied environments. © 2026 Elsevier Masson SAS.
