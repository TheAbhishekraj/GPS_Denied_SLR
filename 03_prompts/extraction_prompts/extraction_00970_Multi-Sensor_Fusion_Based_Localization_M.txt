ABSTRACT: With the rapid advancement of underwater communication technologies, modern military and civilian underwater exploration increasingly demands higher localization accuracy. In this context, multi-sensor fusion–based localization methods aimed at reducing positioning errors have attracted significant attention. Localization of underwater autonomous vehicles (UAVs/AUVs) is typically achieved through onboard inertial navigation systems combined with Doppler velocity logs (DVLs). However, during long-range missions—particularly in extended underwater operations—the velocity integration of DVLs tends to accumulate errors over time, thereby degrading localization accuracy. To address the complexity and heterogeneity of multisource data, this paper proposes a multi-sensor fusion localization method for underwater vehicles. The proposed approach employs Kalman filtering for state prediction and fuses trajectories from an underwater beacon–based positioning platform, effectively suppressing outliers and noise-induced errors to enhance fusion accuracy. Ultimately, the method achieves high-precision underwater navigation and localization.
