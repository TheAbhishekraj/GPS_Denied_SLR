TITLE: A Visual-Inertial Localization Method With Fusion of Vehicle Motion Features
AUTHORS: Tian D.; Wang L.; Liu Z.; Sun H.; Lv W.; Jiang C.
YEAR: 2025
VENUE: ICSMD 2025 - International Conference on Sensing, Measurement and Data Analytics in the Era of Artificial Intelligence
DOI: 10.1109/ICSMD67131.2025.11365501
ABSTRACT: Autonomous driving relies on reliable localization, but the Global Navigation Satellite System (GNSS) often loses accuracy or becomes unavailable in harsh environments, such as tunnels and dense urban canyons. Visual-Inertial Simultaneous Localization and Mapping (VI-SLAM), which fuses camera and Inertial Measurement Unit (IMU) data, is a key alternative in GNSS-denied scenarios. However most VI-SLAM methods overlook vehicles' inherent periodic motion features, such as zero velocity during stops or gear shifts, and linear motion during cruising. To address this, this paper proposes a visual-inertial localization method integrating these features into a tightly coupled VI-SLAM framework. First, based on IMU data, the Generalized Likelihood Ratio Test is used to detect zero velocity and linear motion. Then, Zero Velocity Update (ZUPT) residuals and Nonholonomic Constraint (NHC) residuals are constructed. These two residuals are jointly optimized with visual residuals, IMU pre-integration residuals, and marginalization residuals within a sliding-window nonlinear backend for Visual-Inertial Odometry. Experiments on self-collected datasets show that compared to the baseline algorithm, both the ZUPT-integrated algorithm and the NHC-integrated algorithm yield higher accuracy, lower error variance, and stronger robustness. © 2025 IEEE.
