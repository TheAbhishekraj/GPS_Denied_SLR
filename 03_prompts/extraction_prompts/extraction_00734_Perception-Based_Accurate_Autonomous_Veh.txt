ABSTRACT: In this paper, we propose a full-stack system for perception-based accurate autonomous vehicle following that does not rely on vehicle-to-vehicle communication, map data, or accurate global localization. Our system consists of simple components for localization, object tracking, behaviour and waypoint management, trajectory planning, and control. We assessed the system's performance through different practical experiments using a real autonomous car. The experiments were conducted in various scenarios, including driving on- and off-road with speeds of up to 20 m/s and in the presence of dynamic obstacles.
