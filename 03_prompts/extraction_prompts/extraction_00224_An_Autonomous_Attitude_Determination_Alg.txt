TITLE: An Autonomous Attitude Determination Algorithm Adapted to the Maneuvering for UAV
AUTHORS: C. Xu
YEAR: 2025
VENUE: NOT_IN_ABSTRACT
DOI: 10.1109/ICMTAE66890.2025.11427995
ABSTRACT: A fully autonomous horizontal attitude estimation algorithm is proposed in this paper, the algorithm based on the airspeed and inertial navigation when maneuvering to fulfilling the long-term attitude measurement requirements of middle speed fixed-wing UAV (Unmanned Aerial Vehicle) in GNSS (Global Navigation Satellite System) denied environment, an outlier detection algorithm is adopted to reduce the influence of sudden acceleration or any other disturbances, the major error sources are analyzed. The main contribution of this paper is an autonomous attitude estimation algorithm without any external information is built, which will improve the accuracy and reliability of attitude measurement of such type of UAV in GNSS denied environment greatly. As the attitude error introduced by maneuvering and accelerometer errors are sudden and much larger than that introduced by gyroscope error, a data fusion algorithm combining innovation orthogonality detection is adopted, the algorithm can identify and eliminate the outliers induced by carrier maneuvering and accelerometer measurement errors accurately. The experiment result indicated that the accuracy is $0.17 \text{deg}(1 \sigma)$ for roll angle and $0.60 \text{deg}(1 \sigma)$ for pitch angle.
