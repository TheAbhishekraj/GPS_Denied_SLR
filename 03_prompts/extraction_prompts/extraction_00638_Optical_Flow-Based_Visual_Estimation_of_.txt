TITLE: Optical Flow-Based Visual Estimation of Translational Displacement Using a Raspberry Pi® Embedded Computer
AUTHORS: Mota López B.J.; Delgado Reyes G.; Hernández Pérez M.A.; Carpio Santamaria F.A.; Marquez Dominguez S.
YEAR: 2025
VENUE: 2025 Mexican International Conference on Computer Science, ENC 2025
DOI: 10.1109/ENC68268.2025.11311932
ABSTRACT: This study presents the implementation of an optical flow-based algorithm for estimating translational displacement in an Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV), using both a personal computer (PC) and a Raspberry Pi 4®. The algorithm was tested under five configurations, including Gaussian filtering and the Global Positioning System (GPS). The Raspberry Pi(R) with Gaussian smoothing yielded the best results. Quantitative evaluation showed superior accuracy compared to inertial sensors in indoor environments. The approach highlights the potential of onboard optical flow algorithms in low-power platforms for navigation, offering portability and autonomy in GPS-denied or infrastructurelimited scenarios.  © 2025 IEEE.
