AUTHORS: Prashasti; A. Grover; S. Chatterjee

ABSTRACT: Efficient and reliable object detection is essential for ensuring the safe navigation of autonomous drones, particularly in GPS-denied environments such as warehouses, tunnels, and disaster zones. This work presents a customdesigned analog ultrasonic detection system, purpose-built to address the latency, energy, and hardware complexity challenges associated with conventional microcontroller- and FPGA-based approaches. The proposed system operates without a digital processor, achieving a response time between $1.3-3.2 \text{ms}$ and an average power consumption of 52.4 mW, verified through experimental testing using calibrated measurement tools. Compared to commercial alternatives like the HC-SR04, the system offers a substantial improvement in energy efficiency and response speed while maintaining an accuracy of $\pm \mathrm{1.8 cm}$ up to 1.2 meters. Its minimalistic design, low-power footprint, and rapid detection capabilities position it as a commercially viable solution for critical navigation tasks in mission-driven UAV applications.
