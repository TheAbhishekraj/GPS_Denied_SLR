TITLE: Automated SLAM–GPR integration for robotic surface–subsurface mapping in GNSS-denied environments
AUTHORS: Eldemiry A.; Ali E.; Abdelkhalek S.; Zayed T.; Abdelkareem M.A.A.
YEAR: 2026
VENUE: Automation in Construction
DOI: 10.1016/j.autcon.2026.107118
ABSTRACT: Manual cart-based GPR surveys typically depend on straight-line grids and external positioning, limiting GNSS-denied indoor and semi-indoor deployment where motion is constrained by stopping, repositioning, and near-planar surfaces. This paper advances LiDAR–SLAM–GPR integration through a trajectory-aware automation framework supporting distance- and time-triggered acquisition. The framework turns free-form robotic motion into structured georeferenced GPR profiles under bounded GNSS-denied inspection conditions. Its contributions are: (i) mode-aware pose–trace association preserving metric consistency across Time and Distance modes; (ii) velocity-direction masking to handle Distance Mode non-recording behavior and prevent chainage inflation; and (iii) automatic scan-line extraction and slicing from free-form trajectories using heading and velocity consistency. Proof-of-concept experiments validate end-to-end SLAM–GPR co-registration against TLS and tape checkpoints, achieving 1–3 cm median errors in Distance Mode. An 87 m2 corridor slab case study demonstrates automated scan-line extraction and georeferenced corrosion-likelihood visualization from GPR amplitude attenuation. © 2026 Elsevier B.V.
