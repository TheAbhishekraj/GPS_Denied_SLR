TITLE: An interactive virtual mobile assistance for indoor navigation using wireless Beacon technology
AUTHORS: U. A. A. Niroshika; W. A. G. C. Weerakoon
YEAR: 2020

DOI: 10.1109/IICAIET49801.2020.9257870
ABSTRACT: Indoor navigation and product recognition in a supermarket is highly challenging task for visually impaired people. Present systems designed for autonomous shopping often produce unsatisfactory results due to high cost and less accuracy. A novel autonomous indoor navigation solution, capable of providing complete mobile solution for both indoor navigation and product identification for visually impaired people in a supermarket, is presented in this paper. The indoor navigation is done by analyzing the position data obtained through set of wireless location technology called beacons. Further the product identification is done using series of advanced image processing techniques. As the proposed solution is interactive, it provides more attractive and effective solution for visually impaired people. The performance of the solution is tested with few supermarkets and the results are demonstrated.
