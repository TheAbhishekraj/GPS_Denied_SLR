TITLE: Pitch Angle Estimation from a Single Drone Image Without Landmarks and Its Application in UAV Visual Navigation
AUTHORS: Ye Y.; Li Z.; Guan B.; Wang K.; Liu X.; Yu Q.
YEAR: 2025
VENUE: Proceedings - 2025 International Conference on Image and Video Processing, ICIVP 2025
DOI: 10.1109/ICIVP66296.2025.00030
ABSTRACT: Pitch angle, as part of the 6 degrees of freedom (DoF) drone pose, is crucial for UAV navigation. Existing vision-based drone pose estimation methods often rely on pre-selected specific landmarks or require image matching, which can be quite complex. This paper introduces a landmark-free module to estimate the drone camera's pitch angle with monocular images. Extensive experiments demonstrated that our method can achieve accurate pitch angle estimation results (with an average error of 8.7°). The visual localization algorithm enhanced with our module outperforms the baseline method where the pitch angle is unknown.  © 2025 IEEE.
