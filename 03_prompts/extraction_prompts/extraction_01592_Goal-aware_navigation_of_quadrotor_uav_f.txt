TITLE: Goal-aware navigation of quadrotor uav for infrastructure inspection
AUTHORS: Biswas S.; Sharma R.
YEAR: 2019
VENUE: NOT_IN_ABSTRACT
DOI: 10.2514/6.2019-1691
ABSTRACT: In this paper, we solve the problem of navigation of a quadrotor Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV) in a previously unknown and Global Positioning System (GPS) denied en-vironment. We formulate the problem in a robot-centered reference frame with the UAV’s destination (or goal) as the anchor of the map. This helps in estimating the relative orien-tation of the UAV and other Landmarks (LMs) with respect to the goal. We corroborate our problem formulation for estimation and navigation through extensive simulations. We also present results for real-time goal and LM tracking, particularly with (1) robustness to the presence of similar-colored or similar-looking objects, and (2) elimination of tracking error with the increase in target or goal size as the UAV draws near to it. � 2019 by German Aerospace Center (DLR).
