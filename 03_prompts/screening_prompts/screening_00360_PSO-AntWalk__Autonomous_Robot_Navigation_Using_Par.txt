TITLE: PSO-AntWalk: Autonomous Robot Navigation Using Particle Swarm Optimization with Ant Colony Optimization Based Antwalk Function
AUTHORS: S. R. Addula; R. B. N; A. D. S. C; D. Kesavan; G. Malathi
YEAR: 2026
ABSTRACT: Robots that are autonomous are becoming a part of the modern lifestyle and can work in various complex settings, including both industrial plants and medical institutions. Traditional navigation systems, however, commonly have difficulty in trade off global path optimality and real time local reactivity, and in such a way tend to lose themselves in a local minimum in the presence of dynamic obstacles. This poses a serious bottleneck in Multi-Goal Path Planning (MGPP). To address this shortcoming, this research proposes a PSOAntWalk, which is hybrid model that combines the global search strategy with local flexibility. Proposed model is a hybrid approach that uses the Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO) to route the path globally and an enhanced Ant Colony Optimization (ACO) with a novel mechanism of AntWalk to negotiate obstacles in real-time. Moreover, the system uses SLAM-generated occupancy grids and simulated annealing to avoid local optimum. The proposed method was rigorously validated through comprehensive experiments in fourteen diverse routes in four different environments. The superiority of the framework is numerically validated, as the PSO-AntWalk algorithm shows much better performance than other variants such as the Levy Flight PSO, such as path lengths that are always close to optimal values such as 48.2 m and an optimal of 47.7m.
