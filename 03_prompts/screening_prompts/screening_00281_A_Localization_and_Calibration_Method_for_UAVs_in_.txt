TITLE: A Localization and Calibration Method for UAVs in Underground Environments Using IMU, Barometer, and Randomly Deployed UWB Tags
AUTHORS: Zhang G.; Wei Z.; Qi Z.
YEAR: 2025.0
ABSTRACT: Precise localization and navigation in Global Positioning System (GPS)-denied underground environments, such as caves and mines, are critical for applications like terrain mapping and search-and-rescue missions. This paper proposes a novel framework for Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs) integrating an Inertial Measurement Unit (IMU), a barometer, and randomly deployed Ultra-Wideband (UWB) tags. The UWB tags provide high-precision slant range measurements to correct IMU position errors. A cooperative optimization algorithm enables simultaneous localization and calibration, enhancing UAV trajectory and tag positioning. Simulations show the framework's effectiveness, providing a cost-effective solution for autonomous navigation in challenging underground conditions. Copyright © 2025 The Authors.
