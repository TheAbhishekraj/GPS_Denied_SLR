TITLE: Autonomous Positioning System for UAV Scene Matching Under GNSS-Denied Environments
AUTHORS: Du S.; Ma C.; Wu P.; Yu A.; Li D.; Dong Z.
YEAR: 2026.0
ABSTRACT: To address the significant decline in UAV navigation and positioning accuracy caused by severe error accumulation of inertial measurement units (IMUs) in GNSS-denied environments, which severely restricts operational capabilities, we propose an autonomous UAV positioning system based on image matching/inertial navigation fusion. First, a Multi-scale Oriented Gradient Fusion (MOGF) image matching algorithm is developed to achieve rapid and high-precision matching for UAV scene imagery. Subsequently, an autonomous positioning model integrating image matching with INS is established to accurately determine the spatial coordinates of the UAV. Finally, a prototype system for autonomous positioning via image matching is designed and implemented on the RK3588 domestic computing platform, with field flight tests conducted using the DJI M210 UAV platform. Experimental results demonstrate stable system performance, achieving a positioning accuracy of 16.4 meters and a real-time processing rate exceeding 8.3 Hz. These outcomes satisfy the requirements for high-precision and real-time applications. © 2026 The Authors.
