TITLE: Development of a 3D-Printed Differential-Drive Mobile Robot with ROS 2
AUTHORS: Z. -Y. Chew; B. -H. Kwan; C. -H. Goh; S. -C. Chan; M. -Y. Ooi; B. I. Ismail; Q. Zhang; D. W. -K. Ng
YEAR: 2026
ABSTRACT: This paper presents a low-cost 3D-printed differential-drive mobile robot platform designed for hands-on experimentation in autonomous navigation. The robot integrates a low-cost 360-degree LiDAR sensor, an LCD display, and a Raspberry Pi Compute Module 5 running ROS 2 Jazzy as the main processing unit. The complete software stack is containerized using Docker and incorporates SLAMToolbox for simultaneous localization and mapping and the ROS 2 Navigation (Nav2) framework for autonomous navigation. A cloud-based IoT system is also developed to enable remote monitoring and control via a web-based interface. The proposed platform was evaluated through a hands-on workshop involving 20 participants, demonstrating reliable indoor navigation performance and ease of deployment. The results indicate that the combination of 3D-printable hardware and a fully containerized ROS 2 navigation stack provides a reproducible and accessible mobile robot platform suitable for rapid prototyping and experimentation.
