TITLE: Autonomous UAV Localization with Synthetic Aperture Radar
AUTHORS: Y. Zhou; J. Qian; H. Lyu
YEAR: 2025
ABSTRACT: This paper presents a new framework of autonomous positioning for unmanned aerial vehicles (UAV) with synthetic aperture radar (SAR) systems, named SAR odometry. As a potential approach to autonomous positioning and mapping for drones in global positioning system (GPS) denied scenarios, mature solutions of simultaneous localization and mapping (SLAM) systems are based on LiDAR and camera, mainly for near-field application, e.g., autonomous driving car, robotic dog, etc.. However, these frameworks do not work for UAVSAR. Thus, we first propose the SAR-SLAM framework. To make it available for real-time UAV localization, we adapt dechirped images from sub-aperture data as input. The oriented FAST and rotated BRIEF (ORB) algorithm, incorporated with adaptive filtering designed for dechirped images, are used for feature extraction and matching. Lastly, pose estimation is based on a custom geometric model explicitly developed for dechirped images, which computes the angular and displacement offsets using matched feature points in successive frames and converts them into a global coordinate system to reconstruct the complete trajectory of the UAV. Experimental results based on measured data demonstrate that the proposed SAR odometer framework achieves high accuracy and robustness.
