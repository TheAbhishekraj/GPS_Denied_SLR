TITLE: Multi-Agent Collaborative Navigation Based on Cross-View Matching
AUTHORS: Qiao T.; Hu F.; Jin Z.; Zhou Z.
YEAR: 2025.0
ABSTRACT: Collaboration between intelligent agents have gained significant research attention due to their practicality across various scenarios. This work addresses the problem of collaborative navigation for intelligent agents and proposes a distributed estimation framework centered on cross-view matching. To obtain cross-view observations, by integrating object detection algorithms with depth cameras, this work proposes a multi-view matching strategy that combines semantic and positional information. The proposed fusion framework employs a Error-State Kalman Filter (ESKF) to fuse ranging data, visual-inertial odometry (VIO) information, and cross-view observations. It further establishes cross-view geometric constraints by leveraging environmental features and targets that are visible to multiple agents. Compared to traditional integrated navigation methods, experimental results demonstrate that introducing co-visibility constraints effectively enhances pose estimation accuracy while ensuring both precision and robustness in GNSS-denied environments, and highlight the potential of the proposed approach as a lightweight and reliable navigation solution in GNSS-denied environments. This work provides technical support for vision-centric collaborative autonomous systems. © 2025 IEEE.
