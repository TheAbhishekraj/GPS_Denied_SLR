TITLE: A Semantic-Aided Fusion Localization Method for Intelligent Vehicles in GNSS-Denied Dynamic Environments
AUTHORS: Wei K.; Li X.; Tian Z.; Ni P.; Sun Z.
YEAR: 2024.0
ABSTRACT: The LiDAR localization method is a crucial solution for intelligent vehicles in GNSS-denied environments. However, moving objects in environments can cause the error of point cloud registration and reduce the localization accuracy. Aiming at above problems, this paper proposes a semantic-aided fusion localization method using LiDAR and IMU, which makes use of static object segmentation to optimize point cloud registration and multi-sensor fusion. Firstly, a feature extraction module based on Transformer is presented to capture the geometric and semantic features from LiDAR data. Then, a segmentation and registration module based on the multi-task learning is presented to recognize static objects and predict the transformation matrix between two point clouds. Lastly, taking advantage of the segmentation results, an adaptive fusion localization module based on the factor graph is presented to estimate the position by fusing LiDAR and IMU measurements. The qualitative and quantitative evaluations are conducted on the KITTI dataset and our real vehicle dataset. The experimental results demonstrate that our proposed method can achieve the high-precision localization for intelligent vehicles in GNSS-denied dynamic environments.  © IEEE. 2016 IEEE.
