TITLE: A Decision-Making Framework for Autonomous Aerial Vehicles in Responding to GPS Loss
AUTHORS: Prell R.; Rubio R.; Joseph S.; Negash N.; Sun L.
YEAR: 2026.0
ABSTRACT: Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs) face critical challenges when operating in environments where Global Positioning System (GPS) signals are degraded or unavailable. Existing work on GPS-denied navigation has produced capable localization and control techniques, but these contributions are often siloed and rarely integrate energy constraints, decision logic, and regulatory considerations into a cohesive architecture, especially in Beyond-Visual-Line-Of-Sight (BVLOS) operations. This paper proposes a unified, modular framework that couples resilient multimodal localization, such as visual–inertial odometry and simultaneous localization and mapping, with an energy-aware trajectory planner and a contingency-aware decision layer that manages actions such as rerouting, hovering, reorienting, returning to home, or performing a safe landing. The proposed framework explicitly encodes navigation confidence, command-and-control integrity, and logging for post-flight audit, enabling traceable behavior suitable for certification and large-scale deployment. By moving GPS-denied response from ad-hoc failsafe logic to a standardized, closed-loop decision-making model, this work provides a blueprint that can inform future performance standards and accelerate trustworthy beyond-visual-line-of-sight and advanced air mobility operations in real-world industrial, public-safety, and urban contexts. © 2025, American Institute of Aeronautics and Astronautics Inc, AIAA.
