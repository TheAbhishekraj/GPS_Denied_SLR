TITLE: Advancing Autonomy: A Thematic Synthesis of Active SLAM Research in Robotic Exploration and Mapping
AUTHORS: Z. Dai; D. Tu; Y. Peng
YEAR: 2025
ABSTRACT: Active Simultaneous Localization and Mapping (Active SLAM) enables robotic agents to not only build maps and localize but to actively plan trajectories that improve mapping quality, reduce uncertainty, and enhance exploration efficiency. This review synthesizes approximately thirty key papers from 2018 to 2025, focusing on exploration strategies, uncertainty management, path planning, sensing modalities, deep learning integration, multi-robot systems, and application domains. Recent trends show a shift from frontier-based methods toward information-theoretic approaches and learning-based strategies, with growing emphasis on multi-modal fusion and multi-robot coordination. Information-theoretic metrics such as entropy and Fisher Information are widely used for uncertainty handling, while reinforcement learning (RL) methods demonstrate adaptive exploration in dynamic environments. Persistent gaps include scalability to large, real-world deployments, computational efficiency on resource-constrained platforms, and a lack of standardized benchmarks. We argue that hybrid approaches combining classical optimization with AI are a promising direction to address these challenges, accelerating progress toward robust autonomy in GPS-denied environments such as search and rescue, space, and underwater robotics.
