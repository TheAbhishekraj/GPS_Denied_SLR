TITLE: Synchronized Multi-Directional FMCW mmWave Radar–Inertial Odometry: Robust Positioning and Autonomous Navigation Experiments for UAVs in Low-Light Indoor Environments
AUTHORS: Jing Y.; Sipahi R.; Martinez-Lorenzo J.
YEAR: 2026.0
ABSTRACT: Highlights: What are the main findings? An engineering-ready Hybrid-RIO indoor localization and navigation system is developed, leveraging an ESKF-based fusion of four-directional FMCW mmWave radars and a high-rate IMU to provide stable state estimation for UAVs in GPS-denied, low-light, and airflow-disturbed environments. A radar ego-velocity estimation method based on three-point RANSAC-LSQ is introduced, together with an independent vertical mmWave radar for altitude fusion, effectively mitigating barometer disturbances and improving 3D positioning accuracy and consistency. What are the implications of the main findings? The proposed approach demonstrates the practical feasibility of radar–inertial fusion for real-world autonomous UAV flight and can be integrated into PX4 and ROS frameworks as a general-purpose indoor navigation solution. The results establish a reusable engineering pathway for UAV operations in confined and complex environments, with strong potential for deployment in tunnel inspection, underground exploration, and emergency rescue applications. This paper presents a robust approach for achieving accurate indoor positioning and autonomous navigation of quadcopters through the fusion of multiple radars and an inertial measurement unit (IMU) named hybrid-filtered Radar–Inertial Odometry (Hybrid-RIO). The Hybrid-RIO system integrates four-directional Frequency-Modulated Continuous-Wave (FMCW) millimeter-wave (mmWave) radars simultaneously with a high-performance IMU to continuously estimate the quadcopters’ position, velocity, and orientation even in low-light and indoor environments. The autonomous flight commands from the system further enable indoor navigation without requiring human intervention. Experimental results reveal notable advancements in both the accuracy and consistency of positioning. The integration of the proposed Hybrid-RIO approach holds promise in a wide spectrum of domains, including cave exploration, tunnel rescue operations, and indoor navigation solutions. © 2026 by the authors.
