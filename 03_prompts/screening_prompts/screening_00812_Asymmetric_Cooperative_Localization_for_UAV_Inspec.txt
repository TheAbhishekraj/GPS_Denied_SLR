TITLE: Asymmetric Cooperative Localization for UAV Inspection Inside Wind Turbine Towers Based on 3D Semantic Segmentation
AUTHORS: Z. Wang; W. Deng; R. Xia; B. Xu; B. Zhang; R. Shen
YEAR: 2026
ABSTRACT: To ensure the safety of wind energy infrastructure, an asymmetric cooperative localization framework is developed for UAV inspection inside wind turbine towers. Addressing the challenges of Global Navigation Satellite System denied environments and geometric repetitiveness, the methodology offloads perception and computation to a ground-based station. The approach involves a geometric preprocessing sequence to align point cloud coordinates and suppress platform pose drift, followed by a 3D semantic segmentation network to isolate the UAV from the complex tower environment. The UAV's position is then determined through geometric statistics of the segmented data. Experimental results in real-world scenarios demonstrate that the framework provides robust and stable position estimates, effectively distinguishing small targets in large-scale structured spaces. This research offers a practical solution for automated industrial inspections, reducing UAV payload while enhancing system reliability.
