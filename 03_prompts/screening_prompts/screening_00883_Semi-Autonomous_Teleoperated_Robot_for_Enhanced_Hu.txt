TITLE: Semi-Autonomous Teleoperated Robot for Enhanced Human-Machine Interaction
AUTHORS: G. B. Menti; V. R. Chowdary; N. L. P. Nagasarapu; G. K. Godugunuri; P. Abburi; J. Yerramsetty
YEAR: 2025
ABSTRACT: In recent years, advancements in robotics have increasingly focused on improving human-machine interaction (HMI) through semi-autonomous systems, particularly by integrating cutting-edge technologies such as the Internet of Things (IoT) and Robotic Operating System 2 (ROS 2). This paper presents the design, fabrication and evaluation of a semi- autonomous teleoperated robot specifically engineered for dynamic field navigation. Equipped with a differential drive system, the robot is optimized to maneuver freely and effectively across diverse terrains. It operates under the ROS2 Humble framework on a Raspberry Pi 4 (RPi 4), which ensures precise control and stable maneuverability in real-world conditions. The robot's teleoperation capabilities are enhanced by an optimized teleop - key algorithm and its IoT integration facilitates seamless communication, real-time feedback, and adaptive control. This combination significantly boosts the robot's efficiency and versatility, enabling it to execute tasks with greater precision, flexibility and safety in field environments. The integration of the Internet of things is a crucial element providing for real-time monitoring, remote control, and data-driven decision-making, hence enhancing both the operating potentials of the robot and its reliability. The findings highlight the potential of these technologies to advance field robotics, making them more effective for applications such as teleop exploration, environmental monitoring and complex operational tasks.
