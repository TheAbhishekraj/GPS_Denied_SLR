TITLE: Radar-Aided Navigation System for Small Drones in GPS-Denied Environments
AUTHORS: Klein K.T.J.; Uysal F.; Cuenca M.C.; Otten M.P.G.; De Wit J.J.M.
YEAR: 2021.0
ABSTRACT: Continuous navigation is crucial for small drones when flying a pre-planned route or during autonomous flight. In scenarios where a global positioning system (GPS) signal is unavailable, drift in the onboard sensors causes the inertial navigation system (INS) to quickly deviate from the planned route. This paper proposes a radar-aided navigation method for small drones which is able to estimate the horizontal velocity and height of the platform independently from GPS. The method is based on an omnidirectional radar system and takes advantage of multi-look processing to increase estimation precision. Simulations and experiments verify that an omnidirectional radar can be used to successfully estimate the velocity and course of a small drone platform. © 2021 IEEE.
