TITLE: Single-Image Depth to 3D Path planning for Collision-Free Drone Navigation
AUTHORS: R. J. Alitappeh; M. H. Bahar; A. Ramzanian
YEAR: 2025
ABSTRACT: This project presents the development of an autonomous drone navigation system tailored for indoor environments using computer vision and 3D path planning techniques. While initial experiments utilized Optical Flow for obstacle detection and navigation, this method proved to be computationally intensive, highly sensitive to lighting conditions, and dependent on precise camera calibration, making it unsuitable for our hardware setup. To overcome these challenges, we adopted a depth estimation approach using the ZoeDepth model, which generates accurate per-pixel depth maps from a single RGB image without requiring motion or stereo input. These depth maps were converted into 3D voxel grids for obstacle detection, and the RRT* algorithm was employed to generate optimized, collision-free paths. Our implementation, developed in Python using PyTorch and OpenCV, demonstrated efficient and robust performance in complex 3D environments. Experimental results highlight the superiority of the ZoeDepth-based pipeline in terms of accuracy, stability, and ease of integration, offering a practical solution for autonomous navigation with limited computational resources.
