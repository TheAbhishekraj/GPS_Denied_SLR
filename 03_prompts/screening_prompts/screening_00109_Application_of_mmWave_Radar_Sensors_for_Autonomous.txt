TITLE: Application of mmWave Radar Sensors for Autonomous Robotics Navigation
AUTHORS: X. Huang; N. Patel; K. P. Tsoi
YEAR: 2023
ABSTRACT: This paper explored the application of mmWave radar sensors in robotic navigation. With the growing need for indoor navigation and object avoidance employing mmWave technology, a mobile robot equipped with a mmWave radar system was developed using the ROS libraries and the mmWave ROS driver package. The system exhibited promising performance, showcasing its potential for addressing the rising demand in this field.
