TITLE: MAPFL: GNSS-Free Map-Based Particle Filter Localization for Autonomous Vehicles
AUTHORS: S. Z. N. Zukhraf; N. Muhammad
YEAR: 2025
ABSTRACT: Localization is very essential in autonomous vehicles (AVs) as it ensures safe navigation and decision-making. Typically, it is performed using global navigation satellite system (GNSS). In case of GNSS-denied environments, an AV will not be able to localize itself. Although some studies have focused on this problem but none of them worked on GNSS-free environment on a highway scale. A fully autonomous vehicle must be capable of localizing itself without the aid of GNSS. In this study, a GNSS-free localization system for AVs is proposed that operates in highway environments using map-based localization. It is inspired by how humans use milestone boards from the road side as landmarks. The proposed method uses a particle filter (PF) to estimate latitude and longitude of the vehicle by maintaining multiple hypotheses for expected location. This approach is tested on a real-world dataset acquired using an AV over a 51.79 km stretch on the Tartu-Tallinn highway. Visual inspection confirmed the suitability of the proposed method, especially near milestone board detections. The mean and minimum errors were 8.59 km and 0.13 km, respectively, achieved with the configuration providing the best localization performance. This approach offers a promising solution for GNSS-free localization for AVs.
