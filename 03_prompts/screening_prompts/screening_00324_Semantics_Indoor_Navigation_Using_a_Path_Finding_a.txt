TITLE: Semantics Indoor Navigation Using a Path Finding and Spatial Mapping for Assisting Visually Impaired Person
AUTHORS: B. K. A. Asante; H. Imamura
YEAR: 2024
ABSTRACT: Visually impaired individuals face significant challenges when navigating unfamiliar indoor environments. Due to the complexity of indoor navigation, existing aids for the visually impaired, such as guide dogs and white canes, provide limited assistance in complex indoor settings. In this paper, we present an innovative approach to generating paths for indoor navigation for the visually impaired, utilizing JSON data based on floorplans combined with conventional pathfinding algorithms. Experimental results demonstrate the system's effectiveness in various indoor settings, highlighting its potential as a valuable tool for visually impaired individuals.
