TITLE: RoboGuide: LQG-Based Autonomous Navigation for Four-Wheel Mecanum Campus Robot
AUTHORS: A. P. Jaramillo-Ortega; A. L. Ocampo-Ramirez; O. A. Gutiérrez-Rivadeneira; L. M. Bohórquez-Ardila; H. F. Garcia; J. Arcos-Legarda
YEAR: 2025
ABSTRACT: Efficient indoor navigation within large, complex environments, such as university campuses, remains a critical challenge due to the limited availability of wayfinding aids and dynamic conditions. This paper presents RoboGuide, an autonomous guide robot developed for the Faculty of Engineering at the University of Antioquia. We model a four-wheel Mecanum platform in discrete-time state-space form and design a Quadratic-Gaussian (LQG) controller that combines an optimal Linear-Quadratic Regulator (LQR) for trajectory tracking with a Kalman filter for robust state estimation under realistic sensor and process noise. A multimodal perception stack fuses LiDAR, RGB-D, and inertial data to ensure resilient localization in GPS-denied, cluttered indoor spaces. Although this paper focuses on the evaluation of the control layer, the perception stack serves as the foundation of the overall system architecture. We validate the kinematic behavior and wheel coordination in CoppeliaSim, then assess control performance in Python/Colab, where Gaussian measurement noise is added to approximate sensor imperfections. LQR serves as an ideal, noise-free baseline, whereas LQG explicitly accounts for uncertainty and provides improved robustness. No hardware experiments are reported at this stage. These simulation provides a necessary verification step before embedded deployment. The results highlight the controller's robustness under simulated conditions and indicate its potential for future deployment in campus guidance applications.
