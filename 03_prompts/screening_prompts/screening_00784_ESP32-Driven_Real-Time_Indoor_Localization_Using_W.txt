TITLE: ESP32-Driven Real-Time Indoor Localization Using Wi-Fi RSSI and a Simple Path Loss Model
AUTHORS: R. Waddar; A. A. Patil; G. Korimath; R. Kalyani; S. Shirol; N. C. Iyer
YEAR: 2025
ABSTRACT: Indoor navigation remains a significant challenge in GPS-denied environments such as warehouses, office buildings, and underground facilities. This study presents a low-cost, real-time Wi-Fi-based localization and navigation system for autonomous ground vehicles using ESP32 microcontrollers. Unlike traditional RSSI-based methods, the proposed approach combines a weighted averaging technique with a path loss model to improve distance estimation accuracy from multiple access points (APs). The system uses trilateration for position estimation and is implemented on the QCar robotic platform. Key innovations include the integration of real-time RSSI smoothing to mitigate noise, and a comparative evaluation of anchor geometries-triangular and square layouts-to analyze their impact on localization accuracy. Experimental results show that the system achieves an average localization accuracy of 97.6% in triangular configurations and 90.6% in square layouts. This work demonstrates a scalable and effective alternative to traditional localization systems, with future improvements focused on sensor fusion and algorithmic optimization.
