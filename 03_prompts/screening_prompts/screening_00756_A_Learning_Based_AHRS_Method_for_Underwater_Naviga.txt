TITLE: A Learning Based AHRS Method for Underwater Navigation
AUTHORS: P. Cui; C. Cao; F. Zhang
YEAR: 2024
ABSTRACT: Autonomous Underwater Vehicles (AUVs) are used for hydrological monitoring and exploration but face challenges with navigation due to the high costs of Strapdown Inertial Navigation Systems (SINS) and Doppler Velocity Logs (DVL). Micro Electro Mechanical System Inertial Measurement Units (MEMS-IMUs) are a lower-cost alternative but suffer from rapid drift in orientation due to inaccurate angular velocity measurements. To address this, the paper introduces a learning based method, which uses dilated convolutions and LSTM to correct these inaccuracies and reduce orientation errors. Experimental results demonstrate thatthe performance of the proposed method is close to that of fiber-optic SINS, making it a viable low-cost navigation solution when coupled with DVL.
