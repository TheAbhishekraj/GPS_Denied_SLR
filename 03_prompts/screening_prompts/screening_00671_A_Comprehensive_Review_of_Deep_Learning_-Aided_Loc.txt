TITLE: A Comprehensive Review of Deep Learning -Aided Localization Techniques for Autonomous UAV Landing
AUTHORS: Mohamed Sameer T.K.; Susitra D.
YEAR: 2025.0
ABSTRACT: Unmanned Aerial Vehicles have increasingly important applications such as surveillance, disaster management, logistics, and precision agriculture. However, precise localization and autonomous landing, especially in a Global Navigation Satellite System-denied environment, are still major research challenges. This paper provides a comprehensive review of deep learning-aided localization and autonomous landing techniques for UAVs. The recent advancements in vision-based sensor-fusion and learning-driven frameworks are analyzed. Considerable attention is given to approaches that combine visual odometry with simultaneous localization and mapping and multi-sensor integration. The current research is shifting from traditional feature-based approaches to intelligent hybrid solutions with deep learning, reinforcement learning, and an Extended Kalman Filter. In this regard, a deep learning-based EKF approach for improving the detection and precision landing is discussed. Comparative analysis is done across multiple methodologies to enhance robustness and accuracy in dynamic and GNSS-denied conditions. Future research directions include swarm-based cooperative localization and adaptive landing strategies driven by hybrid optimization.  © 2025 IEEE.
