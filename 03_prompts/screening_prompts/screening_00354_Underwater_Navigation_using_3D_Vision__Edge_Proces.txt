TITLE: Underwater Navigation using 3D Vision, Edge Processing, and Autonomy
AUTHORS: J. Mattern; L. Bezanson; V. Nguyen; J. Seawall; R. Conescu; D. Moore
YEAR: 2021
ABSTRACT: The use of unmanned, underwater vehicles (UUVs) has grown in applications such as mine countermeasures, infrastructure maintenance, oceanographic research, and deep-sea exploration. Many UUVs rely on GPS or acoustic sensors to achieve the precision necessary for underwater navigation. However, in situations where GPS surfacing and acoustic localization are unavailable, a UUV is subject to accumulated drift. In this paper, we present a method of reducing navigational drift by integrating feature-based navigation (FBN) and Self Localization and Mapping (SLAM) navigation solutions on a UUV equipped with Edge Computing sensors enabling distributed AI solutions. The edge enabled sensors also enable advanced adaptive autonomy behaviors. The resulting system allows UUVs to navigate the local and global waterspace, without relying upon GPS or acoustic localization, while continuously refining the global pose relayed to mission autonomy software.
