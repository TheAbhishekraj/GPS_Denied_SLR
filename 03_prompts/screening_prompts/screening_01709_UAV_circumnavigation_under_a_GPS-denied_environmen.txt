TITLE: UAV circumnavigation under a GPS-denied environment: Algorithms and experiments
AUTHORS: Cao Y.; Kingston D.; Rasmussen S.
YEAR: 2015
ABSTRACT: This paper reports our recent effort on evaluating two circumnavigation algorithms using a Piccolo UAV when a GPS-denied environment is simulated. These two circumnavigation algorithms can drive the UAV to orbit around a target by using range and range rate measurements, where range refers to the distance between the UAV and the target and range rate refers to the rate at which range changes. These range-based control algorithms are GPS independent, and hence can be applied in a GPS-denied environment. We first introduce the two circumnavigation algorithms and their motivations. Brief analysis is provided to show the stable UAV motions using the two algorithms afterwards. Then several simulation examples are provided to illustrate the performance of the two algorithms under the AMASE simulation environment. Finally, we present experimental results obtained from our recent flight tests, where the two circumnavigation algorithms are adjusted appropriately. © 2015, E-flow American Institute of Aeronautics and Astronautics (AIAA).
