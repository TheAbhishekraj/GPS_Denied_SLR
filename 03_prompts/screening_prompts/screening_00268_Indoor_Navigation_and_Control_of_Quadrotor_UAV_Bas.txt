TITLE: Indoor Navigation and Control of Quadrotor UAV Based on Low Cost MCS
AUTHORS: Y. Dai; Q. Zeng; J. Lai; K. Sun; Y. Zhou
YEAR: 2018
ABSTRACT: Motion capture system (MCS) has important significance in the Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV) research. Considering that Global Navigation Satellite System (GNSS) signal is hard to be obtained in the indoor environment, a film/television production level MCS was used to provide the navigation information for the quadrotor UAV. A navigation control system is proposed and realized as a test platform of the quadrotor. The attitude and position loops of the quadrotor UAV controller are designed based on the integral backstepping method. The simulation and experimental results indicate that the controller has good stability and tracking performance.
