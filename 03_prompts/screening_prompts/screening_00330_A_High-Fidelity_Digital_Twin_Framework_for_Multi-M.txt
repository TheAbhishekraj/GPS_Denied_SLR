TITLE: A High-Fidelity Digital Twin Framework for Multi-Modal Sim-to-Real Transfer for Robust UAV Autonomy
AUTHORS: R. Priyanshu; M. Agarwal; P. Kumar
YEAR: 2026
ABSTRACT: Autonomous Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV) navigation remains an important challenge under GNSS-denied and communication-jammed conditions. Visual-Inertial Odometry (VIO) systems experience compounding biases, while traditional simulators cannot properly model real-world visual and wireless phenomena with sufficient fidelity, leading to "the reality gap." In this paper, we introduce a new dual-fidelity DT paradigm that jointly leverages Unreal Engine 5 (UE5) for real-time visual and dynamic simulations and the high-fidelity robotics simulator, Colosseum-AirSim. We describe how this DT infrastructure can enable high-quality S2R learning by creating synthetic images with DR and using efficient AI models like ResNet-18 for real-time semantic image segmentation, achieving mIoU of 87.0% or higher. This significantly surpasses state-of-the-art performance reported with pure simulation learning or real-image learning alone. These high-fidelity DT-infrastructure capabilities enable high-quality communication-aware, context-aware, autonomous functioning with localization under HITL control for real-world applications such as smart urban networks or disaster response operations.
