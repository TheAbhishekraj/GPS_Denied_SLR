TITLE: A GPS/BeiDou Dual-Mode Mobile Robot Localization Error Compensation Method Fused with IMU
AUTHORS: Y. Wang; Z. Xue
YEAR: 2026
ABSTRACT: This paper proposes an IMU-fused GPS/BeiDou dual-mode localization error compensation method for mobile robots in urban canyons and vegetation-obstructed areas. The method combines Allan-variance-based IMU stochastic modeling, online ZUPT-assisted bias correction, and a tightly coupled adaptive unscented Kalman filter using pseudorange, pseudorange-rate, and IMU preintegration measurements. A recursive preintegration propagation mechanism is introduced for GNSS outage compensation, while dual-mode satellite selection improves geometry quality and solution stability. Experiments on a mobile robot platform across open-sky, urban canyon, and treecanopy routes show that, compared with a conventional loosecoupled GNSS/IMU EKF, the proposed method reduces horizontal RMSE by 47.2 % and vertical error by 53.8 %, and maintains sub-meter localization for up to 60 s of complete GNSS outage. The results verify improved accuracy, continuity, and robustness for low-cost outdoor robotic navigation.
