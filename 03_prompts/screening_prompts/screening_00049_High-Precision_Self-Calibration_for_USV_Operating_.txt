TITLE: High-Precision Self-Calibration for USV Operating in GNSS-Denied Environments
AUTHORS: J. Liu
YEAR: 2025
ABSTRACT: In complex marine environments where satellite navigation signals are denied, the integrated navigation scheme combining Strapdown Inertial Navigation System (SINS) and Doppler Velocity Log (DVL) has become a mainstream technical solution in the field of autonomous navigation for Unmanned Surface Vehicles (USVs). However, the high-precision performance of this integrated navigation system is not inherent but relies on precise system calibration. To address this critical technical challenge, this study proposes an online high-precision selfcalibration method. By establishing an error model and designing an adaptive filtering algorithm, real-time estimation and compensation of system error parameters are achieved. Simulation results demonstrate that this method effectively improves the accuracy of calibration parameters, with scale factor errors controlled within $\mathbf{0. 4 \%}$. This research provides reliable technical support for the precise navigation of USVs in complex environments and holds significant theoretical and practical value for enhancing the autonomous navigation capabilities of USVs.
