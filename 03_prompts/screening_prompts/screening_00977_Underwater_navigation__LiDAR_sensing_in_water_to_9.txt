TITLE: Underwater navigation: LiDAR sensing in water to 9 m distance through semi-transparent obstacles
AUTHORS: S. M. Pershin; M. Y. Grishin; V. A. Zavozin; P. A. Titovets; N. E. Miroshnikova; M. O. Fedyuk
YEAR: 2022
ABSTRACT: A model experiment on LiDAR underwater navigation has been carried out. A 9-m-Iong tube filled with water was used as a model water medium. The laser beam of the compact LiDAR (Light Detection and Ranging, 532 nm, ~2 ns, ~2 μJ/pulse, 4 kHz) was directed into the tube, and a non-transparent object (wall of the room) was sensed through the water. A unique LiDAR sensing technique based on a gated single-photon avalanche diode detector allowed successful detection of a non-transparent object through several scattering obstacles (up to three 1/2/3-layered plastic meshes). The possibilities of unmanned vehicles navigation and docking under water using LiDARs are discussed.
