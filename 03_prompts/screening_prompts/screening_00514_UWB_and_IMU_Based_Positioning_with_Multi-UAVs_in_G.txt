TITLE: UWB and IMU Based Positioning with Multi-UAVs in GPS-Denied Environments
AUTHORS: J. Guo; S. Yang; T. Li
YEAR: 2025
ABSTRACT: With the rapid development of drone technology and its special platform advantages, the application scenarios of drones have expanded from outdoor open environments to complex and closed scenarios such as emergency rescue and industrial inspection. However, in closed and narrow working environments, mainstream positioning technologies such as GPS, Visual positioning and LiDAR face severe challenges. To over come this, the Ultra-wideband (UWB) positioning technology has become an ideal alternative due to its strong penetration and high temporal resolution. Nevertheless, its dependence on fixed anchor nodes still make it difficult to meet the needs of dynamic scenarios. On the other hand, due to the limitation of single UAV capability and the complex development trend of the detection environment, single-machine positioning can no longer meet the needs of current applications. In response to the above technical bottlenecks, this work introduces a sensor fusion positioning approach based on inertial measurement unit (IMU) and UWB with multi UAVs. With the proposed approach, the requirement of the anchor nodes in the system can be reduced and the positioning performance of the system can be improved.
