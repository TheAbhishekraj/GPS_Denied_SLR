TITLE: SARATHI-DRONE: A Smart Drone for Autonomous Search, Rescue, and Human Identification
AUTHORS: A. Tripathi; A. K. Singh; A. Tyagi; S. Garg
YEAR: 2026
ABSTRACT: In this paper, we propose an affordable intelligent Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV) designed for conducting autonomous search and rescue (SAR) missions in GPSdenied environments (termed SARATHI-DRONE). The system comprises a Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W with an RGB camera and an AMG8833 thermal sensor, an APM/Pixhawk-compatible flight controller, and a lightweight quadrotor frame. On-board edge AI (TensorFlow Lite) performs real-time human detection; Visual SLAM provides localisation and mapping without GPS. A servo-actuated release mechanism enables payload delivery. Experimental evaluations demonstrate feasible real-time performance, mapping capability, and payload release accuracy suitable for rapid disaster response. Index Terms—Search and Rescue, UAV, GPS-denied, Visual SLAM, TensorFlow Lite, Thermal-RGB fusion, Edge AI.
