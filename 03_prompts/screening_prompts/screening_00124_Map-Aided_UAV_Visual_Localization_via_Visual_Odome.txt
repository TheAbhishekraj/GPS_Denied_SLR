TITLE: Map-Aided UAV Visual Localization via Visual Odometry and Orthophoto Map Alignment
AUTHORS: Y. Zhang; Q. Yi; Y. Zong; Z. Yan; H. He
YEAR: 2026
ABSTRACT: Stable localization without relying on GNSS position measurements is a key requirement for autonomous UAV navigation in GNSS-limited or disturbed environments. To address the scale drift, heading error accumulation, and trajectory interruption problems of pure visual odometry during long-term propagation, this paper proposes an orthophoto-map-aided two-dimensional visual localization method for downward-looking UAV images. The proposed method first estimates the inter-frame homography between adjacent UAV images to propagate the visual odometry state and obtain the predicted position of the current frame in the orthophoto map coordinate system. A local orthophoto patch is then cropped according to the predicted position, and UAV-map homography alignment is performed to provide periodic absolute correction. To avoid trajectory jumps caused by unreliable map observations, a quality gating mechanism based on the number of matches, number of inliers, reprojection error, and correction displacement is designed. When the predicted position may contain a large error, a local relocalization strategy searches neighboring candidate map regions to recover the global map anchor. Experiments on two real UAV flight datasets show that the proposed method achieves complete localization outputs on both datasets, with mean localization errors of 8.1 m and 8.4 m, respectively, significantly outperforming the pure VO baseline. Trajectory comparisons further demonstrate that periodic orthophoto map correction effectively suppresses accumulated drift, turning-position errors, and trajectory interruption, thereby improving the global consistency and continuity of downward-looking UAV visual localization.
