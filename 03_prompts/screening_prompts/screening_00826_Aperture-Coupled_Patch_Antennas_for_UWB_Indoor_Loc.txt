TITLE: Aperture-Coupled Patch Antennas for UWB Indoor Localization Systems in the 4 and 6 GHz Bands
AUTHORS: S. Marti; H. Diethelm; H. Mathis; H. -D. Lang
YEAR: 2022
ABSTRACT: Ultra-wideband- (UWB-) based localization systems are becoming increasingly popular, particularly for indoor navigation of unmanned aerial vehicles (UAV). Both the typical indoor channel properties as well as the specific requirements for UWB localization via time-difference-of-arrival (TDoA) pose special and difficult requirements on the antennas. As shown with two specific anchor antenna designs, aperture-coupled patch antennas provide the required bandwidth and visible range, exhibit little dispersion, and their radiation characteristic helps mitigate multipath issues. Beyond that, it is observed that they also show low angular dependency of the transfer characteristics — a key requirement for precise UWB localization applications.
