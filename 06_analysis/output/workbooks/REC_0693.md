TOF cameras vs. other 3D sensors (i.e., scanning LIDAR, solid-state LIDAR)
Scanning LIDARs (e.g., Velodyne, Hokuyo) have larger operative range, but consume
more power, have poor spatial resolution (in the direction perpendicular to the scan
plane), and can be affected by motion blur phenomena.
Solid-state LIDARs (e.g., Innovitz, Quanergy) are promising (no motion blur, spatial
resolution comparable with TOF cameras), but are still under development (release
expected in 2018-2020).
High-resolution TOF cameras can be a key, low-cost technology to enable a wide range of 
applications relevant to autonomous robotic systems.

Classification: measurement principle
Pulsed emitter (Flash LIDAR)
Each pixel computes a distance by measuring the round-trip time delay of the
emitted pulse through a TOF counter.
Operative range several hundreds of meters.
Heavy and expensive sensors.
Low-cost alternatives exist (Basler, Kinect V2) in exchange of a significant
reduction in the operative range.
Continuous-wave-modulated emitter
Each pixel computes a distance by measuring the phase shift between TX and Rx
signals.
Short operative range (up to few meters).
Co-registered RGB and depth images.

states in front of the cameras.

### [[page 16]]
Conclusion
The potential of exploiting a high-resolution, pulse-based TOF camera for autonomous
navigation tasks in the aerospace field was investigated.
The metrological characterization showed that depth measurement accuracy is strongly
affected by variations in the reflectivity of objects in the scene.
Also, the sensor operates poorly under direct sun illumination.
The TOF camera can be used for autonomous localization (indoor or outdoor under no-
/low-light conditions) based on depth odometry algorithm.
Clearly, a proper navigation filter integrating data from inertial sensors shall be designed to
reconstruct longer trajectories while limiting the error drift.
The TOF camera is eligible as the main relative navigation sensor to carry out pose
determination of non-cooperative targets during close-proximity operations (below 10 m).
The target will be mounted on a robotic arm to perform dynamic acquisitions and full
relative navigation architectures.
